## Proposed cadence, health and fallback

Start with 2-minute checks of an approved official release index/version around
announced publication; retrieve details only after a change. Use 5-minute checks
for verified newsroom backstops, and manual NYT observation where automation is
unverified. Back off to 15 minutes or hourly outside publication windows, then
daily through canvass/certification. These are proposals, not measured cadence.
The retained identical setup repeat and publisher's noon administrative schedule
do not demonstrate vote-update timing. Use bounded concurrency, timeouts, jitter,
conditional requests where supported and exponential backoff. Silence alone is
not an outage or proof that all votes are counted.

Maintain last successful request, last genuine vote change, source stage,
expected versus observed compatible units, cache state and manual-fallback
reason. Directory records are complete for 132 localities; retain Covington's
missing state role fields and separate local-page conflicts. Charlotte/Highland

products. These gaps require explicit fallback, not invented contact/feed data.

For failure, preserve the same official page or linked export manually with
URL, time, status and original file. Newsroom substitution must be temporary,
attributed and compatible in election/unit/method; reconcile to official data
when available. AP mirrors are one supplier. WHSV's inactive 12-race setup has
