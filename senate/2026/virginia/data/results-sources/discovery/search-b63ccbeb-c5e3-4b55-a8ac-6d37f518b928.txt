# Welcome to the City of Suffolk General Registrar's Webpage

 Our Duties 

  * Provide an equitable opportunity for qualified voters to register to vote.
  * Maintain accurate registration and election records.
  * Conduct all federal, state, and municipal elections and primaries with integrity and efficiency under the guidance of the City of Suffolk Electoral Board in accordance with State and Federal laws. 
  * Coordinate the filing of candidates for local office.

## 2026 Precinct Changes 

On January 7, 2026, the Suffolk City Council unanimously approved an ordinance to amend the polling place for the Huntersville and Lakeside precincts. Voters impacted by the change will receive an updated Voter Notification Letter. 

  * NANSEMOND BOROUGH: Huntersville (0706) precinct will move from VMAS Center to Fire Station Number 11, 6842 Hampton Roads Parkway, Suffolk, 23435. This change was effective with the April 21, 2026 Special Election.
  * SUFFOLK BOROUGH: - Lakeside (0601) precinct will move from Morgan Memorial Library to the new Downtown Suffolk Library, 325 W. Washington Street, Suffolk, 23434. This change was effective with the August 4, 2026 Dual Party Primary Election.

## Suffolk Electoral Board Meetings

The Suffolk Electoral Board meets at 9:00 a.m. on the first Tuesday of every month, unless otherwise noted.  The meeting occurs in the General Registrar's Office, 440 Market Street, First Floor, Suffolk, Virginia. Agendas and meeting minutes are posted on the City of Suffolk Agenda Center webpage.

## Our Mission Statement

The mission of the Elections and Registration Department is to register citizens of Suffolk City to vote; to ensure that elections are impartial, fair, accurate, and accessible to all voters; to provide excellent customer service to voters, candidates, and the media; and to help the public understand and follow the laws, rules, and regulations.
