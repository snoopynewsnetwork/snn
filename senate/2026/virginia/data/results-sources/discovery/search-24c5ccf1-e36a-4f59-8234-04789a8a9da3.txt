# Notice of Lancaster County Electoral Board Meeting

On November 4, 2026 at 11:00 am, the Lancaster County Electoral Board will hold a meeting at the Office of the General Registrar, located at 8311 Mary Ball Road, Double-wide Office Trailer, in Lancaster, Virginia. The meeting is open to the public. Contact the Secretary of the Board at (804) 424-6572, or the General Registrar at (804) 462-5277 for further information.
