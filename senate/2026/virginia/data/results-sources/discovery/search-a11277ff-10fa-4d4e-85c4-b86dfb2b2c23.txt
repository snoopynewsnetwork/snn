## Who Can Register to Vote in Virginia?

You may register to vote if you meet the following criteria:

  * Be a U.S. citizen
  * Be a resident of Virginia
  * Be at least 17 years old (currently) and will be 18 years old by the next general election
  * Have had your voting rights restored if you have ever been convicted of a felony
  * Have had your capacity restored if you have been declared mentally incapacitated in a Circuit Court

## How Do I Register to Vote?

  * You must fill out and submit a voter registration application online, in person or by mail by completing a voter registration application.
  * Be sure to complete and sign the form before mailing. Make sure you include a copy of your ID (e.g., your Virginia Driver's License, utility bill or bank statement).
  * If mailed, Voter Registration Applications must be postmarked no later than the deadline specified for each election.
  * If delivered in person, either by the applicant or a third party, the Voter Registration Application must be received by the deadline.
  * Voter Registration Applications postmarked or received after the deadline will be held and processed after the election.

Please report your concerns about sign placement to the City's Zoning Office. Please note that the Voter Registrar's Office does not enforce sign placement in the City.

Email the Zoning Office (ZoningComplaints@VBgov.com)

Virginia Beach City buildings and work areas are to be used to conduct City business. The City has established codes, ordinances and directives to reduce the potential for disrupting City business during the election process. 

These regulations are in addition to state laws, and are to be applied evenly, regardless of message and are not meant to limit public expression. Regulations apply to signs and expressive activities (see definitions below).

## About our Election System

Due to changes to our local election system, Virginia Beach will have 10 election districts beginning with the November 2022 election. From here, you can learn more about each of the 10 districts with a District Profile, which contains information such as how many households and individuals residing in a given district, average income, median home value, education statistics, school locations, economic data, and how the land of a district is zoned.
The data in the profiles will evolve along with the districts. Depending on needs identified by our residents and City Council, additional data points may be added and updated as new information becomes available. These profiles are meant to give residents in a district a comprehensive view to better understand the subtleties of their community and how they fit into our community at large.
