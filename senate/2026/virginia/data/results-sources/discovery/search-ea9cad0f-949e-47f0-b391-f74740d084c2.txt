# Voting Information

## Election Day

General and Special Elections on Tuesday, November 3rd, 2026

Updates for The City of Hopewell, Virginia

Deadlines:

Register to vote or update an existing registration:

  * Last day to update existing changes to your voter record is October 23, 2026 by 5:00 p.m.
  * Register to vote or update an existing registration online at  www.elections.virginia.gov by October 23, 2026 at 12:00 am.
  * Voters may register and vote using a provisional ballot in person after October 23, 2026 through Election Day.
  * Contact your local registrar's office for more information.

Early, in-person voting period:

  * Start Date for early voting: Sept. 18th, 2026, 8:30 a.m. until 5:0opm., Monday through Friday.
  * Office will be closed on Oct 12th 2026, in observance of the holiday.
  * Contact your local registrar's office for all early voting and satellite location information.
  * The Voter Registration Office will be open for the last two Saturdays before the election: October 24, 2026, from 8:30 until 5:00 p.m., and Oct. 31, 2026, from 8:30 a.m. until 5:00 p.m.
  * The Voter Registration Office will be open on two Sundays before the election: Oct. 18, 2026, from 11:00 a.m. until 5:00 p.m.; and also Oct. 25, 2026, from 11:00 a.m.

Apply for a ballot to be mailed to you on or before Oct. 23. 2026. by 5:00 p.m.

  * Your local registrar's office must receive your request by 5:00 p.m.
  * Apply for an absentee ballot online at www.elections.virginia.gov.

## Inclement Weather Information

In the event that Hopewell City Government Offices close due to inclement weather, absentee voting will be canceled.

## Contact Us

For additional information on operating status, please call the Office of the General Registrar at 804-541-2232. Information is also available through Hopewell’s website, Twitter, and Facebook.--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
