# Electoral Board

## Meeting

1st week of February and in March each year

## Members

  * Ms. Pearlie Banks, Chairman
  * Ms. Nancy Parrish, Vice-Chair
  * Mr. Gary Wyse, Secretary
  * Ms. Jennifer Maynard, General Registrar

## Overview

1st week of February and in March each year, at a time set by the board and at such other times on the call of any board member. Meetings are held at the registrar's office

### Term

3 years, appointed by the Chief Judge of the Judicial Circuit for the City

### Duties

Appoint the Officers of Elections and Registrar. Oversees elections, canvases election results, and certifies them to the State Board of Elections. Run all elections held in the City of Franklin with honesty and integrity
