# Registrar

The Registrar’s Office is responsible for managing all aspects of voter registration and election administration within the city. The General Registrar ensures that every eligible resident has the opportunity to register to vote and participate in local, state, and federal elections.

## Key Responsibilities

  * Voter Registration: Maintains accurate voter registration records and processes applications for new registrations, transfers, and address changes.
  * Election Management: Oversees the preparation and execution of elections, including managing polling locations and ensuring compliance with election laws.
  * Poll Worker Training: Recruits, appoints, and trains poll workers and election officials to ensure smooth election operations.
  * Public Education: Provides information to residents about voter registration deadlines, election dates, and voting procedures.
  * Record Maintenance: Preserves all election-related documents and ensures proper reporting of election results.

The Registrar’s Office is committed to upholding the integrity of the electoral process while providing exceptional service to Covington’s citizens. For assistance or inquiries, visit the office at 515 E. Pine St., Covington, VA, or call (540) 965-6380.

## City Council Districts & Voting Information

The City of Covington is divided into five electoral districts for representation on City Council. This page provides information to help residents identify their Council district, locate their Election Day polling place, review the official legal descriptions of district boundaries, and access voter registration and election resources.

Residents may use the maps and resources below as helpful references. Questions regarding voter registration, elections, absentee voting, or polling locations should be directed to the City of Covington Registrar's Office.

## Official Electoral District Descriptions

The official boundaries of Covington's five electoral districts are established in Chapter 16, Article II – Electoral Districts of the City of Covington Code of Ordinances.

> There is hereby established a permanent central absentee precinct to be used for all elections. The polling place for such precinct shall be located in the Registrar's Office at the former Jeter-Watson Intermediate School building, 515 East Pine Street. Such central absentee precinct shall be operated as provided in Code of Virginia, § 24.2-712, as amended.

The Central Absentee Precinct is separate from the five City Council electoral districts shown on the maps above. Residents should contact the Registrar's Office for current information concerning absentee voting, early voting, Election Day voting, or other election procedures.
