# Election Calendar

2026 Election Schedule

August 4, 2026 – Primary Election

Offices on the Ballot:

  * U.S. Senate
  * U.S. House of Representatives

Deadlines:

Register to Vote/Update Registration: July 24, 2026

Early In-Person Voting: June 19, 2026 through August 1, 2026

Note: If the local office is closed on June 19 for the Juneteenth state holiday, then the office shall be open for early voting on Thursday, June 18.

Apply for a Ballot to be Mailed to You: Through July 24, 2026

Register to Vote or Update an Existing Registration:

Through Friday, July 24, 2026.

Register to vote or update an existing registration online using the Virginia Citizen Portal.

Voters may register and vote using a provisional ballot in person after July 24, 2026, through Election Day.

 

Early, In-Person Voting Period:

Start Date: Thursday, June 18, 2026 through 5:00 p.m., Saturday, August 1, 2026.

 

Apply for a Ballot to be Mailed to You:

Through 5:00 p.m., Friday, July 24, 2026.

Your application must be received by 5:00 p.m.

Apply for an absentee ballot online using the Virginia Citizen Portal here.

November 3, 2026 – General and Special Elections

Offices and Questions on the Ballot:

  * U.S. Senate
  * U.S. House of Representatives
  * House of Delegates District 20 Special Election
  * Local Offices (General and Special Elections)
  * Proposed Constitutional Amendment #1
  * Proposed Constitutional Amendment #2
  * Proposed Constitutional Amendment #3

Deadlines:

Register to Vote/Update Registration: October 23, 2026

Early In-Person Voting: September 18, 2026 through October 31, 2026

Apply for a Ballot to be Mailed to You: Through October 23, 2026

Register to Vote or Update an Existing Registration:

Through Friday, October 23, 2026.

Register to vote or update an existing registration online using the Virginia Citizen Portal.

Voters may register and vote using a provisional ballot in person after October 23, 2026, through Election Day.

Early, In-Person Voting Period:

Start Date: Friday, September 18, 2026 through 5:00 p.m., Saturday, October 31, 2026.

Apply for a Ballot to be Mailed to You:

Through 5:00 p.m., Friday, October 23, 2026.

Your application must be received by 5:00 p.m.

Apply for an absentee ballot online using the Virginia Citizen Portal here.
