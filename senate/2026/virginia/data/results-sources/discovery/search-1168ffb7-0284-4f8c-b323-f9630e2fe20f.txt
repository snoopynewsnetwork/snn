### 2026 School Sales Tax Referendum

On Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026, Prince Edward County voters will consider a school sales tax referendum on whether to authorize the County to levy a general retail sales tax of up to 1% to fund new construction or major renovations of schools serving Prince Edward County.
