  1. Solicitation & 3rd Party Mailings

Voters should be cognizant of 3rd Party Mailings (50 States, Virginians for Americans First, etc.) that are solicitation not authorized/associated with the Registrar's Office or the Virginia Department of Elections.

3rd Party Mailings:  The Department of Elections has received notice from the Voter Participation Center (VPC) and the Center for Voter Information (CVI) that they will be sending two large mailings to specific voters in specific localities at the end of March and June 2024. ELECT is not affiliated in any way with these third party groups, nor is the Henry County Registrar's Office. 

**Be vigilant in protecting your personal information. Our office does not solicit.**

# Henry County Electoral Board

2026:

The Henry County Electoral Board will meet September 29, 2026 at 4:00pm at the Henry County Administration Building.

______________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________

The Virginia Electoral Board Association  (VEBA) principles are ones that Henry County Elections & Voter Registration Office also promotes in our office, daily duties, as well as on Election Days.

Those Principles are:

  1. Adhere to the Law: Uphold the Constitution of the United States, as well as the Code of Virginia.
  2. Protect & Defend the Integrity of the Election Process: Safeguard against fraud and deceit by demonstrating transparency and lawful practices.
  3. Promote Transparency in the Conduct of Elections: Ensure that we conduct ourselves and actions so that everyone feels secure and confident in our elections.
  4. Treat all participants impartially: All people, including voters, citizens, candidates and political committees should be treated fairly, equally, and respectfully.
