### Isle of Wight County, Virginia

## GENERAL FUND: GOVERNMENT

# ELECTORAL BOARD / REGISTRAR

## DESCRIPTION

The Electoral Board is comprised of a three-member panel appointed by the judges of the Circuit Court. The Electoral Board is responsible for the appointment of the General Registrar (GR). They are also responsible for all elections that are held. Each member is appointed for a three (3) year term. The Electoral Board is made up of two (2) members of the political party of the Governor in office at the time of appointment. The GR is appointed for a four (4) year term by the Isle of Wight County Electoral Board. The GR is charged with carrying out the directives of the State Board of Elections, the Commonwealth of Virginia Election Code and the Electoral Board of Isle of Wight County to serve the voters of the County in the most effective manner possible. The GR assists citizens with the voter registration process – in person, online, and by mail. The GR assists voters with the absentee voting process and ensures that all absentee voting materials are processed and mailed/emailed to voters for 45 days before each election. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
