The office of the General Registrar and Electoral Board of Northumberland County promotes a high level of customer service to our citizens. We are committed to promoting democracy and public trust to ensure fair and impartial elections.

Please send any email correspondence for the Registrar's Office to [email protected]

## Meetings

Northumberland County Electoral Board meets every second Tuesday at 2 pm in the Registrar's Office located at 45 Back Street, Heathsville, VA 22473. The public may attend. Please check the calendar as meeting dates may change due to conflicts.

Special Elections  
April 21, 2026

On the ballot: Proposed Constitutional Amendment - Statewide

April 14, 2026: Final day to register to vote or change your voter registration information for this election. No changes or additions may be made to the voter registration records after this date!  
Mailed voter registration applications must be sent to the Voter Registration and Elections Office postmarked on or before the deadline.

In-Person Early Voting

March 6 – April 18 In-Person or Curbside Early Voting Monday–Friday, 9 am – 5 pm will be available at the address shown below.  
April 18, 2026 Final day to vote early. In addition to regular office hours, office will be open 9 AM-5 PM, Saturday April 11 and Saturday April 18.

Mail Absentee Voting

April 10, 2026, Last day to request mail in ballot. Applications must be received in the Voter Registration and Elections Office by 5 p.m.  
April 21, 2026 Election Day: All mail in voted ballots must be postmarked by April 21, 2026. They must be received no later than Noon on April 24, 2026. Hand delivered ballots must be received in our office no later than 7PM on Election Day.

Canvass of Election/Provisional Ballot Meeting

Wednesday, April 22, 2026: The Northumberland County Electoral Board will convene at 12 noon at the Office of the General Registrar & Electoral Bd to canvass the election and begin considering any provisional ballots. They will reconvene on April 24, 2026 at 12 noon.
