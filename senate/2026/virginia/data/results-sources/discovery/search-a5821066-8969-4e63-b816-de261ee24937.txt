PUBLIC NOTICE

For the  
November 4, 2025, General Election  
In  
Southampton County, Virginia

IN-Person, early voting for Southampton County voters will be available at the location, dates and hours listed below: beginning Friday, September 19, 2025, and ending Saturday, November 1, 2025.

❖ Office of the General Registrar, 22305 Main Street, Courtland, Virginia 23837 – 9:00am – 5:00pm Monday – Friday.

❖ This office will also be open: Saturday, October 25, 2025 & Saturday, November 1, 2025, from 9:00am -5:00pm

❖ Office of the General Registrar will be closed on October 13, 2025, in observance of Columbus Day & Yorktown Victory Day.

Ballot Drop Boxes – for absentee Vote By Mail ballots

❖ Will be available the same dates, times, & location listed above for In-person (early) voting.  
❖ Drop Boxes will also be available at all polling places on Election Day, November 4, 2025, from 6:00am until 7:00pm.

LYNN H. BURGESS  
Director of Elections, Southampton / General Registrar  
Posted: September 2, 2025
