# Important Dates

## November 3, 2026 General Election

Absentee Voting begins: Friday, September 18

Open for Absentee Voting: Friday, September 18 - Friday, October 30 M-F 8:30am- 4:30pm

Also Open for Absentee Voting: Sunday, October 18 12pm - 5pm; Saturday, October 24 9am - 5pm; Sunday, October 25 12pm - 5pm; Saturday, October 31 9am-5pm

Last day to request an absentee ballot to be sent: Friday, October 23

Last day to vote Absentee In-Person: Saturday, October 31

Election Day: Tuesday, November 3, 2026 6am-7pm

# Elections and Voter Registration

## Absentee Voting

### Voting Absentee In-Person “Early Voting” 

In-Person Absentee Voting for the November 3, 2026 General Election will be available at the Goochland Sports Complex, 1800 Sandy Hook Road, Goochland, VA 23063 during normal office hours, 8:30am to 4:30pm on weekdays beginning Friday, September 18, 2026 and continuing until Friday, October 30, 2026. The office will also be open for Absentee Voting on Sunday October 18 from 12pm until 5 pm, Saturday, October 24 from 9am until 5pm, Sunday, October 25 from 12pm until 5pm and Saturday, October 31 from 9am until 5pm. .

### Voting Absentee by Mail

Ballots for the November 3, 2026 Election will be sent out by Friday, September 18th, but you can request them right now! The last day to request an Absentee Ballot to be sent to you is Friday, October 23.

  * Absentee Ballot Application

Applications can be returned in person at the Office of the General Registrar located at 1876 Sandy Hook Rd, Goochland, VA 23063, by mail to the General Registrar at PO Box 1013, Goochland, VA 23063, by fax to 804-556-6323 or by email to vote@goochlandva.us.
