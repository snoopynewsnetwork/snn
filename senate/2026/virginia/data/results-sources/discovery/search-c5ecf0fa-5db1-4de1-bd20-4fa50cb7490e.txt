## Roanoke County's next General Election will be held on November 3, 2026.

The following offices and races will appear on some or all of the County's ballots: 

  * U.S. Senate (1 of 2 seats) - Mark R. Warner (D) vs. Bert Mizusawa (R)
  * U.S. House of Representatives - 
    * 6th Congressional District (Pcts. 101-105, 201-206, 401-406, and 505): 
Beth A. Macy (D) vs. Ben L. Cline (R)
    * 9th Congressional District (Pcts. 106-107, 301-307, 501-504, 506-507): 
Joy A. Powers (D) vs. H. Morgan Griffith (R)
  * Vinton Town Council (2 of 4 seats, vote for not more than 2; Pcts. 403-404) - Sabrina M. McCarty (I) and Michael W. "Mike" Stovall (I)
  * Three proposed Constitutional Amendments will also be on all full ballots.
