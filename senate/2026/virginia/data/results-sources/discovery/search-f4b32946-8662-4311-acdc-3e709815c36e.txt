# Voter Registrar

The General Registrar operates under the direction and supervision of the Madison County Electoral Board and implements the election laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia.

The office is located at 414 N. Main St, Madison VA

Office hours: Monday - Friday 8:30 am - 4:30 pm

## Mission Statement

Our mission is to provide fair and equal access to voter registration and voting for all eligible citizens of Madison County. Voting is a fundamental right of every citizen. The Madison County Office of Voter Registration is committed to the faithful execution of the duties and responsibilities outlined by the Virginia State Board of Elections and by state and federal law in the General Registrar (PDF). Passed by the U.S. Congress in 1993, the National Voter Registration Act and the Help America Vote Act are important pieces of legislation that highlight our role and our goals:

  * Increase the number of eligible citizens who register to vote.
  * Enhance the participation of eligible citizens as voters in elections.
  * Administer elections and protect the integrity of the electoral process.
  * Ensure that accurate and current voter registration rolls are maintained.

# Electoral Board

## Purpose and Typical Duties

The General Registrar operates under the direction and supervision of the Madison County Electoral Board and implements the election laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia. 

The Electoral Board is a three-member board appointed by the Circuit Court to administer the election laws and other regulations established by the State Board of Elections. The Electoral Board appoints the General Registrar, Election Officials and Voting Machine Custodians.

The Electoral Board can be contacted through the Madison County Voter Registrar's Office.  

## Length of Term

  3 Year Term

## Term Limitations

There are no limits on the number of terms a member may serve.

## Number of Members

3 Members

## Meeting Time and Place

The Electoral Board meets on the first Wednesday of every month at 10:00 a.m.  Meetings are held at the Registrar Office.

## Member Qualification

Two electoral board members must be of the same political party that cast the highest number of votes for the Governor at the last election; the third member must be of the political party that cast the next-to-highest number of votes in the last gubernatorial election.

## References

Section 24.2-671 of the Code of Virginia.

## Appointment Process

Members are appointed by the Circuit Court.

## Compensation

Chair and Vice-Chair: $152.50/month

Secretary: $304.92/month

## Early Voting

The Registrar's Office is open for in-person early voting beginning 45 days prior to Election Day.  

Early voting can be done in person at the Registrar's Office at 414 N Main Street, Madison, VA 22727 Monday-Friday from 8:30 am to 4:30 pm. 

Early voting will also be available at the Registrar's Office on the two Saturdays before Election Day from 9 am to 5 pm and on the two Sundays that precede the final Saturday before each election. Sunday voting hours are 12:00 pm to 5 pm. 

Early voting begins September 18, 2026 for the November 3 General Election.

EARLY VOTING IS NOT AVAILABLE ON THE DAY BEFORE ELECTION DAY.

See the attached Important Dates Flyer below for details for this election.

## Apply For An Absentee Ballot (Vote By Mail) 

An absentee ballot application must be completed so that we can mail you a ballot. 

Apply for a ballot online using information from your VA driver’s license on the Virginia Department of Elections website, or 

Email the Registrar (registrar@madisonco.virginia.gov) to request an application.

Print out an application (attached below), complete and submit it through the mail, in person, or by email.

Request an application by phone or voicemail at 540-948-6533.  State your full name, address, and how many applications you are requesting.

Applications for a mailed absentee ballot must be received in our office no later than 5:00 pm eleven days prior to Election Day.

## Completed Absentee Ballots

Completed ballots must be postmarked no later than the date of the election and received in the Registrar's Office by 12:00 pm on the Friday after the election in order to be counted.

Ballots can be mailed, dropped in person at our office at 414 N Main Street, or placed in our Official Ballot Drop Box located next to the main doors at the front of the building.
