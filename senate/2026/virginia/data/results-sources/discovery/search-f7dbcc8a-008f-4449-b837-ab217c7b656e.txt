# Register to Vote

## Voter Registration by Mail

You may apply to register to vote by mail by completing and returning a voter registration application form in the manner and time provided by law. Applications are available at:

  * Public libraries
  * Urbanna town office
  * The Voter Registration and Elections Office
  * Your local post office

The Constitution of Virginia requires that you be registered in the precinct in which you live to be qualified to vote. Any qualified resident of the Commonwealth of Virginia may make application for voter registration at the previously listed locations.

Persons already registered who need to change their name or address may do so by completing a voter registration application; contact the Voter Registration and Elections office to obtain the necessary application form.

## Voter Registration Online

For more information on registering to vote online, visit the Virginia Department of Elections website.

## Other Registration Sites

  * Department of Motor Vehicles
  * State-Designated Voter Registration Agencies
