# General Registrar & Elections

## Office of the General Registrar

It is the responsibility of this office to oversee all aspects of the local electoral process and to make sure that the voting rights of the all Wise County citizens are upheld in accordance to the Code of Virginia.

In-person, early voting for Wise County voters will be available at the locations, dates, and hours listed below beginning on the 46th day preceding all elections, unless otherwise noted. 

  * General Registrar’s Office, 5607 Patriot Drive, Wise, Monday-Friday, 8:30 AM-5:00 PM. This office will also be open on the first and second Saturdays before every election from 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; and on the second and third Sundays before every election from 12:00 PM - 5:00 PM for in-person, early voting. 

Ballot dropboxes will be available the same dates, times, and locations listed above for in-person, early voting. In addition, dropboxes will be available at the General Registrar’s Office during all hours in which the General Registrar's is open to the public.

## Tracking Mailed Ballots

If you requested a ballot by mail, you may track your ballot online.

Please be advised, ballot tracking relies on U.S. Postal Service (USPS) scanning the ballot envelope at each stage of the delivery process. If USPS fails to scan the ballot envelope at any point, gaps in ballot tracking will occur.
