# Voter Registration

The Office of the General Voter Registrar for Grayson County is committed to upholding the democratic process by providing all eligible citizens with fair, equitable, and efficient access to voter registration and participation in elections. The office is responsible for maintaining the integrity and accuracy of all records pertaining to voter registration, elections, and candidate filings.

The General Registrar and the Grayson County Electoral Board are dedicated to ensuring that every qualified citizen has the opportunity to exercise their fundamental right to vote, in accordance with the Constitution of the United States, the Constitution of the Commonwealth of Virginia, and the provisions set forth in the Code of Virginia. This commitment serves to guarantee that all elections conducted within Grayson County are administered with impartiality, transparency, and adherence to the highest standards of electoral integrity.

## General Information

Election results are available on the Virginia Department of Elections Website. To view results, select Grayson County and choose to view by Race/Question or by Precinct.

Sample ballots and other election-specific information will be posted as soon as they are available.

Polling Hours:

Polls are open from 6:00 AM to 7:00 PM on every Election Day.

Voter ID Requirements:

Voters must present an acceptable form of identification to vote a regular ballot.

If you do not bring an acceptable ID or choose not to sign an ID Confirmation Statement, you will be offered a provisional ballot.

### Absentee by Mail Voting

If you requested a ballot by mail, you may track your ballot's progress at the Virginia Department of Elections website.

Please Note: Ballot Tracking relies on the USPS scanning the ballot envelope at each state of the delivery and return process. If the USPS fails to scan the ballot envelope at any point, gaps in ballot tracking will occur.

## Overview

### Purpose of the Electoral Board

The duty of the Electoral Board is to ensure all elections are conducted fairly and impartially. It is the duty of the Registrar to provide for voter registration and voter education. The Registrar performs many electoral duties including managing efficient elections while protecting the integrity of the election process. The Constitution of Virginia requires the appointment of Electoral Boards and Registrars. The Code of Virginia, Section 24.2-114 defines the duties and powers of the Registrar. However, many other duties have evolved and are contained in various parts of Section 24.2 of the Code. Every city and county has a three-member Electoral Board. 

### Main Duties

  * Appoints the General Registrar for your locality
  * Appoints the Officers of Election for your locality
  * Trains the officers of Election
  * Removes a General Registrar or Officer of Election who fails to discharge his or her duties

### Additional Duties

  * Purchasing and maintaining the voting equipment used in your city or county
  * Preparing your Ballots
  * Administering the absentee ballot process
  * Conducting the election
  * Certifying of the results of the election
