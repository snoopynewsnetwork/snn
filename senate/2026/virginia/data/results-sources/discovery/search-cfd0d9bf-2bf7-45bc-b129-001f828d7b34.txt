for the

November 3, 2026 General and Special Elections

in

Rockingham County, Virginia

 

In-person, early voting for Rockingham County voters will be available at the location, dates, and hours listed below beginning Friday, September 18, and ending Saturday, October 31.

  * Rockingham County Administration Building, 20 East Gay Street, Harrisonburg, VA, on the following days and times:
    1. Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM-4:00 PM. 
    2. Sunday, October 18, 12:00 Noon-5:00 PM
    3. Saturday, October 24, 9:00 AM-5:00 PM
    4. Sunday, October 25, 12:00 Noon-5:00 PM
    5. Saturday, October 31, 9:00 AM-5:00 PM
  * The office will be CLOSED and early voting will not be offered on the following date:

 

  1. Monday, October 12—Columbus Day

 

A ballot drop box is available at the same location listed above for in-person, early voting. Ballot drop off locations will also be available at all regular polling places on Election Day, November 3, from 6:00 AM until 7:00 PM.

 

LISA B. GOODEN, General Registrar / Director of Elections

Mission Statement

The mission of the Voter Registrar’s Office is to:

  * Provide equal opportunity for all qualified citizens of Rockingham County to register to vote
  * Maintain accurate voter records used in elections
  * Conduct all elections at the highest level of professional standards, ensuring public confidence in the integrity of the results
  * Be an information resource for citizens regarding voter registration, absentee voting, elections, and elected officials

Absentee Voting
Absentee Ballots are only mailed to voters who request them. Click the link below to complete an absentee ballot application online or stop by the Voter Registrar's Office (20 E Gay St.) to pick up a paper application. Generally, absentee ballots are mailed out beginning 45 days prior to an election. Once you receive your absentee ballot in the mail, it can be returned (by mail, official dropbox or in-person) as soon as it is completed—Be sure to follow the enclosed instructions carefully.
