# Director of Elections / General Registrar

## Election Notice

The next election in Essex County: Tuesday, November 3, 2026 General & Special Elections.

Posted August 27, 2026

## Important Notice

The Essex County Registrar’s Office is aware that many registered voters recently received a mailer containing an absentee ballot application. The mailer did NOT originate from our office. The mailer was sent by a Third-Party Organization.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding your registration status, please feel free to call our office at 804-443-4611.

## Important Dates

Election and voting related dates and deadlines are posted on the Voter Registration calendar. If you cannot find the election information you are looking for on the calendar, please call 804-443-4611.
