Our mission is to serve the citizens of King George County and the Commonwealth by maintaining uniform practices for all elections and providing easy access to both voter registration and the casting of votes within election laws and guidelines.

## Our Duties

  * Protect the integrity of the electoral process
  * Conduct all elections efficiently and under the provisions of Title 24.2 of the Code of Virginia
  * Implement the National Voter Registration Act
  * Provide on-site registration opportunities to high school students and naturalized citizens
  * Maintain accurate voter registration records
  * Maintain a continuous professional level of service to the public.

## Official Voting Information for King George County

The constitution of Virginia requires that you be registered in the precinct in which you live to be qualified to vote. Current law requires everyone who is voting to have a valid ID.

# Election Officers

On Election Day more than 1.4 million Americans work at the polls-but more are needed. By working at the polls you will be supporting the democratic values on which our nation was built.

Serving as an election officer is a rewarding way to participate in your government and help us ensure a fair and well-organized voting process. King George County needs approximately 50 dependable and committed citizens to serve as election officers in its 5 precincts for each General Election.

## Qualifications

  * You must be a registered voter of the Commonwealth of Virginia.
  * You cannot hold elected office or be the employee of an elected official.
  * You must enjoy meeting people and serving the public.
  * You must be able to take direction, pay attention to detail and not be easily distracted.
  * You must be available to work the entire day on Election Day.

## Election Day Responsibilities

Election Officers are required to...

  * Arrive at the polling place at 5 a.m. on Election Day and remain until all tallies have been completed.
  * Set up voting equipment and prepare the polling place for voting.
  * Process voters by checking names on list of registered voters.
  * Direct voters to voting machines.
  * Secure voting machines (Code of Virginia, §24.2-1009: Stealing or tampering with ballot containers, voting or registration equipment, software, records or document is a Class 5 felony).
  * Close the polling place after the election.

## Training

Each election officer is required to attend a training class. Chief and Assistant Chief election officers are required by the Code of Virginia, § 24.2-115, to attend training prior to every election in which they serve.

## FAQs

### 1. Where will I be working?

Whenever possible, Election Officers are assigned to their "home" precinct. However, if there are no vacancies at your polling place, you may be assigned to a nearby precinct.

### 2. What will I be paid?

Your pay will depend on the duty you perform at your precinct.

### 3. How do I apply?

Please email us with the following information:

  * full name
  * date of birth  
  * current address
  * daytime telephone number
  * preference to represent either the Democratic or Republican party on Election Day, as mandated by the Code of Virginia. 

You may also apply by calling our office at 540-775-9186. The King George County Electoral Board greatly appreciates those citizens who help us to make our elections work!
