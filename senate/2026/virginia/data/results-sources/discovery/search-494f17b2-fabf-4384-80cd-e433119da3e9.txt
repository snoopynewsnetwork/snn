November 3, 2026 - General and Special
Mecklenburg County voters will have multiple options for voting ahead of November 3, 2026. Virginia offers three distinct ways in which all eligible voters can choose to vote. 
Early Voting In-Person
Any qualified registered Mecklenburg County voter can vote early in the General Registrar’s Office. Early voting begins on Friday, September 18, 2026 and will end on Saturday, October 31, 2026. The Mecklenburg County General Registrar’s Office will be open Monday - Friday; from 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM to accommodate any voters who wish to vote early. Additionally, the General Registrar’s Office will be open on Sunday October 18, 2026 from 11:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Saturday, October 24, 2026 from 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Sunday, October 25, 2026 from 11:00 AM - 4:00 PM, and Saturday, October 31, 2026 from 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM for Early Voting. Saturday October 31, 2026 will be the last day to early vote. 
Absentee Voting By-Mail
Any registered voter may submit an application to receive an absentee ballot through the mail. We will begin mailing ballots prior to September 18, 2026. To request an Absentee Ballot By-Mail apply online at https://vote.elections.virginia.gov, you may also contact the General Registrar’s Office to request an application. October 23, 2026 is the last day to request an Absentee Ballot By-Mail. 
Election Day Voting
In-person voting will continue to be available at all regular polling locations in Mecklenburg County on November 3, 2026. Precincts will open at 6:00 AM and close at 7:00 PM. If you are in line at 7:00 PM, you will be able to vote. Do NOT get out of line if you are in line to vote at 7:00 PM.
To find out where you vote on Election Day please visit http://vote.elections.virginia.gov or contact the General Registrar’s Office

Welcome to Mecklenburg Elections
​
The duty of Office of the Electoral Board and the General Registrar of Mecklenburg County is to serve the citizens and the Commonwealth by:

  * Providing fair, honest, and lawful elections
  * Being uniform in our practices for all elections
  * Providing easy and readily available access to registration and casting of votes within election laws and guidelines
  * Protecting the integrity of the electoral process and efficiently conduct all elections under the provisions of Title 24.2 of the Code of Virginia
  * Implementing the National Voter Registration Act
  * Providing on-site registration opportunities to high school students and naturalized citizens
  * Encouraging all eligible residents to exercise their right to vote
  * Maintaining accurate voter registration records
  * Conducting elections in a fair, accurate and efficient manner
  * Maintaining a continuous professional level of service to the public

# Registrar's Office / Voting

## This Office registers voters and maintains voting records for the County's nine electoral districts. Any U.S. citizen and Virginia resident who is 18 years or older on Election Day may vote. To register, contact the Registrar's Office or visit us at our office.
