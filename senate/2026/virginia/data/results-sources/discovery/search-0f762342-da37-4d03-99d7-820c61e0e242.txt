Williamsburg News – All

Posted on September 15, 2026

  * ### Early Voting for November General Election Begins Friday, Sept. 18

Early voting for the November General Election begins Friday, Sept. 18, 2026.

Ballots for City of Williamsburg voters include the races for:

    * U.S. Senate
    * House of Representatives – 1st District
    * Williamsburg City Council

The ballots also included three proposed constitutional amendment questions. See a sample ballot at williamsburgva.gov/vote.

City voters can cast their ballots in-person at the Municipal Building, 401 Lafayette St., in the Registrar’s Office between 8 a.m. and 4:30 p.m. Monday through Friday until Friday, Oct. 30, 2026. The office will also be open two Saturdays and two Sundays during this election season:

    * Sunday, Oct. 18: 11 a.m.-4 p.m. 
    * Saturday, Oct. 24: 8 a.m.-4:30 p.m.
    * Sunday, Oct. 25: 11 a.m.-4 p.m.
    * Saturday, Oct. 31: 8 a.m.-5 p.m.

Election Day is Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026; voters will cast their ballots at their designated polling place, either the Community Building or the Williamsburg United Methodist Church. To see a precinct map for polling locations on Election Day, visit williamsburgva.gov/vote.
