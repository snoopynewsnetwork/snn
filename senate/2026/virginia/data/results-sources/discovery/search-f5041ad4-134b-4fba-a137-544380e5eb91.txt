## Important Dates

### Registration

Register to Vote/Update Registration deadline for the Primary: October 23, 2026

  * Voter Registration Office at the Government Center – 5 p.m. Check additional state agencies or the DMV for their operating hours.
  * By mail – applications must be postmarked by October 23, 2026.
  * Online voter registration portal on the Virginia Department of Elections website – available until 11:59 p.m. on October 23, 2026.
Paper applications mailed must be postmarked by October 23, 2026.
Paper applications submitted in person to the Voter Registration Office must be received by 5 p.m. on October 23, 2026.

### Absentee Ballots

Last day to request a ballot by mail: October 23, 2026, at 5 p.m.

Complete the form and mail, email or fax it to:  Voter Registration Office, 18 Government Center Lane, PO Box 590, Verona, VA  24482, registrar@co.augusta.va.us,  540-245-5037 fax.  

Carefully review the instructions to complete and return your ballot to your local registrar by 7:00 p.m. on Election Day. If you are returning your ballot by-mail, it must be postmarked on or before Election Day and received by your registrar by noon on the third day after the election.

If you have submitted an absentee ballot application for a ballot to be sent by mail, you can track your ballot by logging into Ballot Scout through the Virginia Department of Elections to track its progress. Make sure you enter your name exactly as it is on your registration to vote. 

Go to our page for more information on Absentee Voting.

When Do Elections Take Place?
General Elections are held in Augusta County every November on the Tuesday after the first Monday. If the political parties call for a Primary Election, it is always held on the second Tuesday in June. Special Elections are held as necessary.
