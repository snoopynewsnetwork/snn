Polling places are open from 6:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. on Election Day. Use the Virginia Department of Elections Polling Place Lookup to find your assigned polling location and view your sample ballot.

Offices on the Ballot:

  * Republican Primary for U.S. Senate
