# Voter Registration & Elections

Image: GE 2026 Sample Ballot in yellow 08312026_Page_1Image: GE 2026 Sample Ballot in yellow 08312026_Page_2

## Responsibilities of the Office of Voter Registration and Elections include: 

  * Protecting the integrity of the electoral process and efficiently conduct all elections under the provisions of Title 24.2 of the Code of Virginia
  * Implementing the National Voter Registration Act
  * Providing on-site registration opportunities to high school students and naturalized citizens
  * Maintaining accurate voter registration records

Our mission is to serve the citizens of Radford and the Commonwealth by providing fair and honest elections; to administer the election laws by being uniform in our practices for all elections; to provide easy and readily available access to registration and casting of votes within election laws and guidelines.

# Electoral Board

Electoral Board Meeting Minutes

Electoral Board Members

  1. Chris Quesenberry, Chair, Chris.QuesenberryChris.Quesenberry@radfordva.gov  
  2. Charlie Stewart, Vice-Chair, Charles.Stewart@radfordva.gov 
  3. Virginia Ayers, Secretary,  Virginia,Ayers@radfordva.gov

The members of the Electoral Board elect a Chairman and a Secretary, each of whom represent a different political party. The remaining member serves as Vice-chairman. The Chairman presides over meetings of the Board, and the Secretary is responsible for record-keeping and administrative duties.

Responsibilities

The Radford City Electoral Board is responsible for the administration of elections in the City of Radford, including appointing a General Registrar, maintaining equipment and supplies for voting, and tallying votes.

Purpose

Pursuant to Section 24.2-106 of the Code of Virginia, the judges of the Circuit Court for the City of Radford appoints three members to serve staggered three-year terms on the Radford City Electoral Board. At least two of these members are selected from nominations by the political party whose candidate for Governor won the last election, and the other is chosen from the party with the second highest number of votes for Governor. Members of the Electoral Board may not run for or hold elected office, may not chair a political party committee, and may not work as a paid campaign worker for a political candidate.
