ELECTORAL BOARD MINUTES
 

The Office of the General Registrar of Lee County strives to promote the highest level of service to the citizens of Lee County by providing equitable opportunities to register and vote; and by maintaining accurate records relating to voter registration, elections and candidates. The Electoral Board and Registrar are committed to ensuring fair and impartial elections. It is very important to us that each citizen has the opportunity to exercise his or her right to vote in accordance with the Constitution of the United States and the Election Code of the Commonwealth of Virginia.

The Lee County Electoral Board will meet Wednesday June 18, 2025 at 10:00 AM in the Registrar and Electoral Board office located at 133 School Board Place, Jonesville, VA  24263.

The purpose of the meeting is to determine the validity of provisional ballots cast in the June 17, 2025 Democratic Primary Election.

The Electoral Board will conduct the canvass of the June 17, 2025 Primary Election immediately after the provisional ballot meeting.
