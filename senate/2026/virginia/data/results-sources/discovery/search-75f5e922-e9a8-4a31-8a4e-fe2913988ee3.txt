# Voter Information

Registration Information

Where:  You may apply online or at the following sites:

  * County Registrar Office
  * Public Library
  * Department of Motor Vehicles Offices
  * Pub­lic Assistance Agency offices, i.e., those offering food stamps, aid to depen­dent children and/or aid to persons with disabilities, etc.
  * Military Recruitment Offices
  * District Governmental Centers

For other locations and for more information contact your General Registrar.

When:  You may register year round except during the 21 days prior to a general or primary election or 13 days prior to a special general election. Registration will be permanent under most circumstances.

If You Move: To remain a qualified voter, you must notify your local voter registration office of any change in your name or address.

For information, contact your General Registrar, Joyce Pace, jpace@fluvannacounty.org or (434) 589-3593.
