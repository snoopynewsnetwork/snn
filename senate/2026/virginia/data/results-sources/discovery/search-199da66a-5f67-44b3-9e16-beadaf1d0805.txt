# Office of Elections

## November 3, 2026 General Election

In addition to the races for United States Senate and U.S. House of Representatives, 1st District, James City County voters will consider three proposed amendments to the Constitution of Virginia in the November 3, 2026 Election.

For more information about each proposed constitutional amendment, visit the Virginia Department of Elections using the links below:

  * Proposed Constitutional Amendment Question 1
  * Proposed Constitutional Amendment Question 2
  * Proposed Constitutional Amendment Question 3

### Early Voting

Voting early in person? During the Early Voting period, all James City County voters must vote at the designated Vote Center located at the Office of Elections, 4095 Ironbound Road, Williamsburg, VA 23188.

Please note: This applies to Early Voting only. On Election Day, November 3, 2026, voters must vote at their designated Election Day polling place.

## About the Director of Elections

The Director of Elections registers voters and updates records of voters for changes of address and administers elections with the Electoral Board, whose primary responsibility is to oversee the elections. The Director is appointed by the Electoral Board, whose members are appointed by the Circuit Court Judge for 3-year staggered terms. Two members represent the political party receiving the most votes in the last gubernatorial election and 1 member represents the party receiving the second-highest votes.
