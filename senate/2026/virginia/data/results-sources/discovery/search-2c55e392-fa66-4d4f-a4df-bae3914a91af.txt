# Election Notice
November 3, 2026 General Election

 SAMPLE BALLOT (TWO SIDED) 

A statewide GENERAL ELECTION will be held in the Commonwealth of Virginia on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. The following items will appear on the ballot for the City of Salem:

  * United States Senate
  * United States House of Representatives District 6
  * Salem City Council - 2 Seats
  * Three Constitutional Amendments

## REGISTER TO VOTE BY OCTOBER 23, 2026

  * Friday, October 23, 2026 is the last day to register to vote or update your voter registration.
    * Mailed registration applications must be postmarked on or before this date.
  *  Same Day Registration (SDR) is available. These ballots are provisional and will be counted after the election, if accepted.

## EARLY VOTING

Vote early at the City of Salem General Registrar’s Office 

Register online

Or in person at the address below:

19 East Clay Street
P.O. Box 203
Salem, VA 24153

Do I need special information to register?
    

You must provide your full legal name, home address, date of birth, and the last four digits of your Social Security number, under penalty of perjury.

Do I have to register by party affiliation?
    

No. The Commonwealth of Virginia does not have registration by party.

Where will I vote?
    

The registrar will send you a voter registration letter with the name and location of your polling place. Find out where to vote by your address

Do I have to state my political party preference?
Do I have to register for this election?
    

No, your registration remains valid, if you do not move from your original address.
