# Register to Vote

The Constitution of Virginia requires that you be registered in the precinct in which you live in order to be qualified to vote.

### Get Registered

For the convenience of the citizens of York County and other Virginia residents, the following locations are available for the purpose of voter registration:

### Registration Applications

Citizens who have a Virginia DMV issued ID are able to register to vote or update their registration online using the Department of Elections Citizen Portal.

Citizens may also print an application to mail to our office. Please note that the print application cannot be submitted by email or fax.

## Felony Convictions and Voter Eligibility

Under a recent federal court order, individuals convicted of a felony can only lose their voting rights in Virginia if they were convicted of certain “common law felonies.” 

As a result, many individuals who were previously convicted of felonies and released from incarceration may now be eligible to register to vote.

The Department of Elections website has additional information.
