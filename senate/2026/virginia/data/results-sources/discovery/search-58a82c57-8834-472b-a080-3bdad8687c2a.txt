# Electoral Board

## About the Board

New Kent County Electoral Board is appointed pursuant to Virginia Code Section 24.2-106 to administer elections. Three members are appointed by the New Kent County Circuit Court Judge and each serves a three year staggered term ending on the last day of December. The Board meets as needed in the office of the Voter Registrar.

### Contact Us

If you have questions about the Electoral Board or are interested in serving as an Officer of Elections, please contact the Voter Registrar at (804) 966-9699 or by email.

# Registration & Absentee Deadlines

## Register to vote or update an existing registration for the November 3, 2026 General and Special Election

Early voting begins on Friday, September 18, 2026, for the November 3, 2026 General and Special Election and is held in the large conference room in the Health and Human Services Building.  

•        The final day to register to vote in the November 3, 2026 General and Special Election is Friday, October 23, 2026
           5:00 p.m. in the office and 11:59 p.m. online

•        The last day to apply for an absentee ballot to be mailed to you is Friday, October 23, 2026, by 5 p.m.

•        The office will be open on Saturday, October 24 & Saturday, October 31, 2026
           9:00 a.m. until 5:00 p.m.  for the purpose of in person voting

•        The office will be open on Sunday, October 18 and Sunday, October 25
           11:00 a.m. until 5:00 p.m.  for the purpose of in person voting

•        Saturday, October 31, 2026, is the final day for voting in person at the office

  * The drop box located in front of the Health and Human Services Bldg. 7911 Courthouse Way is available 24 hours a day until 7 pm on election day.  All completed ballots may be placed there by the voter.
Register to vote or update an existing registration and/or apply for an absentee ballot online using the Citizen Portal.

## Registration

The Constitution of Virginia requires that you be registered in the precinct in which you live in order to be qualified to vote.

Application for voter registration may be made in person or by mail. Mail-in voter registration applications are available at the Heritage Library in Providence Forge and at the County Administration Building in New Kent. Voter Registration is also available at all DMV offices and at public assistance agencies.

 Registration Deadlines

  * General and Primary Elections: 11 days before election date
  * Special Election: 13 days before election date
  * Special Election called by the Governor: 7 days before election date

 Voting by Absentee Ballot

Beginning 45 days prior to the election, in-person voting is available in the Voter Registration Office during normal office hours, and 9 a.m. until 5 p.m. on the Saturday preceding any Primary Election and the two Saturdays preceding any General Election (§ 24.2-701 and § 24.2-703). For more information contact the General Registrar.
