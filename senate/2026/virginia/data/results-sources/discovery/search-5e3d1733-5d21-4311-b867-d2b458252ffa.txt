# Voter Registration

The deadline to register to vote for the November 3, 2026 General Election is Friday, October 9, 2026.

## Qualifications to Register to Vote in Brunswick County

To register to vote in Brunswick County, you must:

  1. Be a citizen of the United States
  2. Be a resident of Brunswick County, North Carolina, for 30 days before the election in which you intend to vote
  3. Be 18 years of age or older by next general election day
  4. Not be serving a felony sentence, including any period of probation, post-release supervision, or parole.

## How to Register

### Register to Vote Online Through the DMV

If you are a DMV customer with an NC driver's license or DMV-issued ID, you may register to vote or change certain parts of your registration online. You may use this service to register, update your address, and update your party affiliation. You may not change your name. 

Register online

  * Voter registration through the DMV is free.
  * You don’t need to create a special account with the DMV. You can select “Continue as Guest” on the DMV’s website and proceed directly to submitting your voter registration.

### Print a Voter Registration Form

Complete the Voter Registration Form (PDF) and return it to our office. If you are a new registrant in Brunswick County, we need the original form (sent via mail or drop off at our office).  If you are already registered in Brunswick County and are making updates to your information, the form can be returned by email (elections@brunswickcountync.gov) or fax.

### Changes to My Registration

There are several ways an existing voter can make changes to their voter registration record.

  * Address and party changes may be made online through the DMV.
  * Make changes on your voter registration card and mail it back to our office.
  * Complete a Voter Registration Application (PDF) and return it by mail, e (elections@brunswickcountync.gov)mail (elections@brunswickcountync.gov), or fax.

## Same Day Registration

If a Brunswick County resident misses the voter registration deadline, they may go to any early voting site to register and vote the same day. The individual will be required to provide acceptable proof of residence before they can become registered. Same-day registration is only available during early voting; it is not available on election day.

# 2026 Elections

Voters can check wait times at all early voting locations using the Wait Time Tracker.

Early Voting Turnout Statistics are posted every day during early voting after all locations have closed.  

The statewide general election will be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

Voters will be asked to show photo ID when they vote. All voters will be allowed to vote with or without ID. Voters who lack ID can get one for free from their county board of elections. Find out more at ncsbe.gov/voter-id.

## Election Day Voting

Polls will be open from 6:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. on Election Day. Registered voters should go to their designated precinct.  The name, location, and, address of your Election Day precinct may be found by checking your record using the Voter Lookup Tool. Learn more on our Election Day Voting page.

## Early Voting

Early voting will be held at the following locations from Thursday, October 15, 2026, through Saturday, October 31, 2026.  
  * NC House District 17 Sample Ballot (PDF)
  * NC House District 19 Sample Ballot (PDF)

Sample ballots may be printed and marked in advance and brought with you to the polling place for reference. You may also save your sample ballot to your mobile device and mark it digitally for personal use. Please note:

  * Mobile devices may not be used to communicate with others while voting.
  * Photographing your voted ballot is not permitted.

## Voter Registration Deadline

All persons who are registered to vote with the Brunswick County Board of Elections may vote in this election. The voter registration deadline for this election is 5 p.m. Friday, October 9, 2026. Eligible individuals who are not registered by that deadline may register and vote at any early voting site during the early voting period. Individuals registering to vote at an early voting site will be required to provide current documentation of their residence (for example, a government ID, other government document, or a paycheck, bank statement, or utility bill). Learn more about voter registration on our Voter Registration page.

## Accessibility

Voters voting in person are entitled to assistance by an election official, or, if assistance is needed due to disability or illiteracy, by a qualified person of their choice. Voting sites are accessible to all voters.  Curbside voting is available for voters who are not able to enter voting sites without physical assistance because of age or physical disability. All voting locations have an accessible ballot marking device available for any voter to use upon request.  Learn more about accessible voting options on our Accessible Voting page.
