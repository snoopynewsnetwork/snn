# Registrar

Citizens wishing to track the location of their mail-in ballot can do so online via our ballot tracking tool.

Citizens wishing to register to vote, update their voter registration information, or request an absentee ballot are encouraged to visit the Virginia Department of Elections website or scan the QR code below:

Image: QR Code Leading to Virginia Department of Elections Website

## Responsibilities

The General Registrar and the Electoral Board are responsible for managing all activities related to voter registration and the administration of all elections in Patrick County.
