# Registrar's Office

## November 3, 2026 Election

Local offices on the ballot for the Nov. 3, 2026 election include: 

  * Norton City Council: Three (3) seats
  * Norton City School Board: Three (3) seats

## Early Voting

### Early Voting for the Nov. 3, 2026 General & Special Elections (Dates/Times)

• Monday – Friday (starting Sept. 18): 8:30 a.m.-5 p.m.
• Sunday, Oct. 18: Noon-5 p.m.
• Saturday, Oct. 24: 9 a.m.-5 p.m.
• Sunday, Oct. 25: Noon-5 p.m.
• Saturday, Oct. 31: 9 a.m.-5 p.m. 

### Other Important Info:

• The office will be closed and there will be no voting on Monday, Oct. 12 due to the observance of Columbus Day.
• The last day to register to vote or update your registration information is Oct. 23, 2026
• The last day to request a ballot by mail is Friday, Oct. 23, 2026 by 5 p.m.
• The last day to vote early in person is Saturday, Oct. 31, 2026

• The office may be closed for 30 minutes between  noon and 2 p.m. on weekdays.

At the first meeting in January following each councilmanic election, or as soon thereafter as practicable, the council shall appoint the officers provided for in this charter and general laws. (1988, c. 134; 2023, cc. 62, 63)

Chapter 5. Schools.

§ 5.1. Creation of separate division.  
The territory embraced within the corporate limits of the City of Norton as hereinabove described or as may be extended or contracted in the future, shall constitute a separate division for school purposes and government. Such division shall be known as the Norton City School, City of Norton, Virginia, and shall be a separate and distinct unit within itself, insofar as the Constitution of Virginia permits. (1988, c. 134)

§ 5.2. Election of school board members; terms.  
The school division shall have a board of five members elected at large by the qualified voters of the city for staggered terms of four years. The election and terms of school board members shall coincide with those of council members such that two school board members shall be elected at the general election in November 2024, and every four years thereafter, and three school board members shall be elected at the general election in November 2026, and every four years thereafter.
