## Current rules and public outstanding-ballot evidence

Current official statute pages were retained, not inferred from an old election guide. They reflect the Legislature’s October 2026 extraction; the 2018 scanned RCV rules still linked by SOS are preserved as older materials and do not override amended law.

- **Polls:** [21-A §626](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/statutes/21-A/title21-Asec626.html) permits local opening from 6–8 a.m.; municipalities below 500 may open as late as 10 a.m. The November workbook actually has 6, 7, 7:30, 8, 9 and 10 a.m. entries. Regular closing is 8 p.m.; municipalities below 100 may close after all registered voters vote, and voters present at closing may vote. [§673(6)](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/statutes/21-A/title21-Asec673.html) requires court-extension ballots in federal elections to be challenged, segregated and counted separately. Preserve a real extension/order, not a statewide assumed close.
- **Time:** ordinary November3 8 p.m. Eastern standard time converts to **November4 01:00 UTC**, using `America/New_York`. [Maine 1 §151](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/1/title1sec151.pdf) contains a conditional year-round daylight provision requiring federal permission/SOS notice. The current [federal time rule](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title15-section260a&num=0&edition=prelim) was web-readable; direct US Code attempts returned retained500s, DOT returned403. Reconfirm changes/orders at preflight; do not infer timezone from a timestamp lacking an offset.
- **Absentee eligibility/request:** [§751](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/statutes/21-A/title21-Asec751.html) provides no-excuse absentee voting for a properly requesting voter. [§753-B](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/statutes/21-A/title21-Asec753-B.html) restricts ordinary issuance after the third business day before election—**Thursday October29**, subject to local office hours—and provides enumerated special circumstances. Current SOS guidance says any non-UOCAVA voter may apply for ongoing absentee status as of December31,2025; older municipal age/disability-only wording must not control current eligibility.
- **Receipt/cure:** [§755](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/statutes/21-A/title21-Asec755.html) requires delivery before polls close. [§756](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/statutes/21-A/title21-Asec756.html) and [§756-A](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/statutes/21-A/title21-Asec756-A.html) distinguish signature mismatch, missing signature and aide/witness defects. Notification generally occurs within one business day, with election-eve/day good-faith handling. Cure procedures depend on the defect and method, use the ballot-return deadline, and can result in accepted-but-challenged rather than accepted-without-challenge or rejected. No general post-election cure period is assumed.
- **UOCAVA:** [§777-A](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/statutes/21-A/title21-Asec777-A.html) and [§781-A](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/statutes/21-A/title21-Asec781-A.html) specify noon the day before election for registration/request; current SOS guidance requires voted ballots, mailed or electronic, by 8 p.m. election day. No postmark grace period is imported from another state.
- **Early processing:** [§760-B](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/statutes/21-A/title21-Asec760-B.html) permits processing beginning seven days before election, with notices/inspection/security requirements. The actual linked November notice lists October27–November2 schedules and locations. [§759](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/statutes/21-A/title21-Asec759.html) separates processing from counting/releasing results after polls close and processing/casting requirements are met. A processing schedule says neither how many remain nor when a public upload occurs.
- **Municipal returns:** [§711](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/statutes/21-A/title21-Asec711.html) requires ward/precinct first-choice returns and ballots-cast counts, municipal aggregation where applicable, signed/attested copies and filing within **two business days**; the second-day 5 p.m. courier provision is explicit. For this election that second business day is November5. The statute permits documented obvious-error corrections to attested copies while preserving the warden’s original.
- **State final/corrected results:** [§722](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/statutes/21-A/title21-Asec722.html) sets tabulation/certified submission within **20 days** (November23), except pending recounts; permits correction from certified local records; and reports declared write-ins individually at ≥5%, otherwise “others.” [§724](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/statutes/21-A/title21-Asec724.html) concerns election certificates within a reasonable time. Certification is not a same-night reporting flag.
- **RCV stages:** current [§723-A](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/statutes/21-A/title21-Asec723-A.html), including 2025 amendments, first tests **more than 50% of all ballots cast for the office**, including blank first ranks, first-rank overvotes and first ranks for excluded candidates. Later rounds count each continuing ballot for its highest continuing candidate and separately track exhausted ballots. With **two or fewer continuing candidates, the most votes wins**; otherwise the last-place candidate is removed. A late excluded candidate is defined separately and skipped for the next continuing preference under subsection2-A. Do not replace the first denominator with candidate-only votes, or invent a later-round early-majority stop. The complete CVR must be posted when certified results are reported.
- **Recounts:** [§737-A](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/statutes/21-A/title21-Asec737-A.html) and the [current SOS recount explanation](https://www3.maine.gov/sos/news/maine-secretary-states-office-announces-recount-schedule) distinguish ordinary five-business-day requests from RCV requests by eligible top-three penultimate-round candidates within five business days after results are announced. Recounts occur as soon as reasonably possible, may proceed in stages and can produce corrected certified tabulations. The June19 RCV completion notice demonstrates a historical schedule, not a promised November completion date; no current November central-count schedule was found.

The current [SOS Voter Data page](https://www3.maine.gov/sos/elections-voting/voter-data) explicitly publishes request/issue/return records, warns of municipal entry lag, and says UOCAVA requests/issues are included. Its actual October16 text file returned **206**, `Content-Range: bytes 0-65535/16143837`; all 65,536 requested bytes survive. This is a complete HTTP range response and an **incomplete statewide file**. Its actual header has **23 pipe-delimited columns**, unlike the published layout’s additional name fields. Native fields include residence municipality, designators, voter record number, party, W-P/districts, ballot type, request/issue/receipt methods and dates, receipt time, duplicate sequence, status, challenge and rejection codes. The [layout](https://www3.maine.gov/sos/elections-voting/absenteelayout) names ACT/ACU/ACH/PEN/REJ/RNC statuses; the source’s original received-time field has no timezone.

Requests, issues, returns, pending cure, challenges and rejections are not all uncounted eligible ballots, and duplicate/replacement records matter. This sample is neither a full statewide status census nor a count of ballots awaiting tabulation. No exact remaining Senate count was inferred from it, registration, issued ballots, precinct percentages or early-processing notices.

## Ingestion constraints and remaining gates

Treat each publication as a versioned cumulative observation unless its actual semantics establish otherwise. Retain old and replacement counts, including decreases; never add successive downloads. Preserve election, candidate, native unit, source row, method, administrative group and RCV round identities. A local official label does not erase a contradictory state export or establish a release chronology. Reject old elections, ballots/placeholders, application shells and test-mode products as November votes.

State, county, municipal, ward/method, local preliminaries, corrected/final exports and RCV rounds overlap. Never sum overlapping views or add neighboring-state/regional totals to Maine. Source coverage/completion is not vote completion. Source HTTP Date/Last-Modified, document clocks, source as-of dates and UTC retrieval are separate fields; no observed vote-arrival sequence or guaranteed/client vote refresh was measured.

Before live use, resolve the current Senate feed/IDs and complete authority/boundary roster, T22 MD host, exact municipal/method crosswalks, all pending candidate exclusions, raw access to the nine blocked priorities, Portland’s actual result catalog, complete statewide outstanding-ballot semantics, genuine timestamped arrivals/revisions and the local/RCV discrepancies above. In particular, the coordinator’s historical BDN June primary residual813, null/duplicate VCU IDs, method zeros and stale flags are not official unit identities or STATE UOCAVA allocations.
