## Municipal geography, RCV and source precedence

The saved BDN primary JSON was independently checked beyond the page shell. Its 16 county arrays contain **514 native VCU records**, whose four candidate sums exactly reproduce each county. Those county sums are **813 votes below the 216,402 topline**: Costello 63, Mills 146, Platner 596 and Laflamme 8. That remainder is not geographically allocated here; its source is unestablished. The source's 514/514 flag and zero method/estimate fields cannot resolve it. Positive votes coexist with zero method subtotals, so those zeros are not verified statements that no absentee/Election Day votes occurred.

Native IDs also need care: 27 VCU rows have null IDs, and Madawaska occurs twice under the same nonnull ID `5142` with distinct positive counts. Both rows remain; no silent deduplication or invented ward assignment is made. VCU `fips` values are five-digit local codes, not county FIPS. The 502-name town lookup is a different inventory from the 514 VCU rows or the 512 historical Senate baseline. `uncontested:true` accompanies four candidate identities, and a `call_times` candidate ID `8245` is absent from that roster. [Offline inspection](data/news/inspection.json) retains hashes, exact scope arithmetic and duplicate-ID details; [script](data/news/inspect_evidence.py) reproduces the checks. These are historical schema limitations, not validation of a November collector.

Maine's town/city/plantation and other election reporting units must be retained. A county-level newsroom map can be useful, but it does not replace the municipal officials who report returns. Match source-native town identifiers and names to the official baseline; do not join precinct numbers without a jurisdiction key or allocate unorganized territory by assumption. The [official results inventory](official-results.md) defines full-state coverage and the [turnout report](turnout.md) defines the exact deep-inspection set.

The [Secretary of State's current RCV resources](https://www.maine.gov/sos/elections-voting/upcoming/ranked-choice-voting-tabs), updated October 6 in research-browser text, include a November Senate sample ballot. Direct HTTP to that page returned 403 in this environment. Keep municipal first-choice returns separate from centrally tabulated later rounds and final/certified results. An early lead or a newsroom call is not an additional count. Do not relabel an independent candidate Democratic or silently merge independent and Democratic historical coalitions.

The current [RCV statute](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/21-A/title21-Asec723-A.html), retained HTTP 200, has 2025 amendments affecting the first-choice majority denominator and excluded candidates. Its ballot denominator includes first-rank blanks, overvotes and excluded-candidate rankings; later counts use continuing ballots and separate exhaustion. With two or fewer continuing candidates, the most votes determines the winner. Historical simplified newsroom captions must not replace these current rules or be applied retroactively to a different contest vintage.

Preserve election/contest/candidate ID, municipality, voting method where available, round, count, publisher time, retrieval UTC and source status. Compare the same geography and round; never add statewide totals to town subtotals, mix different RCV rounds, or add two publishers' cumulative votes. BDN/DDHQ and AP products have different reported collection chains, but may ultimately share clerks' releases. Track shared upstream observations and measure lag rather than claiming statistical independence from branding.

A news observation can temporarily stand in for missing official data only with explicit source/time, unit coverage and substitution reason. Reconcile it with the official clerk/state record when available. Retain negative revisions and candidate/municipal corrections rather than silently forcing monotonically increasing totals. Keep incomplete towns, unallocated administrative units and uncertain remaining ballots visible.

Before November, verify active candidate IDs following ballot replacements, actual Senate election selection, first-choice/round semantics, town coverage, current access and supplier credits. No inspected page establishes a complete tested November Senate feed. Preserve NYT and other external calls, estimated completion and forecasts separately from observed candidate counts. The [collection strategy](live-polling-strategy.md) specifies proposed request intervals and manual operation; none is a guaranteed publisher cadence.
