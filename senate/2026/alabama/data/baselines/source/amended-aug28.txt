Dear Secretary Allen:

The Alabama Republican Party has been made aware of a race and candidate that was
inadvertently left off our previously certified list of results. Allen Treadaway, Republican
Candidate for House District 51, was previously certified in February by the Party as having
qualified for office. In the Party’s certification letter after the May 19 primary, we erroneously
omitted his name from the list of certified candidates. The ALGOP has been made aware of
this by the Office of the Jefferson County Probate Judge (the only county with HD51 on the
ballot) and we are hoping to get your assistance in remedying this error.

Concurrently with the delivery of this letter, we are also delivering a corrected certified list of
candidates.

Thank you in advance for your assistance with this, and please let us know if there is anything
that we can do to facilitate the correction of this error.

                                       Very truly yours,




                                       Scott Stadthagen
