L109: ## cite82†View your sample ballot†myballotmn.sos.mn.gov L110: 
L111:   * The sample ballot shows a list of the candidates and ballot questions you'll be voting on.
L112:   * Information is posted about 45 days before an election.
L113:   * A sample ballot is not available for all local elections. You may need to contact the local government (city, town or school district) holding the election.
L114: ## Ballot Questions
L115: 
L116: Questions that will be on November 3, 2026 state general election ballots are compiled in the cite83†Questions on 2026 General Election Ballot spreadsheet. Includes ballot questions shared with this office as of 9/23/2026.
L117: 
L118: ## Proposed Constitutional Amendment
L119: 
L120: In 2026, there will be one proposed constitutional amendment on the ballot in Minnesota. The cite79†Constitutional Amendments page has the ballot language, ballot instructions, and other information about the proposed amendment.
L121: ## What's on the Election Day ballot in 2026?
L122: 
L123: All voters will have these races on their general election ballot:
L124: 
L125:   * U.S. Senator
L126:   * U.S. Representative
L127:   * Governor & Lt. Governor
L128:   * Secretary of State
L129:   * State Auditor
L130:   * Attorney General
L131:   * State Senator
L132:   * State Representative
L133:   * Constitutional Amendment
L134:   * Judicial Seats
L135: 
L136: Voters may have one or more of these races on their ballot:
L137:   * County Officials
L138:   * City Officers
L139:   * School Board Members
L140:   * Township Officers
L141:   * Local ballot questions
L142: ## Special Elections
L143: 
L144: Special elections are used to fill vacant offices or decide ballot questions; they can be scheduled at various times during the year, and may be held in conjunction with a regularly scheduled election.
