L192: ## Voter Registration
L193: ### Pre-Registered Voters
L194: 
L195: Minnesota voters may pre-register up to three weeks before Election Day. Total voters who pre-registered before the general election was 3,686,596. In the map image below, counties with darker shades had a higher percentage of eligible voters pre-registered. County percentages ranged from 74.6% to 98.0%. L229: ## Voter Turnout
L230: ### Total Turnout
L231: Voter turnout is the number of voters divided by the number of eligible voters. The total number of voters in the 2024 general election was 3,272,414. This office does not have exact eligible voter data, so uses statewide data from the cite81†United States Election Project†www.electproject.org on election night. cite82†Citizen Voting Age Population (CVAP)†www.census.gov data from the U.S. Census is less exact, but available by county. In the map image below, counties with darker shades had higher turnout.
L232: County percentages ranged from 59.9% to 88.6%. Click on the image to view a pdf with rates for each county.

L282: Some areas in Minnesota are designated to vote by "mail ballot" which means that ballots are mailed to registered voters and voters do not go to a normal polling place on Election Day. About 117 thousand voters voted this way for the 2024 general election. In the map image below, counties with darker shades had a higher percentage of votes cast by mail voting. Fifteen counties had no mail balloting, and other counties had up to 76.2% of voting by mail ballot.

L300: Voters throughout Minnesota may vote before election day as absentees without excuse, in person or by mail. Voting absentee in person at a county or city location is sometimes called "early voting." About 839 thousand voters completed their absentee ballots in person before Election Day. In the map image below, counties with darker shades had a higher percentage of votes cast via early voting. County percentages range from zero to 34.9%.
