        var prompt = "I'm Looking For...";
        var $s = $(".search.bar .searchField");
        $s.focus(clearPrompt).blur(putPrompt).change(trimfield);
        function clearPrompt() { if ($s.val() == prompt) $s.val(""); }
        function putPrompt() { if ($.trim($s.val()) == "") $s.val(prompt); }
        function trimfield() {
        var searchval = $.trim($('#searchField').val());
        searchval = searchval.replace(/\s{2,}/g,' ');
        $('#searchField').val(searchval);
        }
        putPrompt();
        });

*New in 2026 will be Early Voting*Early Voting will be available during hours of operation beginning Friday, October 16th through Monday, November 2nd.Wilkin County residents can now vote in person at the Wilkin County Auditor/Treasurer's office ahead of election day.Similar to Absentee Voting, you will fill out an Early Voting certificate, be issued a ballot, fill out your ballot and place in a tabulatorIf you are not registered, you will be required to fill out and Election Day Registration form, provide proof of residency and complete an Early Voting CertificateThis is open to all voters whether you have a polling location or are in a Mail Ballot precinctHours of operation are Monday-Friday 8:00am-4:30pm*We will be open normal business hours on Columbus Day, October12th.ON ELECTION DAY, November 3rd, 2026Breckenridge Precincts 1, 2 and 3 as well as the Wilkin County Auditor's office acting as the absentee location will be open 7:00am-8:00pmAkron Township polling location will be open from 10:00am-8:00pmAbsentee Ballots must be returned by 5:00pm on election day, while mail ballots precincts will have until 8:00pm to return in-personAdditional hours are: Saturday, October 24th, 2026 from 9:00am-3:00pmTuesday, October 27th, 2026 from 8:00am-5:00pmSaturday, October 31st, 2026 from 9:00am-3:00pmSunday, November 1st, 2026 from 9:00am-3:00pmMonday, November 2nd, 2026, from 8:00am-5:00pm*If you live in a Mail Ballot precinct, please bring in the ballot that was mailed to you*If you lost your ballot or did not receive a ballot, please contact our office*If you live in a Mail Ballot Precinct, and plan to come on election day. Please be sure your Signature Envelope is filled out completely*If dropping off ballots for others, please bring your ID as you will need it and then be required to sign the agent delivery log*Agents may drop off ballots for up to three voters*Early Voting via tabulator ends the day before the election on November 2nd. On election day, November 3rd, you will still be voting via envelopesAny questions, please contact our office at 218-643-7165
