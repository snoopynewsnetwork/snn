The Pine County Auditor's office administers most elections in Pine County (some elections are administered by local governments and schools as well) in conjunction with the Secretary of State. If you are unsure where your polling place is, please use the Polling Place Finder.If you would like to vote absentee or early, there are several options to do so which are described on this webpage.   To track your absentee or mail ballot, you can use the Ballot Tracking tool.  You may also use this ballot tracking tool if you live in a mail ballot district to track your ballot.  If you need to register to vote, you may visit the Secretary of State's website or or you may print and complete this application.  You may fax your voter registration application or an absentee ballot application to 320-591-1671, or you may email Elections or you may mail them to our office:  Pine County Auditor's Office, Elections, 635 Northridge Dr NW,  Suite 240, Pine City, MN  55063
If you would like to find out what is on your ballot for the upcoming election, you may do so here for the MyBallot Online Tool.If you have questions regarding elections in Pine County, please call the Pine County Auditor-Treasurer's Office at 320-591-1670, toll free 1-800-450-7463 ext. 1670 or you may email at elections@pinecountymn.gov.
