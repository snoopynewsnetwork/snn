2026 State General Election Results
2026 State Primary Election Results
The Todd County Auditor-Treasurer’s Office is providing this communication for transparency of the results reporting from the August 11, 2026 State Primary Election event.
 During the processing of the results files, our team inadvertently uploaded an incorrect results file which was identified when balancing final voter counts to the paper reports.  The issue was identified, reviewed by staff and submission of the revised results file was posted at 12:17 AM.  Due to this file error, there was a brief time where vote totals were not displayed accurately on the state website.
 The Todd County Canvassing Board will meet on August 13, 2026 to review the unofficial results prior to final certification of the official results.
