Primary Election- August 11, 2026
General Election- November 3, 2026

Voter Registration
Online Registration
Register to Vote-Minnesota Secretary of StateTo register online you will need to have an email address and a Minnesota-issued driver's license, Minnesota ID card or the last 4 digits of your social security number. 
In-Person Registration
If you reside in Red Lake County and would like to register to vote, download and complete the Voter Registration Form and mail it back to:Red Lake County AuditorPO Box 367Red Lake Falls, MN 56750

Mail Ballot Precincts
Mail balloting is authorized by resolution of the governing body and remains in effect for subsequent state and county elections until it is revoked. Sixteen precincts in Red Lake County vote by mail ballots for all State Primary and General Elections (mail balloting does not apply to stand-alone school district elections):

Browns Creek Township
Emardville Township
Equality Township
Garnes Township
Gervais Township
Lake Pleasant Township
Lambert Township
Lousiville Township
City of Oklee
City of Plummer
Red Lake Falls Township-North
Red Lake Falls Township-Southeast
Red Lake Falls Township-Southwest
River Township
Terrebonne Township
Wylie Township

If you live in one of those mail ballot precincts, your traditional polling place will not be open on Election Day. Instead, if you are registered to vote at your current address, you will receive your ballot in the mail approximately one month prior to each state election.
Absentee and Early Voting Information
Absentee voting for each election begins 46 days prior to the election day, and early voting begins 18 days before each election. All absentee and early voting occurs at the Auditor's Office in the Courthouse located at 124 Langevin Ave N in Red Lake Falls. The Auditor's Office is located on the second floor.
General Election
Absentee voting for the November 3rd General Election begins Friday, September 18th and continues until Monday, November 2nd. The voting hours are from 9 am until 5 pm Monday through Friday. Additional voting hours occur on:

Saturday, October 24th- 9 am to 3 pm
Tuesday, October 27th- Until 7 pm
Saturday, October 31st- 9 am to 3 pm
Sunday, November 1st- 9 am to 3 pm

Early voting (voters are able to deposit their ballot directly into a tabulator) begins on October 16th and continues until 5 pm on Monday, November 2nd with the same hours as absentee voting.
