2026 Minnesota Caucasus Finder  click hereVoter Registration: Voters must register if they are new to voting or have changed their name and/or address.  To save time at the polling place, we encourage unregistered voters to register more than 20 days before Election Day.  You can register online here (link to https://mnvotes.sos.mn.gov/) or at the Auditor’s Office at the Cook County Courthouse.  Please note that pre-registration ends 20 days before Election Day, but you can still register after the 20th day with proof of current residency.
Mail Voting: All Cook County residents who reside outside of the city limits of Grand Marais vote by mail.  If you are a registered voter residing outside of the city limits of Grand Marais, you will automatically receive your ballot in the mail around 3-4 weeks before each election.  You do not have to request your ballot.  If you do not receive a ballot, make sure you are a registered voter and/or that your address is current and correct.  Check your status online here (link to https://mnvotes.sos.mn.gov) or contact the Cook County Auditor’s Office at 218-387-3640.
Absentee Voting:  If you are temporarily away and need your ballot sent to a temporary address, you can apply for an Absentee ballot.  Application must be in writing.  You can apply online here  (link to https://www.sos.state.mn.us/elections-voting/other-ways-to-vote/vote-early-by-mail/) or contact the Cook County Auditor’s Office for a paper application.
ElectionsThe Cook County Auditor’s Office provides voter registration and election administration for federal, state, and county elections. Duties include:

Maintenance of voter registration records
Candidate filings
Ballot preparation
Absentee Voting
Election Judge Training
Election Results reporting
Campaign finance reporting
Providing election-related information to the public
