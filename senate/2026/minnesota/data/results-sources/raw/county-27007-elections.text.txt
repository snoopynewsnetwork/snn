                Frequently Asked Questions

            
        
        


                        
                        
                        
                                I am going to be out of town on Election Day; can I still vote?
                            
                        
                        
                            Yes, you can vote by mail or vote in person.
                        
                        
                        
                                I am not registered; can I still vote on Election Day?
                            
                        
                        
                            Yes, Minnesota offers same day registration. Be sure to bring a photo ID and proof of residence to your polling place.
                        
                        
                        
                                I am a college student; how do I vote?
                            
                        
                        
                            You must first register at an address; for many students that is their school residential address or their parent's house. You can vote in person on Election Day or vote by mail. 
                        
            




                    
        
    
      

        
                    

        
        
            

                Election Responsibilities

                    
                        This office administers federal, state, and county elections, as well as assists in city, township, and school district elections. Responsibilities include:

Process voter registration applications
Program ballots and test voting equipment
Issue absentee ballots
Train and certify election officials
Compile and report election results

    $(document).ready(function(){
        let lastCtrlTime = 0;
        // milli seconds
        const doublePressWindow = 500;
        document.addEventListener('keyup', function (event){
            if(event.key === "Control"){
                const now = Date.now();
                if(now - lastCtrlTime <= doublePressWindow){
                    let metaPageId = document.querySelector("meta[name='pageID']")
                        .getAttribute("content");
                    // reset time
                    lastCtrlTime = 0;
                    var backofficeEditUrl = window.location.protocol + '//' + window.location.hostname + '/umbraco/#/content/content/edit/' + metaPageId;
                    window.location.href = backofficeEditUrl;
                }
                else{
                    lastCtrlTime = now;
                }
            }
        });
    });
