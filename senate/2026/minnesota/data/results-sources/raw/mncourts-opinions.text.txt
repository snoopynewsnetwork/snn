                Overview
                
        IMPORTANT NOTICESElectronic Notification ProceduresAppellate courts transmit all notices, orders, opinions, and case-related correspondence electronically. To ensure timely receipt of court communications, all attorneys with pending cases must maintain a registered email address with the court.Unrepresented parties are also encouraged to participate in the e-notification system by registering their email address. For detailed registration instructions, please visit the Clerk of Appellate Courts page.Formatting UpdateThe Minnesota Supreme Court has updated the formatting of its published legal opinions and orders. These changes—including standardized alignment and optimized spacing—are part of our commitment to digital accessibility and ensuring our public documents are fully accessible to all individuals.PREVIOUSLY PUBLISHED OPINIONSPlease visit the Minnesota State Law Library's Appellate Opinions Archive for previously published Supreme Court Opinions.
