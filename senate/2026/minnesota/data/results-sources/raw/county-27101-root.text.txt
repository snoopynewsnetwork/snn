			  Murray County
			  Established in 1857 and formally organized in 1872, Murray County is located in southwest Minnesota. The low rolling hills, prairies and lakes include wildlife management and waterfowl production areas. The nine county parks and one state park include miles of trails for walking, biking and birding. This agricultural community includes 9 cities, 20 townships and has a population of just under 8,100.
