//		append "active" to className of all ancestor <LI> tags until the parent of the <LI>
//		tag is not <UL> or the parent of the <UL> is not another <LI> tag.
var functionRef = function setClassActive()
{
	var e;
	try
	{
		var pattern = new RegExp("http[s]?://.*?/(.*)[?#]?","");
		var tags = document.getElementsByTagName("A");
		if (tags)
		{
			for (var i=0;i<tags.length;i++)
			{
				var el = tags[i];
				var resultsTag = el.href.match(pattern);
				var resultsPage = location.href.match(pattern);
				if (!resultsTag || !resultsPage) continue;
				if (resultsTag[1] != resultsPage[1]) continue;
	
				// go up li tree marking this and all parent class=active
				var li = el.parentNode;
				while (li && li.tagName == 'LI')
				{
					if (li.className != '') 
						li.className += ' ';
					li.className += 'active';
					var ul = li.parentNode;
					if (!ul || ul.tagName != 'UL') break;
					li = ul.parentNode;
				}
				//let's catch all links e.g. topnav, leftnav, quick links, etc
				//break;	//found url match, quit checking tags
			}
		}
	}
	catch (e) {}
}
//----- Activate topnavActive once page loads
if (typeof addEventListener != 'undefined') addEventListener('load', functionRef, false);	//standards browser
else if (typeof attachEvent != 'undefined') attachEvent("onload", functionRef);				//IE early versions

Absentee Voting Locations 
All residents of Roseau County may vote in-person at
Roseau County Courthouse, 606 5th Ave. SW Room 160, Roseau, MN 56751. Hours are 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM, Monday through Friday 
ADDITIONAL HOURS FOR VOTING: 

Saturday, August 8, 2026 9:00 am to 3:00 pmMonday, August 10, 2026 8:00 am to 5:00 pmSaturday, October 24, 2026 9:00 am to 3:00 pmTuesday, October 27, 2026 8:00 am to 7:00 pmSaturday, October 31, 2026 9:00 am to 3:00 pmSunday, November 1, 2026 9:00 am to 3:00 pmMonday, November 2, 2026 8:00 am to 5:00 pm

 
Voters can apply for an absentee ballot online at mnvotes.org or contact the Auditors Office and an application will be mailed or faxed to you.
Return the completed application as soon as possible to:
Roseau County Courthouse
606 5th Ave SW Room 160
Roseau, MN 56751
Upon completed application ballot will be mailed with instructions on how to complete process and return to the Roseau County Election Office. 
Voters will have the option to track their ballot online at https://mnvotes.sos.state.mn.us/AbsenteeBallotStatus.aspx.
If you have any questions, please contact the Roseau County Auditors Office at 218-463-1282, or email elections@co.roseau.mn.us  
Mail Ballot Cities/Townships
All townships, and cities having fewer than 400 registered voters on June 1 of an election year, may apply to the county auditor for permission to conduct balloting by mail.
Mail balloting is authorized by resolution of the governing body and remains in effect for subsequent state and county elections until it is revoked.
Ballots will be sent to all registered voters in mail ballot precincts no more than 46 days and no later than 14 days before a regularly scheduled election for federal, state, and county elections.
A postage-paid return mailing envelope, a ballot secrecy envelope and instructions for marking and returning ballots will be included with each ballot.
Check to see if you are registered to vote by visiting the Secretary of State website.

Voter Registration
Online Registration Is Now Available
To register online you will need to have an email address and a Minnesota-issued driver's license, Minnesota ID card or last 4 digits of Social Security Number. Visit the Secretary of State website for more information for online registration.
In-Person Registration
If you reside in Roseau County and would like to register to vote, download and complete the Voter Registration Form and mail it back to:
Roseau County Courthouse
606 5th Ave SW Room 160
Roseau, MN 56751
In Minnesota, a person must register to vote before you can vote. There are two options to registering to vote:

You may register up to 20 days before Election Day
On Election Day at your polling place

Your name will appear on the polling place roster if you choose to register to vote at least 20 days before Election Day. Your registration will remain current until you move, change your name or do not vote for four consecutive years.
Voter Eligibility
If you want to vote, you must certify:

You are 18 years old on Election Day
You are a citizen of the United States
You will have resided in Minnesota for 20 days immediately preceding Election Day
You are not currently incarcerated for a felony conviction
You are not under court-ordered guardianship, where a court has revoked your voting rights
You have not been ruled legally incompetent by a court of law

Election Day Registration
If you are not registered or you need to update your registration on Election Day, you may do so at your polling place. Your polling place is based on the location of your residence. Find your polling place. You are required to provide proof of residence when registering on Election Day.
You must have one of the following with your current name and address in the precinct to verify your residence:

A Valid Minnesota driver's license, learner's permit, Minnesota ID card, or a white receipt for any of these
A Valid student ID card including your photo, if you are on a student housing list on file at the polling place
A Tribal ID card that contains your picture and signature
A Valid registration in the same precinct under a different name or address
A Notice of late registration sent to you by your county auditor
A Voter registered in the same precinct as you who can confirm your address with a signed oath
An Employee of a residential facility, including nursing homes, battered women's shelters, homeless shelter and other licensed facilities, may vouch that you live there (only if the facility has provided the county with a certified list of employees)

Or, you may provide both a photo ID from the list below, and one of the current bills listed below with your current name and address in the precinct.
Photo IDs (May be Expired)
ID must contain the voter's name and photo, and may be expired.

Driver's License, state ID card or learner's permit issued by any state
United State Passport
United Stats Military ID card
Tribal ID card with the name, signature and photo of the voter
Minnesota university, college, or technical college ID card
Minnesota high school ID card

Approved Documents
The document must have the voter's current name and address in the precinct, and can be shown electronically or on paper.

Residential lease or rental agreement (must be valid through Election Day)
Current student fee statement
Bill, account or start of service statement due or dated within 30 days of election for:

Phone (landline, cell, VOIP, etc.)
Television (cable, satellite, etc.)
Internet services
Solid waste or sewer services
Electric, gas or water
Banking or credit card
Rent or mortgage payments



For more information on registering to vote visit the Secretary of State website.
