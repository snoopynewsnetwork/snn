# 2026 Senate races by likelihood of a Democratic win

[All 2026 Senate races](README.md)

**Assessment date: 2026-10-17. General election: 2026-11-03.**

For Republican-held seats, the current ordering is **North Carolina → Alaska ≈
Ohio → Maine ≈ Texas → Iowa → Kansas → South Carolina → Florida ≈ Mississippi**.
The middle of that list is close enough that a one-place difference carries
little weight.

This is a dated research snapshot of the assessment, not a published probability
model or a continuously updated forecast. It combines recent polling, candidate
strength, statewide Cook Partisan Voting Index (CPVI), and the ratings from
[Cook Political Report](https://www.cookpolitical.com/ratings/senate-race-ratings?os=v)
(2026-10-06), [Inside Elections](https://insideelections.com/ratings/senate/)
(2026-10-15), and
[Sabato's Crystal Ball](https://sabatoscrystalball.substack.com/p/five-senate-rating-changes-as-close)
(2026-10-14). Source pages may change after this snapshot.

PVI measures presidential partisanship relative to the country; it is not a
projected Senate margin. Statewide figures use the
[2025 Cook PVI compilation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cook_Partisan_Voting_Index#By_state).
“Democratic win” means electing a Democratic senator. Independent challengers
are listed separately, without assuming how they would caucus.

## Judgments behind the ordering

- **North Carolina has separated from the other pickup opportunities.** Cooper's
  advantage appears across pollsters. ECU's 2026-09-30 through 2026-10-03 survey
  had him ahead 50–43.
  [ECU poll](https://surveyresearch-ecu.reportablenews.com/pr/ecu-poll-cooper-s-lead-holds-at-7-points)
- **Alaska and Ohio are nearly interchangeable.** Sabato gives Alaska a
  Democratic lean; Brown has the stronger polling average, including a newly
  released BGSU/YouGov survey showing 48–42. Alaska's ranked-choice ballot adds
  uncertainty.
  [Sabato](https://sabatoscrystalball.substack.com/p/five-senate-rating-changes-as-close),
  [BGSU](https://www.bgsu.edu/arts-and-sciences/democracy-and-public-policy-research-network/bgsu-poll.html),
  [Cook's Alaska assessment](https://www.cookpolitical.com/senate/race/488531)
- **Maine's favorable PVI does not make it an easy flip.** Collins retains
  crossover appeal, and Jackson's polling advantage is narrow. Texas has a
  similarly small Democratic lead against a much redder baseline; Marist's
  2026-10-08 through 2026-10-11 survey put Talarico ahead just 47–45.
  [Cook on Maine](https://www.cookpolitical.com/senate/race/488601),
  [Marist](https://maristpoll.marist.edu/polls/2026-midterm-elections-in-texas-october-2026/)
- **Minnesota deserves more attention than its usual reputation suggests.**
  Sabato downgraded it to Lean D while upgrading New Hampshire to Likely D on
  2026-10-14. That supports placing New Hampshire above Minnesota despite
  Minnesota's slightly bluer PVI.
  [Sabato's analysis](https://sabatoscrystalball.substack.com/p/five-senate-rating-changes-as-close)

## Senate control

Democrats need a net gain of four seats. Holding their seats and winning North
Carolina would leave them needing three more pickups. Several of those
opportunities have only small polling leads, so a shared polling error could
move the Senate outcome substantially.
[Inside Elections](https://insideelections.com/ratings/senate/)
