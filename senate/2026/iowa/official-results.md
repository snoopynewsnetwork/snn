**Counting stages and exceptions, source-scoped research.** Current 2026 Code§50.45 declares the greatest-vote recipient elected; this is a plurality contest, with tie handling under§50.44. Regular general polls are 7 am–8 pm under§49.73, also stated in the September official November notice and current county notices. Local November 3 closing is 8 pm Central Standard Time (UTC−06:00); the retained local Washington page explicitly uses CST. Date/file/fetch clocks are not votes-as-of clocks.
