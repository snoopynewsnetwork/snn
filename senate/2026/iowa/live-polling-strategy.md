# Iowa election-night collection strategy

Observed **2026-10-18 UTC / 2026-10-17 Central**. This is a collection/scenario proposal, not an implemented live collector or calibrated Needle. Read the [official inventory](official-results.md), [turnout geography](turnout.md), [historical targets](county-partisanship.md), [news survey](local-news-results.md) and [root plan](../../../ELECTION_NIGHT_PLAN.md).

Collect all **99 counties**. The accepted county research priority contains 41 counties,1,332,107 / 1,663,506 presidential candidate/generic-write-in marks. The first 40 reach 1,322,507; Henry’s 9,600 crosses 80%. Top 15 contribute 991,869. Every county remains in statewide arithmetic. Generic Write-in marks are not established named-valid write-ins; under/over are excluded from this volume denominator. [Coordinator reconciliation](data/coordinator-baseline-reconciliation.json) checks original all-99-county controls and full order; native-unit/2026 geographic compatibility remains separate. Retain county FIPS plus native precinct, central absentee and method IDs; combined or administrative units may not have polygons. A 2024 presidential comparison is distinct from Iowa's most recent Senate/midterm contest; no 2024 Senate election is presumed. Compare equivalent geography, voting method and boundary vintage rather than whole-county margins against a selective first batch.

Use the verified state-linked election-night channel and county auditor publications documented in the inventory. [Cass County's current official page](https://www.casscountyia.gov/county-government/elections/) visibly separates local result/canvass documents from SOS election-night links by election date. That is an observed authority-chain example, not proof that every county uses the same vendor or that a November API exists. Pin actual November Senate links and candidate IDs from official publications, reject old primaries and test scaffolds, and preserve unofficial/canvassed/certified status.

These are proposed request intervals, not measured or guaranteed publication cadence. Use conditional requests where supported, jitter, per-host concurrency limits, timeouts and backoff. Track last successful fetch separately from last changed votes. A stable count can be a healthy source. Reject challenge HTML, app shells, empty/test data and wrong elections even under HTTP 200.

Archive immutable changed response bytes before parsing, with URL, UTC retrieval, HTTP metadata, hash, publisher timestamp/timezone where known, election/contest/candidate/unit/method IDs, parser version and status. Default to a coherent official state county record; use a county auditor view when it demonstrably supersedes or explains that record. Keep one observation per unit/time. Replace cumulative snapshots, then derive compatible batch differences; retain decreases and candidate/precinct corrections. Reconcile candidate totals, method sums and county/state views at compatible release versions. Never add state and county totals that overlap, or combine multiple publishers' cumulative counts.

The [September 2026 official general-election notice](https://sos.iowa.gov/news-resources/secretary-pate-encourages-iowans-make-plan-vote-2026-general-election) states an 8 p.m. November 3 absentee return/poll-closing time. Verify updated instructions, timezone, exceptions and any extensions before defining outstanding ballots. Receipt, eligibility review, tabulation and publication are different stages. A closed/reported precinct is not proof that all final valid votes are included. UOCAVA, Safe at Home, provisional/cure and correction rules require their own verified treatment; do not infer that no votes remain from a general deadline notice.

Keep counted votes fixed. Estimate only the remaining ballots with low/base/high ranges and evidence source/time. Use method-compatible baseline shares where possible; if remaining units or geographic crosswalks are unknown, use a coarser supported comparison and widen uncertainty. Do not infer candidate preference from registration or allocate central absentee ballots to precinct polygons by population. For a conditional two-candidate scenario with counted D-minus-R lead `L` and positive expected remaining major-party votes `U`, `(1-L/U)/2` is the D share needed to tie. Show already-secured/impossible thresholds and turnout sensitivity; it is not a win probability. Keep other candidates separately modeled when relevant.

The [news survey](local-news-results.md) records historical primary/AP/NPR channels, test-display settings, empty or future configurations and blocked NYT access. A newsroom substitute for a missing county needs source/time, coverage, reason and a rule for returning to the reconciled official observation. Shared suppliers do not provide independent confirmations. Keep outside calls, estimated completion and forecasts separate from counted candidate votes. Original reporting about delayed batches or county board activity can inform remaining-vote ranges when clearly attributed as estimates.

The retained NPR/AP June Senate files illustrate a completion mismatch even with exact candidate arithmetic. The source's vote-completion estimates remain below 100%, and its displayed candidate universe omits candidates below 1%. Preserve these separate fields and June count timestamps; an October page metadata date does not make those counts current. Never invent the missing unit or turn the reporting gap into a specific remaining-ballot total. These are primary format observations, not November source approval.

Before polls close, verify the November ballot and contest configuration, source schemas/status, native units, boundary versions, poll-closing notices and manual failovers. Manual entries retain source bytes/screenshot, exact units/candidates/values, publisher and observation times and operator. Rehearse genuine saved cumulative releases to prove duplicate handling, negative corrections, overlapping sources, missing counties, renamed/combined units, stale-source display and outages. The coverage board shows all counties, last fetch/change, counted votes, available method/precinct detail, unmatched units and remaining uncertainty. Publish probabilities only after genuine historical replay, held-out evaluation and calibration required by the root plan; synthetic report ordering cannot establish calibration.
