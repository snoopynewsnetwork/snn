## Comparing observations

Maintain one row per source/election/contest/geography/candidate/time, including the original vote count, publication timestamp when available, retrieval time, status and upstream supplier. Compare statewide and county totals only at compatible times and geographic coverage. AP embeds at WBTV and WITN are the same supplier and destinations. WUNC/NPR race calls may also be AP-derived. Agreement among those products is not three independent count confirmations.

A missing official county can temporarily use a clearly attributed news observation, with its source and substitution time visible. Never add county and statewide views together; never add two news sites' cumulative totals, or average disagreements. Return to the official observation when reconciled. Preserve corrections, including decreases, as a new snapshot. Escalate differences after checking vintage, voting methods, candidate IDs and incomplete coverage.

The source date matters more than the URL containing “2026”: WWAY's embedded election and WECT's selected contest are March primaries. WBTV/WITN test-display settings and WLOS's empty playlist must not enter the count. An HTTP 200 or newly transformed page is not evidence of fresh candidate votes. The regional coverage identified here is useful for preflight and manual reporting, but **no inspected product supplies a verified complete November Senate feed today**.

Before November: verify each newsroom's actual election selection, current ballot names, county coverage, upstream attribution and access; preserve a zero/test distinction; observe a small number of genuine changes to measure lag. For NYT, preserve counts separately from estimated completion, external calls and any Needle forecast. The [polling strategy](live-polling-strategy.md) specifies proposed checks and fallback operations.
