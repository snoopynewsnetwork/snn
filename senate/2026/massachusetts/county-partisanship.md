# Massachusetts historical partisanship and conditional paths

Independent research observed October 19 Central / October 20, 2026 UTC. **The 2024 Warren–Deaton baseline already favors Warren by 676,228 votes: 59.923783% of their named pair, a 19.847566-point pair margin.** Holding every native pair volume fixed, a uniform **−9.923783 percentage-point A-share shift ties**; **−9.423783 share points yields an illustrative A+1 pair margin**, or **34,071.08 net votes**. Positive improvement needed from that historical baseline is zero. These conditional arithmetic paths do not predict Markey’s 2026 performance. Counts come from the Secretary’s [actual 2024 Senate publication](https://electionstats.state.ma.us/elections/view/165304/), its linked [native CSV](https://electionstats.state.ma.us/elections/download/165304/precincts_include:1/) and [municipal CSV](https://electionstats.state.ma.us/elections/download/165304/precincts_include:0/).

The complete [native baseline CSV](data/county-baselines.csv) contains 9,351 contest/native records across four distinct electorates; the compatibility filename `county-` does not mean counties. [Municipal baselines](data/baselines/municipal-baselines.csv) contain all 351 parents per contest. Every unit, including the 924 native contexts outside the primary priority set, participates in each corresponding statewide calculation. [Schema](data/baselines/source/SCHEMA.md), [reproduction](data/baselines/REPRODUCE.md), [validation](data/baselines/validation.json) and [asset manifest](data/baselines/asset-manifest.json) make scopes and gaps explicit.

## Statewide and every-unit pair targets

For source unit i, declared pair volume `T_i`, anchor A share `s_i`, `T=sum(T_i)` and `M=sum(T_i*(2*s_i-1))`, the fixed-weight unbounded shift is `(target_margin*T-M)/(2*T)`. For a tie this is `(B-A)/(2*(A+B))`; A+1 **pair margin** adds **0.5 share points**, not one share point. One A-share point produces two margin points and `0.02*T_i` net votes. A transfer of one vote B→A produces two net votes. All display rounding follows computation.

The calculator explicitly clips `s_i+delta` into [0,1] and solves the resulting monotone bounded sum. Raw unbounded shifts and shares remain visible. These actual tie/+1 rows need no clipping; the saved synthetic bounded case exercises it. Already-winning, no-positive-pool, impossible and secured selected-unit cases remain explicit. A fixed 1% pair margin corresponds to a different percentage of all reported votes, and fractional scenario net votes are illustrative, not integer winning totals.

## Offline conditional live remainder calculator

[calculate.py](data/baselines/calculate.py) accepts complete supplied candidate identities, counted votes per source-unit key, an explicit known remaining **candidate-vote** pool or explicit unknown status, and remaining allocations across every candidate. It never revises counted A/B/other votes. Unknown stays unknown. It reports **final denominator = all counted candidate votes + all remaining candidate votes**, separately from the final A+B denominator. Ballots containing blanks/invalids would require a separately supplied candidate-participation assumption; they cannot be passed as candidate votes without that conversion.

For counted pair lead L, counted pair total C, and remaining pair total U, an all-unit target m requires `(m*(C+U)-L+U)/(2*U)` of remaining pair votes for A. With selected-unit pool R, subtract the assumed remaining pair net in every other unit and use R in the variable term. The target uses the **final** pair denominator C+U. Other candidates’ remaining totals stay as explicitly allocated. The tool separately computes the strict A-share threshold needed to exceed each supplied rival; an A/B target alone can fail that full field. Ties remain unresolved, no-pool has no division, and raw/clipped/secured/impossible requirements stay visible. These are arithmetic scenarios, **not calibrated probabilities**, actual returns, a collector or a Needle.

Run, fully offline:

```sh
python3 senate/2026/massachusetts/data/baselines/calculate.py live senate/2026/massachusetts/data/baselines/synthetic/known-final-denominator.input.json
python3 senate/2026/massachusetts/data/baselines/calculate.py historical senate/2026/massachusetts/data/baselines/scenarios/2024-senate-own-native-input.json.gz
```

[Eleven saved synthetic cases and validation](data/baselines/synthetic/validation.json) exercise unknown, zero and negative pools, secured/impossible shares, clipping, already-winning baselines, fixed-other selected-unit assumptions, and A+1 pair that loses to C. Sixty-five additional named arithmetic/input checks reject nonfinite/null/text/bool/negative votes and weights, out-of-range targets/preferences, missing/empty/duplicate/nonstring global source keys, incomplete candidate fields, bad allocations and duplicate/nonfinite JSON. Global key checks occur even without a selected unit or positive pool. Overflow produces a safe error. These tests do not estimate reporting-order error or candidate preference.
