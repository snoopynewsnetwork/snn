L202: ## 2018
L203: 
L204: In 2018, Massachusetts voters were able to vote early in a midterm election without needing an excuse for first time. Early voting, which began in 2016, was available over a 12-day period for the November 6, 2018 State Election.
L205: 
L206: Early voting was not available for the September 4, 2018 State Primary. Aside from a small number of absentee ballots, which could only be cast by voters who qualified with a specific excuse, all State Primary ballots were cast on Election Day.

L217: ## 2016
L218: 
L219: Beginning with the November 8, 2016 State Election, Massachusetts voters were able to cast their ballots before Election Day, without needing an excuse, for the first time. Over a 12-day early voting period, over 1 million ballots were cast across the Commonwealth.
L220: Early voting was not available for the September 8, 2016 State Primary. Aside from a small number of absentee ballots, which could only be cast by voters who qualified with a specific excuse, all State Primary ballots were cast on Election Day.
L221:

L225: Early – 30.7%
L226: Election Day – 64.8%
