L191: Municipal Aggregation Plan (Updated 9/10/26)
L192: 
L193: For Windsor participants in Electricity Aggregation:
L194: 
L195: As of August 1st, Eversource has posted the new rate for residential electricity: $0.15934/ kWh. This is an increase that puts Eversource's rate higher than the aggregation rate, which is $0.14292/ kWh.
L196: The Green Committee is recommending that everyone who opted out of aggregation over the last period, (when Eversource's rates were the lower of the two), consider opting back in. Anyone who had opted out of aggregation at any point is welcome to join in for lower rates. If you never opted out... you are automatically in!
L197:
