                    Hudson Precinct Changes

Every ten years, the legislature and local governments must redraw boundaries that
take into consideration population, communities of interest, and state and federal
constitutional requirements, amongst other factors. Population is determined by the
most recent Federal Census. Hudson’s population per the 2020 Federal Census is
20,092 which represents an increase of 1,029 from the 2010 population of 19,063 (or
.5%)

                     Part One: Local Re-Precincting

How will the results of the 2020 Federal Census impact the voters in Hudson?

In Hudson, there are 6 precincts for elections. This is a decrease from 7 precincts.
Some polling locations have added an additional precinct to their polling location.
Removing Precinct 4 from Joseph L. Mulready School and Precinct 5 from the
Forest Avenue School and relocating Precinct 4 to David J. Quinn Middle School
and Precinct 5 to Hudson High School.
