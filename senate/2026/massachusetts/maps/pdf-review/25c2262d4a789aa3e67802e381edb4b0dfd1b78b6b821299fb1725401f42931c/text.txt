TOWN ELECTION WARRANT

And you are further required to notify and warn the inhabitants of the Town of Hampden
qualified to vote in elections of Town Officers to meet at the Hampden Town House, 625
Main Street, Hampden, on Monday, May 19, 2025, AD at seven o’ clock in the forenoon, then
and there to give in their votes on one ballot to the election officers of said Town for the
following officers, to wit:

To choose for the term of one year the following:
Town Moderator (1)

To choose for the term of three years the following:
Selectboard (1)
Town Clerk (1)
Collector of Taxes (1)
Town Treasurer (1)
Board of Assessors (1)
Cemetery Commissioner (1)
Parks and Recreation Commissioner (1)
Hampden Free Library Trustee (1)
Planning Board (1)
Constable (3)

To choose for the term of five years the following:
Planning Board (1)
Hampden Housing Authority (1)


To choose all other necessary Town Officers.
