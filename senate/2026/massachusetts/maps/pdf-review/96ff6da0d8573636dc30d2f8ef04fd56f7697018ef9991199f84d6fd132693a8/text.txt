Guidelines Used to Create Adopted Precincts
•  Use of Block Groups within each Ward to create precincts.
•  Keep precincts at 2000 Voters with a margin of +/- 10%.
•  Use of linear features such as roadways, railroads, waterways
   and/or other easily identifiable features as allowed, to use as
   precinct boundary lines.
•  Use of projected residential growth was taken into consideration.
