               TOWN OF EASTON
    ENVIRONMENTAL STEWARDSHIP COMMITTEE CHARGE


Mission:     The Environmental Stewardship Committee’s (formerly Green Communities
             Committee) mission is to promote environmental health and sustainability
              through awareness, education, and initiatives that will empower community
           members to be change agents for a greener, more environmentally friendly
              Easton.

Membership: Seven (7) persons to be appointed by the Select Board. The committee shall be
             comprised of one (1) Select Board representative serving for a one-year term, one
               (1) student representative (high school or college) serving for a one-year term,
             and five (5) at large members who are residents of Easton serving for staggered
               three-year terms.

Goals and
Initiatives:
                1. Work with the Town of Easton departments and committees to support
                 environmental goals and amplify the Town’s messaging to the public.

                2.  Foster opportunities and partnerships with community members, businesses,
                and other approved entities that result in synergistic efforts to protect natural
                  resources and the health and quality of life in Easton, MA.

                3.  Support programs that help the town in various ways:
                       •  Conserve and protect our water sources
                       •  Reduce and manage waste in ways that are positive to the
                          environment
                       •  Promote alternative energy use to align with Massachusetts’ Clean
                         Energy and Climate Plan goals
                       •  Minimize and reduce the use of harmful and dangerous pesticides
                             or practices that hurt vital ecosystems
                       •   Participate in discussions about climate change and how to address
                               in town
                       •  Educate the public about environmental issues and sustainability
                               practices
                4. Meet monthly to discuss outreach, initiatives, and news that affect the Town
                  of Easton. Present important findings to key officials.

                5.  Explore all ways to get messages to the public through social media, local
                  cable television, the town website, town newsletter, printed flyers, and an
                  online directory of services. Also, appear at appropriate public-facing events
                  or meetings when possible.
