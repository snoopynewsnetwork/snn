                     AGENDA


        •  ORDERED: That the City Solicitor prepare a Home Rule Petition
        requesting authorization of the Massachusetts Legislature to amend the
         City Charter to provide for Ranked-choice voting (RCV) in local elections.
              If the draft Home Rule Petition is approved by the City Council and
        subsequently approved by the State Legislature, Salem voters would then
        decide whether to adopt RCV through a ballot measure.
