               Groton Stewardship Committee Minutes

                Hersch

                 Absent: Lisa Murray, Chair

Location: Virtual Meeting (Zoom)

6:32 PM K. Kuliesis (Vice Chair) called the meeting to order


Discussion on Site Walk at Fitch-Best Woods on October 10th, 2024

C. Steeves said that both she and A. Eliot were present for the site walk along with several abutters
on Longley Road. She said she put together a monitoring report from the site walk. She said that the
parcel is mostly clear of encroachments. She said there was a problem with some invasive plants
along Longley Road, mostly Oriental Bittersweet and Barberry. She said that most of the property
had been surveyed by an abutter (along the Best property line). She said there were no trails on the
property.

Discussion ensued regarding the site walk and future management endeavors.

O. Lathrop said that he would invite the Invasive Species Committee out to the property to take a
look at some of the invasive plants.

A Eliot left the meeting at 6:55 PM, meaning that there was no longer a quorum.

Committee Updates

R. Hersch said that the boards on Lost Lake have been pulled out to begin the drawdown period.

O. Lathrop said that the Invasive Species Committee has been conducting follow-up treatments at
several properties. He said that some large invasive stumps had been cut earlier in the Spring at the
St Regis Papermill Conservation Area off of Fitchs Bridge Road. He said that the Committee has
treated some of the cut stumps. He said that the Committee has seen some impressive results due to
their treatment efforts.
