         NOTICE TO WESTBOROUGH VOTERS

         CHANGE OF POLLING LOCATION

              ALL PRECINCTS - 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, AND 6



Pursuant to M.G.L. Chapter 54 Section 24, by vote of the Select Board on
September 8, 2026, the polling location for all voters in the Town of Westborough
has changed to:


          Westborough Community Center
            1500 Union Street  – 1st Floor



This change in polling location is effective immediately beginning with the General
Election on November 3, 2026. This change is for election day voting only. Early
voting will remain at Town Hall in Memorial Hall, 34 West Main Street.

This notice will be mailed to every household in Westborough containing one more
registered voter(s). The notice should arrive in mailboxes by October 5, 2026.

Questions regarding the change of polling location can be directed to the Town
Clerk’s Office at 508-366-3020 or townclerk@westboroughma.gov.


Respectfully, Deborah E. Ledoux, Town Clerk
