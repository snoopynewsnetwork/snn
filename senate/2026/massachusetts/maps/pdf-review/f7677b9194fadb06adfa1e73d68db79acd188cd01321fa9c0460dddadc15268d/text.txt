CITY OF BOSTON
  PRECINCTS
          (2022)

The precincts displayed in these maps were developed based on
the 2020 U.S. Census blocks and revised by the City of Boston
Election Commission to conform to the voting precinct guidelines.
These precincts were adopted in 2022 by the Board of Election
Commission and by the State Legislature.

Sources: U.S. Census, voter Registration database, and Massachusetts Secretary of
the Commonwealth.

This map is intended for planning and visualization purposes only. Map prepared
by the BPDA Office of digital Cartography and GIS. 2022
