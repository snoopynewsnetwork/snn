                        CERTIFICATE OF ASCERTAINMENT
        In accordance with sections 7-19-70 and 7-19-120 of the South Carolina Code of Laws,
as amended, the Secretary of State has received the certified determination of the Board of State
Canvassers regarding the results of the General Election held on November 5, 2024, and the
Secretary of State has certified to the undersigned the names of the persons elected to the office
of elector for President and Vice President of the United States as stated in the certified
determination, who shall be deemed appointed as electors. Based on the information provided
by the Secretary of State regarding the results of the aforementioned General Election, by virtue
of the authority vested in me as Governor of the State of South Carolina and pursuant to the
Constitution and Laws of this State and of these United States and the powers conferred upon me
therein, I, Henry Dargan McMaster, hereby certify the appointment of the electors for President
and Vice President and set forth herein below the names of the electors and the canvass or other
ascertainment under the laws of this State of the number of votes given or cast for each person
for whose appointment any and all votes were given or cast:

REPUBLICAN PARTY
Presidential Candidate:          Donald J. Trump                   Total Votes:    1,483,747
Vice Presidential Candidate:     J.D. Vance                        Total Votes:    1,483,747

      Electors:    Susan Aiken; J.D. Chaplin; Cindy Costa; Moye Graham; Tyson Grinstead;
Xiaodan Li; Drew McKissick; Alexia Newman; William L. Oden.

DEMOCRATIC PARTY
Presidential Candidate:          Kamala D. Harris                  Total Votes:    1,028,452
Vice Presidential Candidate:     Tim Walz                          Total Votes:    1,028,452

      Electors :   Helen Bennett; Laurie Slade Funderburk; Maggie W. Glover; James H.
Hodges; Walt McLeod; Lessie B. Price; Richard W. Riley; Margaret Sumpter; Sue Yearwood.

LIBERTARIAN PARTY
Presidential Candidate:          Chase Oliver                      Total Votes:    12,669
Vice Presidential Candidate:     Mike ter Maat                     Total Votes:    12,669

        Electors:     Melissa Couture; Casey Crowe; Jessica Ethridge; Steve Hoffman; Michael
Lee, Jr.; Kendal Ludden; Hunter Savirino; Charles E. Whitener, Jr.; Kathryn D. Whitener.
