## Native precision, completeness and topology

The primary source CRS is **ArcGIS103146 / EPSG:6570, NAD83(2011) / South Carolina (ft), international feet (0.3048m)**. Areas are square international feet; source `Shape__Area` and length fields remain unchanged alongside separately calculated diagnostics. No geographic-coordinate area substitution is made. All29 primary pages request `outFields=*`, native geometry, `returnZ=true`, `returnM=true`, `returnTrueCurves=true`, with no output CRS, quantization, geometryPrecision or allowable-offset simplification. The layer's native hasZ/hasM flags are bothfalse, every returned position is2D and all2,315 returned geometries use rings; no returned curveRings were linearized or discarded. This is the complete exposed native service geometry, not a claim to have acquired an unpublished source geodatabase.

Both pre/post count queries return2,315. Complete all-ID sets match before, after and across all acquired features, with no duplicate/missing OIDs. Explicit batches are at most80 IDs, below source maxRecordCount2,000; per-page requested/returned ID sets match, no error/true transfer-limit flag or response transform is present. [Native audit](maps/native-audit.json.gz) retains complete sets, every page's flags/CRS, full attributes and source JSON pointers. Each feature has original uncompressed UTF8 byte start/end offsets and an exact byte-slice hash. Priority subsets copy those feature slices unchanged, preserving numeric lexemes, every coordinate/part and field value; subset order and origin locators are documented. Statewide originals are retained once as their full raw response pages. Subsets are gzip Esri JSON, not rounded GeoJSON exports.

GEOS3.14.1 / Shapely2.2.0 audit interprets native clockwise shells/counterclockwise holes without repair, reversal, snapping, buffering or allocation. **No missing/empty geometry;2 invalid source features remain:** Allendale Woods,OBJECTID1960, ring self-intersection at `[1870710.23031496,421858.81463255]`; Laurens Trinity Ridge,OBJECTID2121, at `[1684030.38418636,973023.479330704]`. All priority geometries are individually valid under this test, which does not establish a clean precinct partition.

All-state spatial-index intersection testing found **791 positive-area feature pairs:349 within a source county and442 across counties**;18 candidate pairs involving invalid geometry were explicitly skipped. Every positive measured pair is retained with native square-foot area, without sliver thresholds or deletion. The largest is Dorchester OID1546 /Charleston OID1680:9,327,739.597652372 square international feet. Pairwise overlap areas are diagnostics, not a union-area total or evidence to reassign precincts/counties. Closed rings, ring lengths, dimensions, finite coordinates, winding, duplicate consecutive positions, source areas and GEOS reasons are recorded per feature. No trusted same-vintage legal outline was acquired for certifying inter-precinct gaps; lack of a gap finding is not a gap-free claim.

Gzip entities are lossless single-member storage; validation decompresses every member and checks CRC/trailer plus original/stored hashes and byte lengths. No ZIP/geodatabase archive was acquired or substituted, so ZIP-member/CRC auditing is inapplicable. PDF pages and byte hashes remain actual originals. All stored artifacts are strictly below40,000,000 bytes; no dependency trees or caches are part of this package.
