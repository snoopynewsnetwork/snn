# 2026 U.S. Senate races

[Repository home](../../README.md)

**General election: November 3, 2026.** Coverage includes **35 races**:
33 regular Class II elections for terms ending January 3, 2033, and two special
Class III elections (Florida and Ohio) for the remainder of terms ending
January 3, 2029. Scope and seat-holder information checked **2026-10-17**.

## Sources and scope

- [FEC 2026 congressional election calendar](https://www.fec.gov/documents/5910/2026pdates.pdf): states with Senate elections and the general election date. The calendar is dated May 18, 2026; check state offices for later schedule changes.
- [U.S. Senate Class II roster](https://www.senate.gov/senators/Class_II.htm): the 33 seats up for regular election and their current holders.
- [U.S. Senate Class III roster](https://www.senate.gov/senators/Class_III.htm): the class and current holders of the Florida and Ohio special-election seats.
- [Florida offices up for election](https://dos.fl.gov/elections/candidates-committees/offices-up-for-election): confirms the Senate seat is on the 2026 ballot.
- [Ohio special-election directive](https://www.ohiosos.gov/assets/directive-2025-54-writ-of-special-election-for-united-states-senate-election.pdf): confirms the November 3 special election and January 3, 2029 term end.
- [FEC directory of state election offices](https://www.fec.gov/introduction-campaign-finance/how-to-research-public-records/state-election-offices/): source for the state-office research links in each tracker.

The seat holder is background information, not a claim that the senator is running.
Confirm candidates and their ballot status with the state election office; FEC
registration and financial filings alone do not establish ballot access. Include
independent, minor-party, and qualified write-in candidates when researching the
ballot. Record primary outcomes and withdrawals rather than deleting candidates.

For polls, record field dates, sponsor, sample size, voter population, method, and
margin of error alongside toplines. For finance, record the reporting period.
For results, distinguish a media projection, an unofficial count, and state
certification. Link a source for each added fact and date every update.

Each race starts with verified seat details and research links. Candidate, polling,
finance, news, and results sections are awaiting research. Updates happen through
commits and pull requests; there is no automatic feed.
