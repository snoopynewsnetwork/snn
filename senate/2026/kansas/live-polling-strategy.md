A selected county target subtracts assumed net contributions elsewhere before solving its share. Show secured/impossible thresholds outside 0–100%. Retain third-candidate counts and verify the actual legal outcome rule. Uniform historical share shifts and conditional remaining-vote targets are deterministic scenarios, not probabilities.
