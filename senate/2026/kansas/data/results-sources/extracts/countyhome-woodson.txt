***The Treasurer’s Office is aware of the rising taxes in Woodson County. We are looking for ways to cut expenses so we do not have to raise our budget and contribute to higher taxes. Postage is a major expense in the Treasurer’s Office. Effective May 1, 2026, The Treasurer’s Office will no longer mail tax receipts. We are happy to email receipts to anyone requesting them. Please include an email address with all transactions you would like a receipt for.

County Partners
RevitalizeYC
RevitalizeYC was founded in April of 2018 as a nonprofit 501(c)3 focused on the revitalization efforts of Yates Center, KS and Woodson County. Our mission is to facilitate, promote, and support the revitalization of Yates Center and Woodson County by fostering economic development, preserving community heritage, and enhancing civic pride.
You can visit RevitalizeYC’s website at
https://www.revitalizeyc.com/
Woodson County Chamber of Commerce
