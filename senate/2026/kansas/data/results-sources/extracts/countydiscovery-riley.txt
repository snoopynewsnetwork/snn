How to Use Voting Machines in Riley County, KS
Prepare to visit the polls by watching this brief video describing the process to use voting machines.
New DS300 Machine Tabulates Votes - Riley County, KS Elections 2023
The DS300 machine is a new piece of equipment for the 2023 elections in Riley County, KS. Voters will make their selections then print the ballot and insert the paper into the DS300 to be counted. This process is a little different than previous years, and we want to make sure voters know what to expect when they visit the polls.
