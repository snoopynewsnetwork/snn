Welcome to Mitchell County, Kansas
! We're found in the Solomon Valley, home to Waconda Lake and Glen Elder State Park, located in North Central Kansas. Supported by Solomon Valley Economic Development, Beloit, KS, Mitchell County is the hub of living activity and successful businesses along U.S Highway 24 in the region. ​We look forward to meeting you.
