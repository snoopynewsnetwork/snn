Discover Russell County, Kansas
Rooted in a proud history of agriculture, rail, and energy, Russell County is a place built on hard work, resilience, and community pride. That same spirit continues today through growing communities, local businesses, and a strong commitment to creating opportunity for the future.
Russell County offers the best of both growth and quality of life. Businesses benefit from a strategic location, supportive partnerships, helpful resources, and room to grow. At the same time, visitors are drawn to historic downtowns, outdoor recreation, local events, unique attractions, and the genuine hospitality found throughout our communities.
