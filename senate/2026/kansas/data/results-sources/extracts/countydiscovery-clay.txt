Primary Election- August 4th 2026
Voting is 7am-7pm August 4th, 2026
Notice of Ballot Audit
Kansas law required that 1% of ballots cast on or before election day are to be audited after election day and prior to the meeting of the County Board of Canvassers. For Clay County elections, we will audit no less than two races and two precincts for every primary and general elections.
The ballot audit for the August 4th, 2026, Primary Election will begin on Friday August 7th, 2026, at 10:00am in the Commissioner's Room at the Clay County Courthouse
Advance Ballot
To apply for an advance ballot to be mailed to you, call 785-632-2552 to have an application sent to you, or complete the
Advance Ballot Application
(PDF) and submit to the County Clerk’s Office. A signed application must be received by the Clerk’s Office before a ballot can be mailed out.
