Notice of Election Audit, 2026 General Election
10/8/2026
Pursuant to K.S.A. 25-3009, notice is hereby given that an election audit shall be held at 9:00 a.m. on Friday, November 6th, 2026 in the County Commission Room of the Labette County Courthouse, 501 Merchant, Oswego, Kansas.
Precinct selection for conducting the audit of the General Election will be held at the County Clerk’s Office, 501 Merchant, Oswego on Thursday, November 5
th
at 8:00 a.m.
There will be a pre-election test on October 9th, 2026 at 4:00 p.m.
There will be a post-election test on November 19th, 2026 at 8:00 a.m.
