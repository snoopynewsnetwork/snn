Responsibilities
Julie A. Dunkle serves as the County Clerk and Election Officer. Katlyn Farrell Serves as Deputy Election Officer for Jefferson County. This page is intended to assist you in finding election information and to provide useful resources for you.

2026 NOTICE OF PRIMARY ELECTION
When is the next election?
November 3, 2026 - General Election
Where Do I Vote?
Here
Version Options Elections Headline
is a list of polling places for Jefferson County.  Your polling place is open from 7:00 a.m. - 7:00 p.m. on Election Day.  You should expect to wait in line if you vote during busy times such as 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m.  If you are unsure which polling location is yours, you can visit
Vote KS Website
to check or call the Clerk's Office.
How can I get an advance ballot by mail?
If you can't vote in person on Election Day, you can request an advance/absentee ballot.  Ballots can be mailed starting 20 days before the election.  Click
here
Version Options Elections Headline
to access a form, fill out the application form, sign it and return to the County Clerk's office.
If you need to cancel your advance ballot by mail application, fill out the form below and return it to the Clerk's Office.
Advance Ballot By Mail-Cancellation Form
