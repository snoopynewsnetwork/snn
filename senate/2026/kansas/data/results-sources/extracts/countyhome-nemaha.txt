NEMAHA COUNTY, KANSAS
Our Mission
The mission of Nemaha County Government is: To provide high-quality services with honesty, integrity and transparency while maintaining the highest level of public trust. We strive to protect and serve our citizens in a fiscally responsible manner while enhancing the health, safety, and well-being of our community.
The Nemaha County Commissioners will meet every Monday at 9:00 a.m. in the Nemaha County Courthouse in the Commissioner's Meeting room except for Monday's that fall on holidays observed by the county.
