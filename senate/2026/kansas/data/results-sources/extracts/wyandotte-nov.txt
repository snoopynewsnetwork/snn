Important Dates
June 1, 2026 (12:00 p.m.)
- Candidate filing deadline for the Primary/General Election. Candidates must file with the Kansas Secretary of States Office. (Precinct committee candidates file with theElection Office).
October 13, 2026
– Last day to register to vote.
October 14, 2026
– Advance by-mail ballots begin mailing.
October 17, 2026
– Advance in-person voting begins.
November 2, 2026 (12:00 p.m.)
– Advance in-person voting ends at 12:00 Noon (Election Office Only).
November 3, 2026
–
General Election Day
. Polls are open from
7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.
November 5, 2026 (10:00 a.m. until completed)
– Selection of contests and precincts for the post-election audit.
November 6, 2026 (10:00 a.m. until completed)
– Post-election audit.
November 12, 2026 (10:00 a.m. until completed)
– Canvass of the 2026 General Election.
November 13, 2026
– Post-election public test of voting equipment.
November 13, 2026 (5:00 p.m.)
– Deadline to request a recount

Vote on Election Day
On November 3, 2026 you must visit your assigned Polling Place to vote on election day. Polls are open from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. You can find your assigned Polling Place by using the
