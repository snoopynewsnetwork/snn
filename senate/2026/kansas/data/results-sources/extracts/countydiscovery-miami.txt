Early Voting Available - 2026 General Election
Monday, October 19, 2026
Early Voting is available for Miami County electors at the Miami County Administration building prior to election day.
Advance voting is available in person at the Miami County Administration Building begins Thursday, October 15, 2026.
Regular Advance Voting Hours
Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
October 15–30, 2026
Extended Evening Hours
Thursday, October 22 — 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
Tuesday, October 27 — 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
Saturday Advance Voting
Saturday, October 31 — 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 noon
Final Day of Advance Voting
Monday, November 2 —
8:00 a.m. to 12:00 noon
Photo ID is required to vote. Please bring an acceptable form of photo identification.
Voting on Election Day?
Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026. Voters will vote at their assigned Election Day polling location.
Click on this link here,
Kansas VoterView
to verify your voter registration information and confirm your assigned polling location.
For additional election information, visit the Miami County Clerk & Elections by clicking on this link
2026 General Election webpage
or contact the Miami County Clerk & Elections Office at 913-294-3976.
