Welcome To the Halfway Point Between Eastern & Western America!
In 1939, Kansas state’s own Edwards County became famous when its road sign made the cover of The Saturday Evening Post and was nicknamed “Midway USA”. From the date of its publication, the location was recognized as the halfway point that stood at an equal distance from the East Coast state of New York and the West Coast state of San Francisco.

Our Mission
The mission of Edwards County is to provide quality public services in a cost-effective manner through innovation, leadership, and the cooperation of dedicated people.
Our Values
Edwards County is known as Midway USA. Located halfway between New York and San Francisco, we are home to 2,800 residents and consist of 3 cities. Kinsley is home to the county seat with around 1,350 residents and has 3 major highways that run through it. Lewis is located in the eastern part of the county and is the second largest city with approximately 420 residents. The farthest eastern city is Belpre with around 200 people. On the Western side of the county at the edge of the county line is Offerle with 100 people.
Edwards County has rich history and citizens that are proud of their heritage and enjoy the pleasures and rewards of a small-town atmosphere.
