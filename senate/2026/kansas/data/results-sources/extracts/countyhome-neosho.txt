Neosho County Landfill Cleanup Week
Neosho County Landfill will be hosting a free clean up week beginning Tuesday, October 13, 2026, and ending Saturday, October 17, 2026. Daily hours of operation are 8 am to 4 pm, Tuesday through Saturday
during
Clean Up Week
Only
The following stipulations apply:
Open to Neosho County Residents Only.
No businesses or commercial disposal.
Tires will be accepted at half the regular rate.
Municipal Solid Waste (MSW) will be full price.
Click on the Road and Bridge/Landfill link and then click on details link.
Starting January 1, 2026, all Neosho County Commission meetings will begin at 1:00 pm.
