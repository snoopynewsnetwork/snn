The Edwards County Election Department is responsible for overseeing voter registration and election operations to ensure fair and accurate elections. As the central voter registration site for the county, the department maintains the official voter registration database, ensuring that records are up to date and compliant with state and federal election laws.
Key responsibilities include:
✔
Maintaining voter registration records
and processing new registrations, updates, and cancellations.
✔
Generating voter registration lists and reports
for election officials and public use.
✔
Election preparation
, including organizing ballots, managing polling locations, and ensuring a secure and efficient voting process.
The Election Department is dedicated to serving the citizens of Edwards County by providing accessible and transparent election services.
Voter Registration
Central voter registration site for Edwards County
Works with outreach voter registration sites
Voter Registration Database Maintenance
Maintains voter registration database
Voter Registration Lists & Reports
Generates voter registration lists and reports
NVRA-mandated confirmation mailings (required for purging voter registration files)
NVRA-mandated Notice of Disposition mailings
NVRA-mandated Notice of Disposition/Rejection mailings
Election Preparation
Accepts filings for public office
Prepares newspaper publications for elections
Reviews election law
Prepares certification of board workers for party chairs and individual board workers
Solicits election board workers, polling site setup workers, and advanced voting board workers
Prepares ballot proofs for ballot printing and scanner programming
Orders all ballot stock and voting supplies
Election Day & Results
Scans all ballots on election night
Prepares results and provisional ballots for the County Board of Canvassers (convening the Friday following each election)
Certifies all election results to the public, media, and Secretary of State
Prepares “Certificate of Nomination” and “Certificate of Election” forms
Polling Locations
Procures polling precinct sites

Requirements for Voter Registration
Citizen of the United States
Kansas resident
Be 18 years of age before the next statewide election
If convicted of a felony, have had civil liberties restored
Has abandoned former residence and/or name
