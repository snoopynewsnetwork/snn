how_to_vote
Early Voting begins Monday, October 19th at the Shawnee County Election Office during the following hours:
8:00 am - 7:00 pm Monday-Friday, October 19-23 and October 26-30
8:00 am - Noon on Monday, November 2nd
The Election Office is located at the corner of SW Van Buren and SW Croix Streets. The entrance to the parking lot is from SW Croix Street, 400 feet west of S Kansas Avenue.
ballot
Advance Mail Ballots have been mailed via the USPS. Mail Ballots may be returned by mail, in person to our staffed drop box on the north side of the Election Office during the hours listed above. On Election Day Mail Ballots may be dropped off at any Poll Place in the county or at the Election Office. All Mail Ballots delivered in person must be turned in no later than 7:00 pm on Election Day.
Under Kansas statute, the deadline to return your ballot is the close of the polls on Election Day. A district court judge in Douglas County has entered a temporary injunction altering that deadline. However, the Secretary of State, represented by the Attorney General, has filed a motion with the Kansas Appellate Courts to stay the decision so that the deadline remains at the close of polls on Election Day. Because of the legal uncertainty while the matter is pending on appeal, voters are urged to return their ballots by the close of the polls on Election Day. Doing so will ensure that their ballots will be counted.
exclamation
Due to road construction on Topeka Blvd, access to the Election Office from the west is currently unavailable. Instead take S Kansas Avenue to SW Croix Street and turn west. Drive 400 feet to the Election Office parking lot entrance on the north side of the street.
