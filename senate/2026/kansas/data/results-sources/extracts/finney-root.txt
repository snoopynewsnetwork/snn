FAQs
Am I eligible to vote?
Voters in Kansas must be:
Registered to vote
Note - registration deadline is twenty-one (21) days prior to any election
Eighteen (18) years of age or older on or before Election day
Note - new voters can register at seventeen (17) years of age
A citizen of the United States
A resident of Kansas

Preparing for the November 3, 2026 General Election
While the Finney County Election Office is busy preparing ballots, certifying candidates, testing voting equipment, training poll workers, and updating voter registration records, voters can take several important steps now to prepare for the upcoming Primary Election on August 4, 2026.

Under Kansas statute, the deadline to return your ballot is the close of the polls on Election Day.  A district court in Douglas County entered a statewide injunction requiring ballots postmarked by Election Day to be counted if a ballot arrives by the last postal delivery on Friday after the election.  The Kansas Court of Appeals rejected the Secretary of State’s appeal to stay the injunction.
Regardless of the decision, voters are urged to return their ballots by the close of polls on Election Day.  Doing so will ensure that their ballots will be counted.

Local Question Submitted - .25% Sales Tax Renewal
Shall the following be adopted?
Shall Finney County, Kansas, be authorized to impose a one-quarter percent (0.25%) countywide retailers’ sales tax (the “Sales Tax”) upon the expiration of the existing one-quarter percent (0.25%) countywide retailers’ sales tax, and pledge the revenues from the Sales Tax to paying for and/or financing needed existing building and road maintenance to include the purchase of necessary equipment to complete the needs as determined necessary by the Board of County Commissioners; with the collection of the Sales Tax to commence on October 1, 2027, or as soon thereafter as permitted by law, and shall terminate ten years after its commencement; all pursuant to the provisions of K.S.A. 12 187 et seq., as amended?  The apportionment formula provided in K.S.A. 12 192, and amendments thereto, will apply to the revenues received from the Sales Tax.

Kansas Constitutional Amendment - Citizenship Voting Requirement
Precise ballot question text will be provided once the Kansas Secretary of State certifies it in mid-September.
Explanatory statement:
This amendment would clarify that only a person who is a citizen of the United States is eligible to vote in this state.
A vote for this proposition would clarify that only a person who is a citizen of the United States is eligible to vote in this state.
A vote against this proposition would make no change to the constitution of the state of Kansas, and the language concerning voter qualifications would remain the same.
Shall the following be adopted?
“§ 1. Qualifications of electors. Every(a) No person shall be deemed a qualified elector unless such person:
(1) Is a citizen of the United States who;
(2) has attained the age of eighteen years; and who
(3) except as provided in subsection (b) or (c), resides in the voting area in which he or she such person seeks to vote shall be deemed a qualified elector.
(b) Laws of this state relating to voting for presidential electors and candidates for the office of president and vice-president of the United States shall comply with the laws of the United States relating thereto. A citizen of the United States, who is otherwise qualified to vote in Kansas for presidential electors and candidates for the offices of president and vice-president of the United States may vote for such officers either in person or by absentee ballot notwithstanding the fact that such person may have become a nonresident of this state if his or her such person’s removal from this state occurs during a period in accordance with federal law next preceding such election.
(c) A person who is otherwise a qualified elector may vote in the voting area of his or her such person’s former residence either in person or by absentee ballot notwithstanding the fact that such person may have become a nonresident of such voting area during a period prescribed by law next preceding the election at which he or she such person seeks to vote, if his such person’s new residence is in another voting area in the state of Kansas.”
Kansas Constitution

June 1, 2026, 12:00 p.m. (noon)
June 1, 2026 at noon is a key deadline for the 2026 election cycle in Kansas.
Party Change Deadline:
In even-numbered years, the deadline to change your political party affiliation for participation in the primary election coincides with the candidate filing deadline. To update your party affiliation, complete a voter registration application online or submit a form in person at the Finney County Election Office by noon on June 1.
Candidate Filing Deadline:
Individuals who wish to have their name placed on the 2026 ballot must file the required paperwork and submit the applicable filing fee at the Finney County Election Office no later than 12:00 p.m. (noon) on June 1.
Candidate Withdrawal Deadline:
Candidates who have previously filed but decide not to seek office must complete and submit official withdrawal paperwork in person at the Finney County Election Office by noon on June 1.
These deadlines are statutory and cannot be extended. If you have questions about filing requirements, party affiliation, or voter registration, contact the Election Office for assistance before the deadline.
