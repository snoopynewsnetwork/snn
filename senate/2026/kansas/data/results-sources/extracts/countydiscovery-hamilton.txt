County Clerk/Election Officer
County Clerk/Election Officer
Services
Carries out all elections held in Hamilton County.
Serves as county Freedom of Information Officer.
Secretary to the Board of County Commissioners.
Assists and Monitors all county budgets.
Compiles and prepares County Tax Levies.
Coordinates and pays all county expenditures.
Coordinates payroll and benefits for all county employees.
Provides assistance to county residents in preparing Refund Application Homestead Property Taxes.
Kansas Wildlife and Parks licensing agent.
