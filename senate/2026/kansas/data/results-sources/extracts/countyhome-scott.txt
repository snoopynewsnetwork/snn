Welcome to Scott County
Scott County
is home to approximately 5,000 Kansans, and stretches across well over 700 square miles of Kansas prairie.
Scott County
was organized on July 5, 1886, and named in honor of Major General Winfield Scott, United States Army, hero of the Mexican War.
