** MAIL BALLOT NEWS**
Under Kansas statute, the deadline to return your ballot is the close of the polls on Election Day. A district court in Douglas County entered a statewide injunction requiring ballots postmarked by election day to be counted if a ballot arrives by the last postal delivery on Friday after the election. The Kansas Court of Appeals rejected the Secretary of State’s appeal to stay the injunction.
Regardless of the decision, voters are urged to return their ballots by the close of polls on Election Day. Doing so will ensure that their ballots will be counted.

AFFIDAVIT FOR WRITE-IN CANDIDATES
Due to recent law changes, a person who wishes to run as a write-in candidate for national, state, county or 1st class city office, must file an affidavit of write-in candidacy in order to have those votes tabulated. The affidavit for national and state candidates must be filed with the secretary of state. The affidavit for county and 1st class city candidates must be filed with the county election officer. The deadline to file the write-in affidavits is the fourth Monday prior to Election Day.
Write-in candidates for township office, precinct office, school board, and city board (smaller than a 1st class city) are not required to file an affidavit.
The law that covers these changes is K.S.A. 25-305.
