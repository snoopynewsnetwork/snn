Visit Voter View to see your current party, precinct, polling location, district, and voting history. Need to change your information? Fill out a voter registration form and return to the Barber County Clerk's Office. Pay attention to the important election dates.
Important Dates:
October 9th - Public test at 9am
October 13th - Last day to register to vote
October 14th - First day in office voting and advance ballots mailed
October 27th - Last day to mail advance ballots out
November 2nd - Advance voting in office ends at noon
November 3rd - Election day
November 6th - Audit at 2 pm
November 10th - Canvass at 10am
​November 17th - Post public test at 9am
Voting dates and hours
October 14th - October 30th- Monday - Friday: 8:30-4:30
November 2nd - 8:30 to Noon
