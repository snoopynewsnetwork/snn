DEADLINE TO RETURN ADVANCE VOTING BY MAIL BALLOTS
Under Kansas statute, the deadline to return your ballot is the close of the polls on Election Day. A district court judge in Douglas County has entered a temporary injunction altering that deadline. However, the Secretary of State, represented by the Attorney General, has filed a motion with the Kansas Appellate Courts to stay the decision so that the deadline remains the close of the polls on Election Day. Because of the legal uncertainty while the matter is pending on appeal, voters are urged to return their ballots by the close of the polls on Election Day. Doing so will ensure that their ballots will be counted.

REQUEST AN ADVANCED VOTING BALLOT
Voters must submit an application to vote by mail. Follow the steps below to request an advanced voting ballot.
1.
Download the Advance Ballot by Mail Application by clicking this link.
Download the Permanent Advance Ballot by Mail Application by clicking this link.
2.   Fill out the application with the following information:
Date the election will be held
Your name
Address where you are registered
Address to Mail Ballot (if different from residential address)
Date of birth
Your Kansas Driver’s License or Kansas nondrivers ID Number (or submit copy of other ID specified on the form)
Your signature

RETURN YOUR ADVANCE BY MAIL BALLOT
After your ballot arrives in the mail, please complete it carefully by following the provided instructions.
Under Kansas statute, the deadline to return your ballot is the close of the polls on Election Day. A district court in Douglas County entered a statewide injunction requiring ballots postmarked by election day to be counted if a ballot arrives by the last postal delivery on Friday after the election. The Kansas Court of Appeals rejected the Secretary of State’s appeal to stay the injunction.
Regardless of the decision, voters are urged to return their ballots by the close of polls on Election Day. Doing so will ensure that their ballots will be counted.
