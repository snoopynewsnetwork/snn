Advance Ballot by Mail
:
Click here to download and/or print a copy of the Application for Advance Ballot by Mail.
This is a
one-time application
, meaning you must fill out TWO copies if you would like a ballot mailed to your address for both the Primary Election and the General Election. Please label on the application which election it is for. If you do not have access to a printer, call the Geary County Clerk’s office at (785) 238-3912 and we can mail you an application.
__________________________________________________________________
Kansas elections are conducted by the counties with oversight by the Secretary of State’s Office. Voter registration application forms must be submitted to the county election officer where the applicant lives. Kansans may also register to vote or change their registration information online with a valid Kansas driver’s license or non-driver identification card. The deadline to register to vote is the twenty-first day before any election.
Voter Registration Requirements
Requirements for registering to vote and for voting were impacted by the Secure and Fair Elections law (

Vote In Advance
Anyone may vote in advance - you no longer need a reason to vote early in Kansas! In Geary County, we begin mailing advance voting ballots 20 days prior to Election Day.
Vote in advance in person at the County Clerk's Office before Election Day,
this ends at noon, 12pm, the Monday before Election Day
.
Vote in advance by mail, fill out an
Application for Advance Ballot by Mail
.
For more information about a
permanent
need for an Advance Vote by mail, visit the
Application for Permanent Advance Voting Status
.
Please see the
Election Calendar
for deadlines regarding voting in advance.
Federal services voting
Overseas voting, or
Federal Services Voting
, is available for military personnel and their dependents as well as civilians and their dependents living abroad. A voter must meet the
criteria
established by the federal Uniformed and Overseas Citizens Absentee Voting Act.
Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot
Registered voters in Kansas who do not qualify under the federal overseas voting rules may apply for an
advance ballot
.
