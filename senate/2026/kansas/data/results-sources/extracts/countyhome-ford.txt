RELEASE
The recent ICE operation in the Dodge City area has concluded.
Ford County remains engaged with local, state, and federal partners.
Residents can expect continued communication from Ford County as information becomes available.
Ford County has been informed that the recent U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) operation in the Dodge City area has concluded and that federal personnel are expected to leave the area.
While the operation may be ending, Ford County remains committed to supporting the safety, well-being, and informed engagement of our residents. County officials have been in communication with local, state, and federal partners and will continue those conversations as we evaluate the impact of recent events on our community.
Ford County recognizes that many residents have questions regarding the recent operation and the information available at the local level. The County remains committed to working with local, state, and federal partners to encourage clear communication, coordination, and the timely sharing of information when activities may affect residents or county services. Maintaining those partnerships and providing accurate information to the public remain important priorities as we continue serving the community.

Posted 10/7/26 at 9:01 AM
Ford County and the City of Dodge City understand that the events of the past two days have created fear, concern, uncertainty, and questions throughout our community.
We apologize for such a late response, but this situation developed quickly and continues to evolve without any notice to local law enforcement. The current immigration enforcement activity is being conducted by federal authorities without local law enforcement involvement. Please know that we see you, we hear your concerns, and we want to clarify that these operations are not initiated by us. If you see local law enforcement on scene, they have been dispatched by an emergency call.
We recognize that residents may have questions about what is occurring. While local law enforcement does not have operational control over federal enforcement activity, the Ford County Sheriff’s Office and Dodge City Police Department will continue working with each other to provide accurate information when it is available and to keep our community safe.
Our local officials have been in contact with our Federal and State legislators to share concerns, seek additional information regarding the current situation, and request assistance in keeping our communities informed.
Together we are dedicated to serving our communities with compassion and respect for all.
____________________
El Condado de Ford y la Ciudad de Dodge City comprenden que los acontecimientos de los últimos dos días han generado temor, preocupación, incertidumbre y preguntas en toda nuestra comunidad.
Ofrecemos una disculpa por responder hasta este momento, pero esta situación se desarrolló rápidamente y continúa evolucionando sin previo aviso a las autoridades locales. Las actividades actuales de control migratorio están siendo realizadas por autoridades federales sin la participación de las agencias locales de seguridad pública. Queremos que sepan que vemos lo que está ocurriendo, escuchamos sus inquietudes y deseamos aclarar que estas operaciones no son iniciadas por nosotros. Si observa a autoridades locales en el lugar, es porque fueron enviadas en respuesta a una llamada de emergencia.
Reconocemos que los residentes pueden tener preguntas sobre lo que está ocurriendo. Aunque las autoridades locales no tienen control operativo sobre las actividades federales de control migratorio, la Oficina del Sheriff del Condado de Ford y el Departamento de Policía de Dodge City continuarán trabajando en conjunto para proporcionar información precisa cuando esté disponible y mantener segura a nuestra comunidad.
Nuestros funcionarios locales han estado en contacto con nuestros legisladores federales y estatales para expresar sus inquietudes, solicitar información adicional sobre la situación actual y pedir apoyo para mantener informadas a nuestras comunidades.
Juntos, estamos comprometidos a servir a nuestras comunidades con compasión y respeto para todos.
Updated 9/23/26 at 8:09 pm
Notice
Ballots for the November 3, 2026 General Election will be mailed out October 14, 2026. Please be aware of the following when returning your mail ballot.
Under Kansas statute, the deadline to return your ballot is the close of the polls on Election Day. A district court in Douglas County entered a statewide injunction requiring ballots postmarked by election day to be counted if a ballot arrives by the last postal delivery on Friday after the election. The Kansas Court of Appeals rejected the Secretary of State’s appeal to stay the injunction.
Regardless of the decision, voters are urged to return their ballots by the close of polls on Election Day. Doing so will ensure that their ballots will be counted.
Aviso
Las boletas para las elecciones generales del 3 de noviembre de 2026 se enviarán por correo el 14 de octubre de 2026. Por favor, tenga en cuenta lo siguiente al devolver su boleta por correo:
Según la legislación de Kansas, la fecha límite para devolver la boleta es el cierre de las urnas el día de la elección. Un tribunal de distrito del condado de Douglas emitió una orden judicial de alcance estatal que exige contabilizar las boletas con matasellos del día de la elección, siempre que lleguen a más tardar en la última entrega postal del viernes posterior a la elección. El Tribunal de Apelaciones de Kansas rechazó la apelación del Secretario de Estado para suspender dicha orden judicial.
Independientemente de la decisión, se insta a los votantes a devolver sus boletas antes del cierre de las urnas el día de la elección. Hacerlo garantizará que sus votos sean contabilizados.

Updated 12:24 AM 7/24/2026
Ford County has used an opt-in alerting system for several years (CodeRed). We are transitioning to a new system (Smart 911). Even if you registered to receive alerts via CodeRed you will need to register with Smart 911 to receive alerts through this new system. Because it is something you must register for we cannot automatically transfer your data. If you wish to receive emergency alerts related to weather or impending or ongoing emergencies you are now able to do so using our new system at the following link. CodeRed will eventually be discontinued as an alerting service in Ford County.
https://www.smart911.com/smart911/ref/reg.action?pa=fordcountyks
______________
El Condado de Ford ha utilizado un sistema de alertas opcional durante varios años (CodeRed). Estamos migrando a un nuevo sistema (Smart 911). Incluso si se registró para recibir alertas a través de CodeRed, deberá registrarse en Smart 911 para recibir alertas a través de este nuevo sistema. Dado que es un requisito de registro, no podemos transferir sus datos automáticamente. Si desea recibir alertas de emergencia relacionadas con el clima o emergencias inminentes o en curso, ahora puede hacerlo a través de nuestro nuevo sistema en el siguiente enlace. CodeRed dejará de ofrecerse como servicio de alerta en el Condado de Ford.
https://www.smart911.com/smart911/ref/reg.action?pa=fordcountyks
