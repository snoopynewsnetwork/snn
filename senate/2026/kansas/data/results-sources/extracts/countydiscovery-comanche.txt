Public Notice
The Comanche County Election Office wishes to inform all registered voters that the Avilla and Powell polling locations will be permanently closed effective immediately. Beginning with the upcoming election, all voters previously assigned to the Avilla and Powell polling locations will vote at the following location: Coldwater Veterans Building. This change will take effect starting with the next election and will remain in place for all future elections.
The voters effected by this change will receive a formal letter in the mail. Voters with questions regarding their polling place assignment may contact Bri Uhl at 620-582-2361.
Bri Uhl, Comanche County Election Officer
WRITE-IN CANDIDATES:
SB221
Any candidate wishing to run as a write-in candidate for all national, state, county or 1st class city offices must file an affidavit of write-in candidacy. The affidavit must be filed no later than the 4th Monday prior to the General Election. Write-in candidates are not permitted for primary elections [except precinct committee].
