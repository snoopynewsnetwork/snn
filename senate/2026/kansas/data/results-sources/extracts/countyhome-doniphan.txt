About Doniphan County
Doniphan County, Kansas, located in the extreme northeastern corner of the state, is bordered on three sides by the Missouri River. Some of its first towns and cities were founded and
incorporated around 1854, nearly seven years before Kansas was admitted into
the union as a free state.
Doniphan County’s notable history and unique landscape truly make it unlike anywhere else in Kansas.
