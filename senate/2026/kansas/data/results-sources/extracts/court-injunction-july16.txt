      A.     Plaintiffs challenge a recent law eliminating Kansas’s prior
             three-day grace period for accepting and counting mail ballots.

      1.     At issue in this case is SB 4, enacted by the Legislature last year and

codified at K.S.A. 25-1132(b). SB 4 repeals the grace period that previously allowed

election officials to count advance ballots returned by mail but received after

election day, so long as they were postmarked by election day and received in the

mail within three days thereafter. See K.S.A. 25-1132(b) (2017).

had in 2025 and was the rule applied in Kansas elections for nearly ten years

before. If SB 4 is enforced in Kansas’s upcoming elections, it will replace the grace

they were voted and mailed days or even weeks before—and will mandate that

election officials reject any advance ballot returned by mail received any time after

7:00 p.m. on Election Day. K.S.A. 25-1132(b).
