Post-Election Audit Information
The post-election audit will be held Friday, November 8, 2024, at 8:30 a.m. in the Finney County Election Office at 311 N. 9th Street, Garden City, Kansas.  Precinct(s) to be audited will be selected randomly at 1:00 p.m. on Thursday, November 7, 2024, at the same location.
La auditoría posterior de las elecciones se llevará a cabo el viernes 8 de noviembre de 2024 a las 8:30 a.m. en la Oficina de Elecciones del Condado de Finney ubicada en 311 N. 9th Street, Garden City, Kansas.  Las carreras electorales y recintos a auditar se seleccionarán al azar a las 1:00 p.m. el jueves 7 de noviembre de 2024 en el mismo lugar.
___________________________________
Canvass Information
The Board of County Canvassers will convene to canvass election results on Monday, November 18, 2024, at 8:30 a.m. in the Commission Chambers within the Finney County Administrative Center, 311 N. 9th Street, Garden City.
La Junta de Electores del Condado se reunirá para el escrutinio de los resultados de las elecciones el lunes 18 de noviembre de 2024 a las 8:30 a.m. en las Cámaras de la Comisión dentro del Centro Administrativo del Condado de Finney, 311 N. 9th Street, Garden City.

FAQs
Am I eligible to vote?
Voters in Kansas must be:
Registered to vote
Note - registration deadline is twenty-one (21) days prior to any election
Eighteen (18) years of age or older on or before Election day
Note - new voters can register at seventeen (17) years of age
A citizen of the United States
A resident of Kansas
Check your status on
Kansas Voter View

Online
Online Voter Registration
* Note you must have a valid Kansas drivers license or non-driver identification card to register online

Do I need to register before each election?
No.
Once registered to vote in Kansas, you will remain registered until you tell us otherwise; however, you must be registered at your current address, so you should
check
your registration before each election.
Check your information
before each election to ensure a streamlined voting experience and to make sure your vote is counted.
Canceling your registration must be done in writing.
