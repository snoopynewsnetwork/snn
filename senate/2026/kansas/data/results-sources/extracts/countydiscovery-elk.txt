The Elk County Clerk is a member of the Kansas County Clerks Association.
Below is an overview of some of the clerk services:
Payroll, accounts payable, financial reports, maintain tax accounts
Audit all budgets, compute tax levies, provide budget assistance to Townships, Cities and Special Districts
Prepare real estate, person property, State assesed, in Lieu of Tax and special assessment tax rolls
Balance & distribute Motor Vehicle tax and track hearing appeals
Official secretary to the board of county commissioners, the keeper of official records, maintain inventory and fixed assets
Maintain tax unit maps and assist with special districts creation
Service to the public and Homestead preparation for the elderly
Issue permits & licenses
In addition to these duties, the Clerk serves as the County Election Officer and is responsible for the overall supervision of the election process for Elk County.
