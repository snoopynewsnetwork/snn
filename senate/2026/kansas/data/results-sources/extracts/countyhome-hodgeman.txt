City of Hanston Utilities FAQ:
All  new customers and reconnections are required to complete a utility contract.  There is a $50 connection fee to begin service.
Utilities provided by the City of Hanston include Water and Sewer
Sewer is a base $16 per connection.
Water is a base $15 for the first 4000 gallons -- Any usage over 4000 gallons is charged .75 cents per 1000 gallons.
Utility bills are due on the 20th of each month.  A late fee of $10 is charged if the full payment is not received on or before the 20th.  If the payment has not been received by the 28th of the month...services will be disconnected.
If services are disconnected, a reconnection fee of $50 is required, along with the past-due amount and late fees, to be reconnected.
Payments can be mailed, or dropped off at Hanston State Bank.
City Utility Questions?
Call 620-623-2

Hunting and Hospitality
Hodgeman County is well-known for it's abundant wildlife and exceptional hospitality.  Upland game, deer, and now (with the addition of HorseThief Reservoir) waterfowl and fishing.  We welcome generations of outdoorsman year after year.  You can purchase your platt-map books/County Directories in the Hodgeman County Courthouse.

What is an e-community?
Hodgeman County is an e-community ....a partnership with Network Kansas.  The program was created through a Kansas Center for Entrepreneurship Tax Credit Program in 2015 and encourages entrepreneurial activity in the communities of Hodgeman County.
Qualifying entrepreneurial projects may include business startup businesses, as well as existing business purchases and/or expansions.
Learn more HERE.
Business Loans
What is an e-community?
What is an e-community?
Qualifying entrepreneurial projects may include startup businesses, as well as existing business purchases and/or expansions. The minimum loan request is $2,000 with a $45,000 maxium amount available per applicant business. Who qualifies? Anyone wishing to start, purchase or expand a for-profit business in Hodgeman County. Use of Funds:  Purchase of business equipment; Purchase of business inventory; Purchase of real estate; Working capital; or Existing Inventory.

Elk Plaza Business & Culinary Incubator
Location
Located at 203 N. West Street in Hanston, KS  67849.
Amenities
The Elk Plaza Community Center, Business and Culinary Incubator is a revitalized former high school that now serves the community in many ways. It is home to the City of Hanston offices, Little Tracks Pre-School, and several local businesses. The former Home-Ec room and concession stand now operate as a licensed culinary incubator, while the facility also hosts camps, exercise groups, and youth meetings. Amenities include on-site mailboxes, a break room, conference space, and new circuit-style exercise equipment.
Contact Information
There are several numbers for you to contact for Elk Plaza.  Michelle Walters can be contacted to reserve locations for events at 620.623.0108; Lon Ruff can be contacted at 620.623.0123 for rental space.
History...How did a tiny town get an AWESOME SPACE like this???
In 2010, the two Unified School Districts in Hodgeman County consolidated and this building was left empty.  The School Board, Marena Township and community members had the foresight to turn the property into a Community and Business Center.  In 2015 the Culinary Incubator was added.
