Labette County Kansas
A great place to live, work, and raise a family!
Labette County looks forward to seeing you in our community!  Our county is a family oriented friendly community located in Southeast KS.  The county seat is Oswego and the most populous city is Parsons.  As of 2021 the county population was 19,042.
Explore this site to learn about Labette County offices, departments, and services and as a resource for Labette County residents and visitors.
Labette County Administrative Offices are open Monday thru Friday and available to the public during the following hours:
