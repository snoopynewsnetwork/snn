🚨 NEWSFLASH: 2026 GENERAL ELECTION INFORMATION 🚨
The Atchison County Clerk's Office wants to remind voters of several important dates and voting opportunities for the
2026 General Election on Tuesday, November 3, 2026
.
📅 Registration Deadline
October 13, 2026 – Registration Books Close
Please note that this deadline is commonly cited as
K.S.A. 25-2311(a)(3)
rather than (5). Voters must be registered by this date to participate in the General Election.
🗳️ Election Day Voting Hours
Tuesday, November 3, 2026
Polling locations throughout Atchison County will be open from:
7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.
✅ Public Test of Voting Equipment
A public test of the voting equipment to be used in the 2026 General Election will be conducted beginning at
7:00 a.m.
on:
Wednesday, October 7, 2026
Memorial Hall
819 Commercial Street
Atchison, KS 66002
Members of the public are welcome to attend.
📨 Early Voting Begins October 19
Early voting will be available at the:
Atchison County Clerk's Office
423 N. 5th Street
Atchison, KS 66002
Early Voting Period:
October 19, 2026 through November 2, 2026 at
12:00 p.m. (Noon)
Extended Early Voting Hours
October 26 (Monday): 7:00 a.m. - 7:00 p.m.
October 28 (Wednesday): 7:00 a.m. - 7:00 p.m.
October 30 (Friday): 7:00 a.m. - 7:00 p.m.
Regular Early Voting Hours
October 19 (Monday): 8:30 a.m. - 4:30 p.m.
October 20 (Tuesday): 8:30 a.m. - 4:30 p.m.
October 21 (Wednesday): 8:30 a.m. - 4:30 p.m.
October 22 (Thursday): 8:30 a.m. - 4:30 p.m.
October 23 (Friday): 8:30 a.m. - 4:30 p.m.
October 27 (Tuesday): 8:30 a.m. - 4:30 p.m.
October 29 (Thursday): 8:30 a.m. - 4:30 p.m.
📍 Satellite Advance Voting Locations
Take advantage of convenient satellite voting opportunities:
Lancaster
October 21, 2026 | 3:00 p.m. - 7:00 p.m.
Lancaster City Hall
301 Kansas Street, Lancaster, KS
Cummings
October 22, 2026 | 3:00 p.m. - 7:00 p.m.
Cummings Christian Church
2583 McPherson Road, Cummings, KS
Muscotah
October 23, 2026 | 3:00 p.m. - 7:00 p.m.
Muscotah City Hall
604 Kansas Avenue, Muscotah, KS
📬 Ballot Drop Box Available 24/7
A secure ballot drop box is available
24 hours a day, 7 days a week
outside the:
Atchison County Courthouse
423 N. 5th Street
Atchison, KS
Advance ballots may be returned at any time. To be counted, ballots must be deposited in the drop box
no later than 7:00 p.m. on Election Day, Tuesday, November 3, 2026
.
❓Questions?
Atchison County Clerk's Office
423 N. 5th Street
Atchison, KS 66002
📞 Phone: 913-804-6030
📧 Email: clerks@atcoks.org
For additional election information, please contact the County Clerk's Office during normal business hours.
Your Vote. Your Voice. Be Prepared for Election Day!
