Election Results
Election results can be found in the blue box to the left. Select the type of election you are interested in (Special Election, Primary Election, or General Election). Once you choose the election type, you will be taken to the page where the results are posted.
Write-In and Provisional results are available within each specific Election Results page.
You may also view
Archived Election Results
, which include both current and past election years. This section allows you to customize your search by specific race or browse all previous elections.

Poll Workers
Election workers needed.  Join our team today! Interested in earning money? Earn for yourself, your church, charity, or organization! Be a poll worker!
Click here for application
Requirements
Registered to vote in Miami County
Available to work long hours. Election school is paid and is a minimum of two hours and election day is a minimum of 12 hours.
Willing to travel to polling places located within the county, and sometimes on short notice.
Wages
Election day wages at the polls are:
Judges/Clerks - $225
Supervising Judges - $300
Students
We would love to have students who reside in Miami County join our team. Must be at least 16 years of age and be able to attend a day of training and work on election day.
Contact our office for additional information  at 913-294-3976
