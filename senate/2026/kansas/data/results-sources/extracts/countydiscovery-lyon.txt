Election Office
The polling location for Precincts 1, 2, 7, and 8 has been moved from the National Guard Armory to the First United Methodist Church at 823 Merchant Street, Emporia, Kansas.  Voters are encouraged to park in the back parking lot and enter through the doors on the back side of this building on election day.
This change will be for this election only.
