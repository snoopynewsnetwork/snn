Election Information
Current Election Info
Our mission is simple: make voting easy, accessible, and secure for every eligible voter.  Whether you’re registering to vote, exploring information about upcoming elections, or researching ballot issues, our team is here to help you stay informed and confident in your vote.
The Finney County Election Office is committed to providing fair, secure, and accessible elections for every resident of Finney County.  We administer all local, state, and federal elections within the county and work diligently to protect the integrity of the democratic process.
Our office also maintains Finney County’s official voter registration database, ensuring all voter records remain accurate and current.  In addition, we serve as the local reporting hub for the Kansas Governmental Ethics Commission, supporting transparency and compliance in campaign finance reporting.  The County Clerk serves as the County Election Officer and oversees all election operations.
We are committed to voter education and community engagement.  The Election Office welcomes opportunities to partner with schools, civic groups, and local organizations to provide presentations, resources, and information about the election process.
Thank you for visiting.  We hope this site helps you stay informed, engaged, and empowered as a voter in Finney County.
Questions?  We’re here to help.  Contact the Finney County Election Office - call (620) 272-3575 or text (620) 214-7887
