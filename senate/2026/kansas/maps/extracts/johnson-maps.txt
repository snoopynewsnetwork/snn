   District & County Maps 
 


   
 
  
    
       

  
    

  
   
            
     
             
              
   Johnson County, Kansas contains parts or all of 10 state senate districts, 27 state house of representative districts, 3 state board of education districts, 6 county commissioner districts, 20 municipalities, 7 townships, 8 school districts, 2 drainage districts and 1 water district. 
 The  Johnson County AIMS Department  offers an  Online Mapping Application   as a service to the community.  This on-line mapping application includes many different data layers in addition to voting precinct boundaries, which is nested under Admin Layers in the AIMS online mapping application.
