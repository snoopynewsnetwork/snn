  Vote in Honor of a Veteran

 
         
     
               
           
                    
                            
     
 The Vote in Honor of a Veteran Program provides community members with an opportunity to recognize and celebrate veterans and active-duty service members who have served our country. Tributes submitted through this program may be publicly displayed as part of the Johnson County Election Office website, for other members of the community to honor.  
 Submit a tribute recognizing a veteran or service member in your life.
