   Since its inception in 1991, the Data Access & Support Center (DASC) has served as the State of Kansas Geographic Information System (GIS) data
    clearinghouse responsible for archiving and distributing authoritative and best available Kansas geospatial data sourced from trusted local,
     Kansas, and Federal data stewards and data partners. DASC provides a variety of geospatial technical services for state agencies and data partners
      including web-based application development and hosting, database development, data validation and integration, technical support, as well as
       administers the state agency Esri EA agreement.  


DASC plays a vital role in Kansas geospatial coordination activities by participating in national, regional and state working groups,
 committees, projects and user groups to develop and facilitate geospatial relationships and standards between all levels of government
  and data partners.  DASC's Kansas Geoportal Hub content is constantly expanding and evolving through these relationships and efforts.
