# Original map visual review

Observed 2026-10-18 UTC. All 61 pages of the 23 downloaded PDF documents are
rendered into seven [contact sheets](previews/pdf-contact-00.jpg); whole originals
are retained. These counts include a polling notice, a rejected legislative map,
older references and limited context maps: they do not mean 23 current county
precinct maps. Individual full-page previews and complete PDF metadata/text are
in `previews/` and `extracts/`. Source originals remain authoritative at full
resolution; raster previews are display aids.

Representative visual inspections completed:

- Johnson: countywide precinct boundaries and native short labels, entire page;
  printed date October 17, 2026. The current download differs from the older
  date in the web search cache. The downloaded bytes and printed date control.
- Douglas: actual county Voting Precinct Parts map, dense urban parts and rural
  boundary lines, printed July 9, 2026. Precinct parts retain their source scope.
- Miami: Political Townships/Voting Districts map with detailed native labels
  such as `OSG.01`; not just a county outline or polling-point screen.
- Riley: county raster has actual precinct labels, district splits and Fort Riley
  pieces; printed April 2026, visible despite no extracted text. Manhattan's
  complementary map also prints April 2026. Source page bounds are complete.
- Harvey: representative urban pages 1-4 and rural pages 23-31 show highlighted
  precinct outlines, neighboring precinct context and complete legends. All
  39 original pages render. The landing page's 41-precinct statement cannot be
  reconciled by comparing it to page count alone; neither a missing-unit cause
  nor complete current roster equivalence is inferred.
- Lyon: scanned city map prints October 5, 2026; rural map prints April 15, 2026.
  The original November 3, 2026 notice says the polling place changed to First
  United Methodist Church. This documents polling location, not boundary approval.
- Finney: county and city originals show labeled ward/district parts and separate
  legends. Actual county maps, not HTTP viewer shells, were rendered.
- Wyandotte: official county Wards and Precincts PDF has native ward/precinct
  labels, street index and printed June 17, 2025 publication date. Its 153-row
  service returned no geometry; the PDF is distinct evidence.
- Leavenworth: separately downloaded 2025 and 2026 Voting Districts maps show
  complete county areas and detailed voting-unit labels. Both publication labels
  are visible. The original county-linked shapefile request failed with 403.
- Ellis: first original is rural precinct geography, crediting County Clerk 2023.
  Additional Hays PDF shows actual W/P precinct labels and boundary lines.
  Ellis City and Victoria PDFs show city limits/polling context, without an
  independently proved precinct boundary equivalence. Their inclusion does not
  convert city outlines into precinct geometry. All four originals credit 2023.
- Barton: Great Bend is an actual 2023 precinct/ward map printed June 4, 2024.
  Hoisington shows wards. Countywide color shading groups voting locations and
  is retained as polling-area context, not accepted as all rural precinct splits.
- Pottawatomie: downloaded county legislative map depicts state House district
  boundaries and other context. It is wrong-scope for complete precinct mapping
  and is rejected as precinct geometry, with its original retained. The actual
  2022 state native precinct layer supplies the explicit historical fallback.
- Cowley: UF-mirrored county-produced map explicitly prints May 8, 2018; that
  differs from the archive's 2020 category and 2024 accession date. The map shows
  historical township/ward/precinct labels over imagery. It remains a secondary
  repository historical reference, not a current county-approved map.
