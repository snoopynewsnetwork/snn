## Candidate and write-in scope can change membership

**The 765-unit dispatch is named-canvass-only and remains qualified.** The special-election state table publishes only Ricketts and Love; unlike the other 2024 Senate table, it has no scattering column. Retained local originals report **1,117 generic special write-in marks across nine counties**: Douglas 701, Lancaster 377, York 12, Nemaha 10, Clay 8, Nance 5, Garfield 2, Gosper 2 and Deuel 0. These are source observations, not a proven eligible named write-in total. The source-native selected partition has generic observations for Douglas/Lancaster’s 431 contexts (1,078 marks); all other **895 selected HTML generic fields are unavailable**. Nested `Not Assigned` equals the parent `Write-In Totals` in the inspected Electionware sources and is never added again. [Complete universe/bounds body](data/turnout/extracts/candidate-universe.json); [Douglas summary](https://www.votedouglascounty-ne.gov/elections/2024/General/GN24ResultsSummary.pdf); [Lancaster original](https://web.archive.org/web/20260714153214id_/https://www.lancaster.ne.gov/DocumentCenter/View/25879/GN24-Precinct-Summary-Report-Final); [local original controls](data/turnout/extracts/additional-summary-controls.json).

A deliberately incomplete sensitivity adds only those 1,078 actually observed native generic marks to their own Douglas/Lancaster contexts, leaving the 895 unavailable values unallocated. It has named-plus-available-mark sum **936,083**, still requires **765 contexts**, and reaches **749,234**; however, it **replaces `NE:2024-special:html:39:0003` with `NE:2024-special:lancaster-pdf:page-419`**, which becomes the crossing unit. This is not a complete all-mark or eligible-vote ranking. It demonstrates why eligible write-in membership robustness cannot be claimed. [Reproducible original-scope calculation](data/turnout/universe.py); [calculated body](data/turnout/extracts/candidate-universe.json).

Conditional algebra, without asserting any such votes exist: **233 additional genuine eligible votes wholly outside the fixed named 765** would lower that fixed set below 80%; **1,795 additional genuine eligible votes wholly inside the fixed named 764** would let that fixed set reach 80%. These are algebraic thresholds, not observed eligible counts or allocations. The separate statewide **965,236 Total Voting − 935,005 named votes = 30,231** is only a coarse ballot-capacity ceiling; it includes under/over/blank and non-Senate eligibility categories, not measured write-ins. Affidavits, validity and aggregate reporting are distinct under the [current official write-in validity provision](https://www.nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=32-1005), [filing provision](https://www.nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=32-615) and [aggregate reporting provision](https://www.nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=32-1008). Their 2026 observation does not retroactively establish unchanged 2024 law, a complete 2024 affidavit roster, or the legal status of generic marks. No undisclosed eligible candidate is invented. [State voting controls, pp.8–9](https://sos.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/doc/elections/2024/2024%20General%20Canvass%20Book.pdf#page=8).
