                                         Reported Problems
Dawson County
Dawson Public Power District – Dawson Subdivision is a “Vote for up to TWO” but it was erroneously listed as
“Vote for ONE” on the ballot. This error was identified by Dawson County Clerk on October 7. At the time the
error was identified, 240 ballots were already sent out to voters. The county clerk ordered corrected ballots on
October 8 and mailed out corrected ballots on October 11 along with a letter of explanation to impacted voters to
reduce the scope of the error. Of the 240 voters issued a corrected ballot, 239 returned or recast their ballot: 152
voters returned a corrected ballot, 26 indicated they only wanted to vote for one candidate and didn’t want to vote a
corrected ballot, and 61 voters returned only the incorrect ballot without giving any indication that they didn’t want
to vote the corrected ballot sent to them. One voter never returned either ballot.

The difference in votes between second and third place was 20. Despite the outreach done by the clerk to reduce
the scope of the error and corrected ballots being sent to all impacted voters, the 61 voters who only returned the
incorrect ballot could have impacted the outcome of the election.

Gage County
After Election Day, it was discovered that Lower Big Blue NRD – Subdistrict 1 was left off the ballot in 32 out of
41 ballot styles. There were 2,396 voters who did not get to vote on this contest. There was only one candidate on
the ballot and no write-in candidates. As a result, this error did not impact the outcome of the election.

Johnson County
On Election Day, it was discovered State Board of Education – District 2 was left off the ballot in one ballot style
within one precinct. There were 54 voters who did not get to vote on this contest. The difference in votes between
first and second place was 5,534. This error did not impact the outcome of the election.
