                                         Reported Problems
Dodge County
Shortly after early voting ballots were sent out, it was discovered that a candidate for Lower Platte North Natural
Resources District Subdistrict 07, Doug Shuster, was left off the ballot. At that time, 2,761 ballots had been mailed
or voted in office. The Dodge County Clerk posted an apology and explanation on their website, and impacted
voters were notified and provided an opportunity to vote the contest. The Secretary of State notified candidates and
the NRD. Ballots issued after this point, including for election day, were corrected ballots and not impacted.

For the ballots already voted in office, a supplemental ballot was provided with only the Lower Platte North NRD
Subdistrict 07 contest and a postage paid return envelope.

For the ballots already mailed out, a corrected ballot was sent. The voters who already returned a ballot were
provided a postage paid return envelope. The remainder were instructed to spoil the original ballot. If the corrected
ballots were returned, they were counted and the corresponding original ballots were rejected.

All original ballots submitted for counting and not corrected were duplicated by the Dodge County Resolution
Board without the Lower Platte North NRD Subdistrict 07 contest. No votes were counted for Lower Platte North
NRD Subdistrict 07 from original ballots.

The difference in votes between Engel and Shuster was 2,014. All but 208 original ballots submitted for counting
were corrected. As a result, the original early voting ballots that were submitted and not corrected did not impact
the outcome of the election.

Platte County
Shortly after early ballots were sent out, it was discovered that Loup River Public Power District Subdivision 03 was
not included on several ballot styles following redistricting. Of the 166 supplemental ballots sent out to impacted
early voters who already received a ballot, 88 were returned and counted and 78 were not returned. All future early
voters and election day voters received both the original ballot and supplemental ballot at the same time and thus
had no impact.

The margin in this contest was 393. As a result, this error did not impact the outcome of the election.
