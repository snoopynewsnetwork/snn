**Conditional paths with complete weights.** For fixed pair votes `T_i=A_i+B_i`, `s_i=A_i/T_i`, `T=ΣT_i`, and `M=ΣT_i(2s_i−1)`, the raw uniform A-share shift for target pair margin `g` is `(gT−M)/(2T)`. At original weights, tie is `(B−A)/(2(A+B))`. A+1 pair-margin point sets `g=0.01` and adds **0.5 share points**, not one share point. A +1 share-point transfer adds **2 margin points** and **0.02T_i net votes** at fixed pair volume. For all-candidate denominator C, one margin point instead requires `0.01C` net votes: **5,006.06** in 2024 Senate. The A+1 pair cushion is only **0.960937 pp of the 500,606-vote all-candidate denominator**. These quantities are separate columns in [state scenarios](data/baselines/state-scenarios.csv) and [every-unit targets](data/baselines/unit-targets.csv).

Selected-only thresholds hold **every other county at its own unshifted 2024 Senate share and original pair volume**. For selected unit i, `q_i=[T_i+gT−M_other]/(2T_i)`. The full target file supplies the analogous result for every retained native unit/RD/alternative county view, with other-unit assumptions, weights, baseline/target net contributions, gains and both tie/+1 targets. They are alternative combinations, not necessary conditions in each county. Shares are explicitly clipped to [0,1] and a bounded monotone solver is used when the raw uniform shift is infeasible. Unbounded requirements, saturation solution rays, already-winning/secured, impossible and no-positive-pool states remain explicit. Zero-pair units remain rows with undefined share and zero weighted contribution.

Delaware’s legal general-election rule is **plurality over every individual candidate**, subject to a highest-vote tie; it is not a two-candidate majority, RCV or runoff. At the historical 2024 Senate pair tie, each major candidate would have 240,525.5 expectation votes while Katz remains 19,555 under that stated fixed-other assumption, so the illustrative pair calculation is relevant there. It does not guarantee a future plurality if another candidate’s votes change. No transfer/lower-preference or candidate-effect assumption is inferred. [15 Del. C. §5703 and tie provision §5708](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title15/c057/index.html).

**Offline conditional live remainder.** The [calculator](data/baselines/calculators.py) keeps counted A/B/each named other fixed and forecasts only an explicitly stated remaining total and its assumed named-other votes. Remaining pair pool is `U=remaining_total−remaining_other`; **final pair denominator** is `countedA+countedB+U`, and **final all-candidate denominator** is `all_counted_candidates+all_remaining_votes`. With counted lead L, final pair-margin target g needs `[U+g×final_pair_denominator−L]/(2U)` A share of the remaining pair votes. Each individual other candidate is checked separately for plurality. No pool, unknown pools/preferences, negative/nonfinite counts, impossible/secured thresholds, clipping and selected-unit-only assumptions remain explicit. Every remaining pool needs a unique source/geography/method key; duplicates are rejected globally.

There are **21 saved synthetic live scenarios**, **6 bounded-shift/selected cases**, nonfinite NaN/±Infinity target fixtures, and **23 substantive assertions**. For counted A40/B60 and 100 remaining pair votes, a final A+1 pair margin requires **61%** of the remainder because the final pair denominator is200. This differs from adding a 0.5-point share cushion on the remainder alone. A synthetic third-candidate case beats both pair candidates even when A beats B; the calculator preserves that outcome. These are arithmetic stress cases, not calibrated probabilities, observed releases, an implemented collector or measured publication cadence.
