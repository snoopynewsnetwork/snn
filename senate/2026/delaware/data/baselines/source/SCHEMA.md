# Delaware historical baseline data contract

Version 1; independently extracted 2026-10-19. All paths below are relative to `data/baselines/` unless stated otherwise. The owned report is `../../county-partisanship.md`; the compatibility county table is `../county-baselines.csv`. Every county and native unit remains in statewide arithmetic. There is no November 2026 geographic or ballot-ID approval.

**Original evidence and acquisition.** `source/shared-sources.json` freezes complete original response receipts from the read-only newsroom archive, with requested/final URLs, retrieval UTC, status, headers, raw/decompressed and stored SHA256, original size and exact relative shared path. These old receipts lack intermediate redirect history. `source/discovery-chains.json` independently retains actual archive/results hrefs, anchor text and character positions. `source/{current,additional,registration,support}-acquisition.json` record fresh direct original requests, final URL, every intermediate redirect/status/header, parent/discovered href where applicable, UTC, complete entity size/hash and compressed size/hash. `source/current/*.gz` contains complete HTTP entities after requests removed transport encoding; gzip decompression reconstructs the exact hashed entity. No originals are truncated, simplified or split. Every stored file is strictly below 40,000,000 bytes. See `source/source-id-qualifications.json` for the actual structure PDF behind the initial `registration-2022` acquisition ID and an incidental AVR report that enters no control arithmetic. Source document clocks are distinct from HTTP retrieval/Last-Modified. All historical contest clocks retain an unspecified timezone.

**Raw contest extraction.** `extracted/contest-tables.json` contains every row/cell/field/header/data-label in all five views of the five requested contexts: 2020 Senate and president, actual 2022 at-large House, 2024 Senate and president. There are 2,674 tables and 10,699 candidate rows. A table carries year/source ID/hash, view, literal office and geography key/label, character start/end, ordered headers, ordered rows. Rows retain ordered cells, raw unescaped field strings, optional data-labels, source row index and exact original start/end. Offsets are half-open Python Unicode character offsets after `utf-8-sig` decoding; original compressed bytes are authoritative, including any BOM and CRLF. Exact table/row substrings can be recovered from those originals; the raw table cell markup is never lost because the complete entity remains retained. `extracted/table-inventory.json` inventories all 17,232 tables, including unrelated offices and their reporting/administrative identities, without a redundant second derivative of every unrelated candidate row. Source `Candidate Name`/`Party Name` headers in some 2020 RD tables remain raw; the normalized CSV explicitly maps them to Candidate/Party.

`extracted/candidate-geography.csv` has year/election_date, source office identity, view, literal geography key/label, source-scoped county/FIPS evidence, complete candidate/party string, machine/absentee/early/total votes, raw displayed percentage and source positions. County views are horizontally expanded into county candidate rows; their State columns remain controls. They are alternative views, never additional votes. Empty CSV cells mean unavailable, not zero. Printed 0 remains integer 0. The 2020 native Early Voting column is absent; its nulls are preserved, despite the separate statewide CSV printing early=0. `extracted/state-original-csv-rows.csv` preserves every original statewide CSV office row/field with source row number; unrelated offices remain original evidence, outside baseline arithmetic. `extracted/candidate-method-controls.csv` checks each requested candidate/method ED sum to that separate statewide CSV.

**Geography.** Literal ED keys such as `ElectionDistrict0807` mean the source's 08-07 identity. They are scoped by election and contest. `extracted/county-parent-evidence.json` is the immutable original report-only county-office membership evidence, including unresolved keys. `extracted/participation-hierarchy.json` separately extracts actual county/RD/ED nesting from the same-year participation publication. `extracted/district-structure.json` has all 537 printed identities in the official structure PDF, including source page/line/raw text and footnotes. The PDF title says 2022 through 2032; its note says through the 2030 general; update 2024-02-13. Superscripts 1/2 are separate footnote fields, never appended to the ED key. `extracted/resolved-parent-evidence.json` combines only agreeing explicit evidence. Three president-only 2020 keys remain county-unavailable: 0218 (one Biden vote), 3117/4117 (printed zeros). They remain in the state. Neither names, numeric ranges, source sums nor this administrative hierarchy establish a current boundary or cross-year polygon join.

County authority is the Department's New Castle/Kent/Sussex offices and the county Superior Court board of canvass; publication here is the state Department's archive. RD, ED, county, State and Wilmington views overlap. `bycounty` New Castle includes Wilmington; `bycountyw` Wilmington plus Rest of New Castle partitions it. No Wilmington addition to inclusive New Castle is allowed. Method columns are attributes of the ED totals, not extra precincts or separately observed central counting-board IDs. The sources do not provide an exhaustive polling-site/central-board/ballot-style crosswalk; none is invented.

**Separate write-ins and candidate lists.** `extracted/writeins.csv` has all 43 required printed named write-in rows: 2020 president 17/state336; 2022 House2/state18; 2024 president24/county1215. Candidate/party scope, all available methods, null blank cells, printed total, page/y and source hash remain separate. 2020/2022 have no county or ED allocation; 2024 has county but no ED allocation. Party is null where not printed. `source/pdf-words.json`, complete page text/PNGs and `source/visual-review.json` retain source-position and visual evidence. The 2022 sheet says 2020 General Election but prints November8,2022 and November11,2022 7:23PM; the defect is not corrected in the original. No generic write-ins are equated to these named tallies.

`extracted/candidate-lists.json` retains every relevant original filed/declared row, complete raw fields/cells/source positions and source/table/row identity; repeated county-summary/main-list rows are not silently deduplicated. Optional missing `</td>` in current declared-list summaries is parsed by the next cell start. A shortened name prefix is a display helper; raw fields remain authoritative. The historical Senate lists and sheets have no declared Senate write-in rows, which supports the published named field, not an invented generic-write-in count. A qualified candidate absent from the tally is not assigned a fabricated 0. October 2026 qualified/declared names are observations, not final ballot order, vendor IDs or exhaustive signed/later ballot proof. Katz's 2024 independent identity remains distinct from the 2026 Republican list identity.

**Participation and registration.** `extracted/participation-totals.csv` preserves 1,631 `i. Total` rows only, at county/RD/ED/state scope, with four printed registration-party groups (Democrat/Republican/All Others/Total), Registered/Voted/%raw and exact source line/character offsets/raw line. No age/sex derivative is made. Party registration is not candidate choice. `county-participation-controls.csv` exposes all three counties and State. The report-labelled voter-roll date/status is unprinted; Voted is source participation, not an independently certified ballot-count assertion. `source/control-disagreements.json` preserves differing results headers and participation controls, exact state CSV clocks, and unavailable historic blank/under/over/generic-write-in quantities. A residual is not allocated to one of those categories.

`extracted/monthly-registration-original-rows.json` retains all selected Nov1 original rows/raw fields/source positions (2020 HTML; 2022/24 RD/SD/party CSVs). `extracted/monthly-registration-native.csv` preserves RD and SD rows separately, county/RD/SD/ED identity, all printed party counts, null sparse cells, `registered_total` only if every component is printed, `registered_sum_printed`, blank count, date and source position. `extracted/monthly-registration-controls.csv` is the county/State sum of printed cells in each RD/SD/party view; complete party-view controls independently match available RD sums. Blank 02-21 Republican and 07-13 Others cells in each 2022/24 RD/SD file remain null. Dates are explicitly November1, not election day. SD and RD denominators differ; the official notice explains federal-only overseas voters' absence from state Senate district assignment. No active/inactive definition is added where unprinted. `source/native-coverage-gaps.json` and monthly coverage files retain different inventories, including zero-only identities unavailable in a contest.

**Baseline rows and denominators.** `native-baselines.csv` has 2,699 geography/contest rows across five disjoint *alternative* views; `../county-baselines.csv` is its 15 county rows. Unique key: year/office_key/view/geo_key. A and B are the explicitly named Democratic and Republican candidates in these contests; no independent, Libertarian or nonpartisan is relabeled D. Raw candidate identity and all other candidates are in `candidates_json`. Separate known write-ins are in `supplement_candidates_json`.

`pair_total=A+B`; `named_total=pair_total+named_other`; `combined_published_total` adds named PDF supplements only where that source supplies the geographic scope. It is null for finer geographies without the allocation; it is not a generic-write-in or certified-ballot claim. Senate source-scoped supplement0 denotes absence in the published named list/tally, not a universal generic0. Pair share/margin/participation and weights are fractions, not points. `net_A_minus_B` is votes. `named_margin` and `combined_margin` use their explicitly different denominators. Weights reference the complete statewide contest, never only a priority subset. `pair_gain_net_per_1pp=0.02*pair_total` and state pair margin gain pp is `2*pair_state_weight`. Classification uses absolute pair margin <=5pp, >5–10pp or >10pp; historical winner A/B is not a permanent county label.

**Priority.** `geography-ranking.csv` holds full descending orders for each context/view, named and pair metrics separately, with integer votes, cumulative totals, top15 and minimum80 flags. Lexical literal source key breaks equal-vote ties. `priority-contracts.json` stores complete order/keys and exact integer threshold `ceil(4*N/5)=(4*N+4)//5`; preceding sum must be below and selected sum at/above that threshold. The primary contract is 2024 Senate all three printed candidates: 530ED/500606; minimum311 gives400539 after399708; top15 gives35875. County minimum2 is New Castle+Sussex414338; top15 contains all three counties, so the county priority union is all3. Geographic/legal acceptance remains the coordinator's separate decision.

**Scenarios.** `state-scenarios.csv` has 80 scenario/target rows; `unit-targets.csv` has 8,650 complete geography/target rows. Units are alternative exhaustive views, not summed across views. Each target is pair margin fraction0 or0.01. The historical scenarios use their own contest's pair turnout/preferences. The 12 county sensitivities use source county named-candidate volumes from 2022 House (low), 2024 Senate (base), 2024 president (high), multiplied by each county's *2024 Senate pair participation*. Preferences independently use each of four anchors (2020 Senate, 2022 House, 2024 Senate, 2024 president). See exact values in `scenario-settings.json`. Unallocated write-ins stay outside these expressly named-volume anchors. Three native sensitivities scale every 2024 Senate ED by the statewide named-volume ratio, preserving native mix; they do not join cross-year EDs. Historical anchors are not 2026 turnout projections or candidate effects.

For unit pair volume `T_i`, preference `s_i`, statewide `T=sum(T_i)` and `M=sum(T_i*(2*s_i-1))`, raw uniform shift is `(g*T-M)/(2*T)`. At original weights tie is `(B-A)/(2*(A+B))`; A+1 *pair-margin point* adds0.005 share fraction, or0.5 share points. Raw shifts are retained. Apply `clip(s_i+delta,0,1)` and solve the bounded monotone equation by bisection, keeping zero-pool/undefined shares out of weighted arithmetic; every unit remains present. Exact full-saturation targets retain an unbounded solution ray; impossible targets and no-positive-pool cases remain explicit. Share points100*delta, margin points200*delta, net votes and named/combined denominator cushions have separate columns. The +1 all-candidate net requirement is separately `0.01*C`, not `0.01*T`.

Selected-unit-only thresholds hold every other unit at its explicitly indicated unshifted preference and stated volume: `q_i=(T_i+g*T-M_other)/(2*T_i)`. Preserve unbounded q; show clipped display and secured(q<0), impossible(q>1), feasible or no-positive-pool status. These are conditional alternatives, not necessary county targets. Plurality requires A to exceed each individual other candidate; deterministic pair targets cannot replace that rule or imply transfers/lower preferences.

**Offline remaining calculator.** `calculators.py INPUT.json` accepts an object or list. `counted={A,B,other:{named_candidate:votes}}` is fixed. Each remaining row has unique explicit `unit` key (include source/geography/method context), `remaining_total`, named `remaining_other` count map, and assumed `pair_share`. Null pool or null other map yields unknown, never0. Negative/nonfinite counts, nonfinite/nonnumeric/out-of-bounds margin targets and duplicate/invalid remaining keys are rejected explicitly. Share assumptions outside[0,1] are clipped and recorded. Remaining pair pool `U=remaining_total-sum(remaining_other)`; forecast only U. `final_pair_denominator=countedA+countedB+sum(U)`; `final_all_candidate_denominator=sum(all counted candidates)+sum(remaining_total)`. For final pair margin g the all-remainder threshold is `[U+g*final_pair_denominator-(countedA-countedB)]/(2*U)`. Each named other candidate receives a separate final plurality threshold; `plurality_strictly_exceeds_share` is the largest B/other threshold. Ties do not elect A. With integer votes A must have at least one vote above every rival. Fractional outputs are conditional expectations, not actual legal counts.

`selected_unit` holds the other remaining unit preferences fixed; unknown other preference remains unknown. `synthetic-inputs/results.json`, `synthetic-bounded-cases.json`, `synthetic-invalid-numeric-targets.json` and `synthetic-validation.json` cover ordinary/final-denominator targets, unknown/no pool, negative counts, impossible/secured thresholds, all-other pools, clipping, third-candidate victory, duplicate pools and NaN/Infinity targets. Nonfinite tests are constructed in Python and recorded by description/result, never invalid JSON. These are arithmetic checks, not calibrated probabilities.

Raw10699 field/position comparisons, full five native rank sequences and supplemental party/count/null/hierarchy controls are separately checked; no peer derivative supplies baseline values. `source/stable-extraction-v1.json` and `source/stable-supplement-v1.json` seal the original-first inputs. Run `python3 senate/2026/delaware/data/baselines/reproduce.py` offline from repository root. Python3.11, requests and PyMuPDF/fitz are available; no dependency/cache directories are retained. Acquisition scripts are explicit network operations; offline reproduction never fetches. Shared original assets are required at their hashed paths. `check_preflight.py` safely invokes the requested coordinator helper with its output redirected here; independent `extract.py` does not import coordinator extraction code.

`validation.json` records substantive source/candidate/method/denominator/identity/weight/order/integer80/target/bounds/relative-link checks. `asset_audit.py` hashes every owned file, report, county CSV and exact shared original. Its manifest excludes itself to avoid a recursive self-hash; validation is included. A final read-only `validate.py --check-assets` checks the complete seal. No current collector, final 2026 native mapping, cadence measurement, remaining-pool estimate, candidate effect, probability calibration or approved current geometry is claimed.
