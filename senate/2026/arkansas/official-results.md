## Operative rules and bounded legal qualifications

The full [2025 SOS law compilation](https://www.sos.arkansas.gov/uploads/elections/Arkansas_Election_Laws_and_Constitution_2025_Edition.pdf), [2026 board manual](https://sbec.arkansas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026-CBEC-Manual.pdf), current published rules, complete session texts and orders are retained. Findings below are a dated reading, with legal approval still false pending later controlling developments; bounded searches did not establish exhaustive future docket coverage.

| Topic | Supported distinction |
|---|---|
| Senate outcome / tie | A.C.A.7-5-703 covers United States Senator and Representative: greatest legal vote total wins; an equal highest vote triggers a special runoff four weeks after the general election. This is plurality, with a tie exception. County/municipal majority runoffs under7-5-106 and Act991 are separate. Primary nomination, nonpartisan judicial and presidential-elector procedures are not Senate outcome rules. |
| Recount | 7-5-319/Act321 permits a dissatisfied candidate's petition and a board-initiated recount. The candidate deadline depends on whether outstanding overseas plus uncanvassed provisional ballots could change the result: two days after preliminary declaration in the insufficient branch; before final canvass/certification or noon on day7 in the potentially sufficient branch. Cost/deposit provisions and one-recount-per-candidate limit remain in the full text. No universal automatic margin threshold is inferred from the vendor recount dictionary. |
| Absentee / postmark | 7-5-411 and the2026 manual distinguish ordinary mailed receipt by7:30p.m.ElectionDay; personal/bearer/administrator delivery by close of business Friday before; authorized-agent delivery by7:30p.m.ElectionDay. Overseas voters require execution/signing/postmark/mailing by ElectionDay and receipt by5p.m.day10; active-duty military execution by ElectionDay has no postmark requirement and the same receipt window. Ordinary domestic ballots do not gain an overseas postmark exception. |
| Provisional / cure | Amendment51§13,7-5-323 and7CAR91 distinguish provisional eligibility review, ID correction and notice/hearing procedures. ID verification can be supplied by noon Monday after the election. This is not a general cure for every rejected absentee signature/address/statement defect; application/statement mismatches and other statutory rejection grounds remain separate. No rejected or provisional ballot is automatically a counted Senate vote. |
| Opening / release | 7-5-416 permits specified pre-election absentee processing; count/publication restrictions remain separate. 7-5-701 requires preliminary/unofficial precinct transmission as tabulated, no later than24hours after polls close, with outstanding UOCAVA/provisional/duplication statements. A legal reporting duty is not measured website cadence or a demonstrated public exact pool. |
| Canvass / certification | 7-5-701 sets county certification no earlier than48hours and no later than day15. 7-5-703 provides state cast-up in the Governor's presence within30days after the county-return period, or sooner once all returns arrive, followed by Governor proclamation/certificate. Source `isOfficial`, a PDF label, statutory deadline and an actual signed certification event are distinct. |
