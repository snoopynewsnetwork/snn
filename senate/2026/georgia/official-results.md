# Georgia official results: authorities, source behavior and admission limits

Research observed **October 18, 2026 UTC**. This is a retained-source investigation and offline adapter specification. It establishes **all159 county publication scopes**, individually inspects the exact44 priorities, and independently extracts795 historical county payloads and76,400 candidate/method positions. It does not approve a November feed, reporting crosswalk, collector, exact outstanding Senate-vote count or calibrated probability.

The [source inventory](data/official-results-sources.csv) has one SOS row and159 county rows. Every county has five actual historical selected JSON payloads and a fresh current-root test. The44 priorities additionally have individually discovered county government/election pages, actual link inspection, bounded child/download attempts and specific access gaps. The other115 have **state-app channel and schema discovery**, not an individually researched local-website claim. This distinction is explicit in every CSV row. Consolidated Augusta/Richmond, Columbus/Muscogee, Macon/Bibb and Athens/Clarke remain one county each; their city observations overlap their county totals.

## Authority chain and actual current behavior

The [SOS results index](https://sos.ga.gov/page/georgia-election-results) explicitly links the [public results app](https://results.sos.ga.gov/results/public/Georgia), [historical archive](https://sos.ga.gov/page/historical-elections-results) and separate certified write-in publications. Direct SOS index/archive/PDF requests often returned403, retained as failed originals; web-rendered authority observations and successful linked app originals are distinct. The [Georgia.gov county-office authority](https://georgia.gov/locate-county-registrars-office) links the [MVP locator](https://mvp.sos.ga.gov/s/county-election-offices), which returned403. The current app's root locality list is a special-election view, not a statewide county-office directory. All159 county channel identities instead come from the actual2024 statewide election hierarchy, independently checked against Census county FIPS.

The retained app HTML/main/imported service bundle supplies literal `getJurisdiction` and `getData` routes. Returned election/public IDs, `childLocalities.shortName` and `parentBallotItemId` supply request parameters; none were invented. The general pattern is `/results/public/api/elections/{returned jurisdiction slug}/{returned election ID}/data/ballot-item/{returned parentBallotItemId}`. Request ballot ID, response contest GUID, county election GUID, jurisdiction GUID, source option nativeId and Census FIPS remain separate. Original casing is preserved, including upper/lowercase locality GUID representations. [Application excerpts](data/results-sources/extracts/application-evidence.json), [request inventory](data/results-sources/requests.json) and [shared original pins](data/results-sources/shared-inputs.json) make the discovery reproducible.

**Fresh state jurisdiction JSON contains117 listed elections and no November3,2026 general-election entry. All159 fresh county-root jurisdiction requests returned200 redirect/setup objects:** `id:null`, `redirectToShortName:"Georgia"`, `elections:[]`. These are retained negative controls, not genuine zero counts or populated county feeds. Historical **election-scoped** county payloads still work. Separately followed local SOS/Enhanced Voting pages commonly return the10622-byte app shell; a200 shell proves neither a selected election nor a Senate result. Actual local published routes are retained with original link positions in the CSV and per-county reviews.

The public report downloader was independently recovered from a lazy imported bundle. It builds `imageCdn + jurisdiction.id + "/" + report.blobName`, with the main configuration's `imageCdn="/cdn/results/"`. The exact returned `publicReportCategories[].reports[].blobName` values produced **five successful statewide Total Votes XLSX downloads plus Fulton2024**. These repair the public export lane despite blocked older SOS ZIP/query attempts. Original XLSX bytes, ordered ZIP members/hashes, workbook/sheet XML and full cell extracts are retained. These are historical exports, never projected November endpoints. See [download index](data/results-sources/extracts/download-index.json) and [selected export checks](data/results-sources/extracts/export-controls.json).

## Completion, overlap and corrections

A genuine **completion negative control** is [Walton's2024 official summary](https://www.waltoncountyga.gov/DocumentCenter/View/8360/November-5-2024-General-Election-Results-Summary-Report): page1 says **0/19 precincts reported**, while showing58,734 ballots and fully populated presidential rows. The [visually checked preview](data/results-sources/qa/local-13297-1-child-0.png) preserves that juxtaposition. Its58,493 raw reported votes include110 void choices and7 shown named write-ins; the named-four total is58,376. Percent/units reporting cannot be used as percent votes counted or transformed into an outstanding count.
