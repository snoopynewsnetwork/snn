Legal Notices for 2026 Precinct Changes  
Dissolution of Precincts 100/Augusta Christian School and 108/ACS Furys Ferry Road 
and New Boundaries of Precincts 107/Gospel Water Branch, 109/Stevens Creek Church, 
110/Columbia County Fire Headquarters, 115/Martinez Baptist Church, and 135/Christ 
Church Presbyterian 
1. Public Notice of Election Day Precinct Boundary Modification The Columbia 
County Board of Elections does hereby provide notice, as required in O.C.G.A. §§ 
21-2-262(a) and 21-2-262(c), of a change of precinct boundaries and polling 
locations involving the 100/Augusta Christian School and 108/ACS Furys Ferry 
Road and New Boundaries of Precincts 107/Gospel Water Branch, 109/Stevens 
Creek Church, 110/Columbia County Fire Headquarters, 115/Martinez Baptist 
Church, and 135/Christ Church Presbyterian precincts. In order to account for 
the desire to no longer serve as a facility at Augusta Christian School and ACS 
Furys Ferry Road, the proposed boundary modification will adjust the boundary 
lines for 107/Gospel Water Branch, 109/Stevens Creek Church, 110/Columbia 
County Fire Headquarters, 115/Martinez Baptist Church, and 135/Christ Church 
Presbyterian precincts to inherit the voters from 100/Augusta Christian School 
and 108/ACS Furys Ferry Road precincts.  The polling locations located at 
Precinct 100/Augusta Christian School and Precinct 108/ACS Furys Ferry Road 
will be dissolved and the new polling locations will now be located at Precinct 
107/Gospel Water Branch Baptist Church, Precinct 109/Stevens Creek Church, 
Precinct 110/Columbia County Fire Headquarters, Precinct 115/Martinez Baptist 
Church, and Precinct 135/Christ Church Presbyterian. Precinct boundary maps 
are currently on file in the Board of Elections Office, 610 Ronald Reagan Drive, 
Building G3, Evans, GA 30809, and any affected or interested citizens are 
encouraged and invited to view the maps at that office. The available maps will 
depict the current precinct boundaries as well as the proposed boundaries. This 
change in the above-mentioned boundaries will be considered and may become 
final on February 24, 2026. Any person objecting to these modifications must file 
his or her objections with the Director prior to such date. Should you have any 
questions regarding these precinct boundary modifications, please call the Board 
of Elections at 706-868-3355.
