Public Notice of Voting Precinct and Polling Place Changes 
 
The Forsyth County Board of Voter Registrations and Elections voted in a special called 
meeting on June 19, 2026 to propose changes to the boundaries of three voting precincts 
and the elimination of one voting precinct.   Pursuant to O.C.G.A. §21-2-262(d), the 
Forsyth County Board of Commissioners will vote whether to approve and adopt the 
proposal of the Forsyth County Board of Voter Registrations and Elections to change the 
boundaries of the three voting precincts.  The Forsyth County Board of Commissioners 
will discuss the changes at their work session on Tuesday, July 14, 2026 with a final vote 
to adopt the precinct boundary line changes scheduled at their regular meeting on 
Thursday, August 6, 2026.   
  
Any person objecting to any of the proposed precinct boundary line changes must file his 
or her objection prior to August 6, 2026 with Forsyth County Administration, 2435 
Freedom Parkway, Cumming, GA 30041.    
  
Any person with questions regarding these proposed changes may contact Mandi Smith, 
Director, Forsyth County Voter Registrations & Elections at 770-781-2118 ext. 9 or 
Voter@forsythco.com.  An electronic form is available online at www.forsythco.com/vote 
to submit comments.  All comments submitted electronically must be submitted no later 
than noon on Wednesday, August 5, 2026. 
  
A precinct boundary map with the proposed polling places changes is currently on display 
in the Voter Registrations & Elections Office, 1201 Sawnee Drive, Cumming, GA 
30040.

If approved, the proposed changes will result in the elimination of South Forsyth (21) 
Election Day precinct and polling place for a total of 29 Election Day precincts and polling 
places and boundary changes to Election Day precincts Big Creek (01), Johns Creek (35) 
and Daves Creek (38). The changes will take effect with the November 2026 General 
Election and Special Elections. Pursuant to O.C.G.A. 21-2-262 (c) and O.C.G.A. 21-2-
265 (a), the proposed precinct and polling place changes are listed as follows:  
 
Big Creek (01): This is a proposed adjustment of the eastern precinct boundary lines of 
the Big Creek (01) Election Day precinct.  The proposed change modifies the eastern 
boundary line as follows: from Bellehampton Drive at Peachtree Parkway; Peachtree 
Parkway south to Sharon Road; Sharon Road southeast to Old Atlanta Road; Old Atlanta 
Road south to Mathis Airport Parkway; Mathis Airport Parkway southwest to Peachtree 
Parkway at Brookwood Road; Brookwood Road south to Caney Road; Caney Road 
northwest to Old Alpharetta Road; Old Alpharetta Road southwest to McGinnis Ferry 
Road; and then following the current precinct boundary line west along McGinnis Ferry 
Road and the county line to the current precinct boundary lines with precincts Brandywine 
(02), Grassland (41), and Daves Creek (38). The polling place for the Big Creek (01)

precinct remains First Redeemer Church located at 2100 Peachtree Parkway, Cumming, 
30041.   The proposed changes will result in the voters south of Sharon Road who are 
currently assigned to South Forsyth (21) precinct to be reassigned to the Big Creek (01) 
precinct. 
 
Johns Creek (35): This is a proposed modification of the western precinct boundary lines 
of the current Johns Creek (35) Election Day precinct.  The proposed change modifies 
the western boundary line as follows: from Brookwood Road at Peachtree Parkway;  
Brookwood Road south to Caney Road; Caney Road northwest to Old Alpharetta Road; 
Old Alpharetta Road southwest to McGinnis Ferry Road; and then following the current 
precinct line southeast along McGinnis Ferry Road and the county line to the current 
precinct boundary lines with precincts Old Atlanta (19) and Mathis (39).  The polling place 
for the Johns Creek (35) precinct remains Johns Creek Baptist Church located at 6910 
McGinnis Ferry Road, Alpharetta, 30005.  The proposed changes will result in the addition 
of voters south of Old Alpharetta Road and Caney Road who are currently assigned to 
Big Creek (01) to be reassigned to Johns Creek (35).     
 
Daves Creek (38): This is a proposed modification of the southern precinct boundary 
lines of the current Daves Creek (38) Election Day precinct.  The proposed change 
modifies the southern boundary line as follows: from James Creek at Old Atlanta Road; 
Old Atlanta Road south to Sharon Road following the Nichols (36) precinct line; Sharon 
Road northwest to Peachtree Parkway; Peachtree Parkway north to the current precinct 
boundary lines with precincts Big Creek (01), Fowler (34), Lakeland (43), Cumming (07), 
Mashburn (08), and Windermere (25).  The polling place for the Daves Creek (38) precinct 
remains Grace Fellowship of South Forsyth, 2750 Ronald Regan Boulevard, Cumming, 
30041.  The proposed changes will result in the addition of voters assigned north of 
Sharon Road who are currently assigned to South Forsyth (21) to be reassigned to Daves 
Creek (38).
