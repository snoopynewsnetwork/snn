Legal Notices for 2026 Precinct Changes  
Dissolution of Precinct 024/Second Mount Moriah Baptist Church and New Boundaries 
of Precincts 020/Harlem Branch Library and 022/Harlem Senior Center 
1. Public Notice of Election Day Precinct Boundary Modification The Columbia 
County Board of Elections does hereby provide notice, as required in O.C.G.A. §§ 
21-2-262(a) and 21-2-262(c), of a change of precinct boundaries and polling 
locations involving the 024/Second Mount Moriah Baptist Church, 020/Harlem 
Branch Library, and 022/Harlem Senior Center precincts. In order to account for 
the desire to no longer serve as a facility at Second Mount Moriah Baptist Church, 
the proposed boundary modification will adjust the boundary lines for 
020/Harlem Branch Library and 022/Harlem Senior Center precincts to inherit 
the voters from 024/Second Mount Moriah Baptist Church. The polling location 
located at Precinct 024/Second Mount Moriah Baptist Church will be dissolved, 
and the new polling locations will now be located at Precinct 020/Harlem Branch 
Library and Precinct 022/Harlem Senior Center. Precinct boundary maps are 
currently on file in the Board of Elections Office, 610 Ronald Reagan Drive, 
Building G3, Evans, GA 30809, and any affected or interested citizens are 
encouraged and invited to view the maps at that office. The available maps will 
depict the current precinct boundaries as well as the proposed boundaries. This 
change in the above-mentioned boundaries will be considered and may become 
final on February 24, 2026. Any person objecting to these modifications must file 
his or her objections with the Director prior to such date. Should you have any 
questions regarding these precinct boundary modifications, please call the Board 
of Elections at 706-868-3355.
