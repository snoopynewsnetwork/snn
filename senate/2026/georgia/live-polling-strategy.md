## Current rules and remaining-vote uncertainty

The [official-source investigation](official-results.md) retains current primary rules, the [SOS detailed calendar hosted by Augusta](https://www.augustaga.gov/DocumentCenter/View/19921/2026-Elections-Calendar-Summary), the [August 10, 2026 tabulation rule](https://seb.ga.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/seb-rule-183-1-12-12-tabulating-results-effective-2026-08-10.pdf) and the [Governor's signed SB3EX](https://gov.georgia.gov/document/2026-special-session-signed-legislation/sb-3ex/download). Nominal November Senate poll close is 7pm Eastern Standard Time, or 00:00 UTC November 4; in-line voters and actual court extensions require separate treatment. Ordinary absentee ballots must arrive by poll close. Eligible military/overseas ballots and cure/provisional documentation have separately scoped November 6 deadlines. County certification is scheduled November 9 at 5pm, state certification November 20. These dates do not establish when a public source will release votes; retain later orders and source-specific clocks.

A Senate candidate needs a strict majority of valid office votes. If necessary, the scheduled December 1 runoff is a fresh stage; its ballot, qualified candidates, source IDs and electorate require separate verification. The candidate-request recount threshold is at most 0.5% of total office votes, with a request within two business days after certification. SB3EX's new mandatory manual provision enumerates state executive offices and excludes Senate; its separate audit provisions must not be conflated with that definition. The retained 2025 court opinion and current amended rules qualify stale 2024 certification/hand-count text. The calendar's printed reconciliation dates disagree with the rule's interval measured from actual certification; keep both source statements visible rather than inventing an upload schedule.

The current final sample ballots print Mike Collins and Jon Ossoff. Official county copies of the SOS FINAL list identify qualified Senate write-ins Elbert Arthur Bartell, Christina Clement and Cassandra Easton. A complete later withdrawal/change register remains unavailable. All current app election/contest/candidate IDs remain unapproved. Fresh state metadata lists 117 elections without the November general; all 159 county-root requests are HTTP 200 redirect/setup objects with null jurisdiction IDs and empty elections. Historical election-scoped routes work, but those current stubs supply no November counts or outstanding-vote inventory.

A general-election D-versus-R tie is not automatically the legal winning threshold when other candidates can prevent a majority. Show all-candidate majority arithmetic and potential runoff status separately from two-candidate margin scenarios. A runoff is a separate election with its own electorate, turnout, ballot and reporting configuration; November counts do not roll forward as December counted votes.

Keep observed candidate votes fixed and estimate only the remainder. Use aggregate source quantities with scope, denominator and as-of times. Ballot requests, returns, accepted ballots, tabulated votes and provisional/cured ballots may overlap; reconcile eligibility and counted status before constructing ranges. Do not infer candidate preference from party/administrative records or collect unnecessary personal voter details. Reporting-unit completion is not a percentage of votes counted or proof that all qualifying late ballots are resolved.

Compare incoming batches to compatible historical county/method/boundary observations. Early/mail-heavy first batches are selected, not random. Keep low/base/high remaining-vote assumptions and their provenance visible; widen uncertainty or use coarser comparisons when geographic crosswalks are unresolved. As counting proceeds, inspect counties contributing most remaining uncertainty as well as historical-volume priorities.

For an explicitly labeled two-candidate comparison with counted D-minus-R lead `L` and positive remaining major-party votes `U`, the conditional tie share is `(1-L/U)/2`; a selected county target also subtracts assumed net contributions elsewhere. Thresholds below zero or above one are secured/impossible under those assumptions. They do not establish all-candidate majority, runoff qualification or probabilities. The baseline assignment must keep November 2022 and December 2022 electorates separate and identify any prior same-seat runoff explicitly.
