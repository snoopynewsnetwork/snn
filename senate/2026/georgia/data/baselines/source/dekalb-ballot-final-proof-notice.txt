                   NOTICE OF BALLOT PROOF AVAILABILITY
                NOVEMBER 3, 2026 GENERAL / SPECIAL ELECTION

Candidates in the November 3, 2026 General / Special Election are hereby notified,
pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 21-2-283.1, that final ballot proofs for DeKalb County are
available in the office of Voter Registration and Elections, located at 4380 Memorial
Drive, Suite 300, Decatur, GA 30032-1239. Candidates or their agents may review the
proofs and request corrections to their name and other identifying information such as
party or incumbency.

The review period will be held during our regular office hours of 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. and
will extend until 9 a.m. on Thursday, September 10th. If requests are received during this
period, the Election Superintendent of DeKalb County may make changes to the proofs
if it determines that the requested changes will ensure the accuracy of the information
displayed on the ballot.


This 8th day of September 2026,
