# Georgia primary-source preflight

The official [results index](https://sos.ga.gov/page/georgia-election-results) links the newer [results application](https://results.sos.ga.gov/results/public/Georgia), a [historical archive](https://sos.ga.gov/page/historical-elections-results) and separate certified write-in publications. The research web parser exposed these links; direct SOS page, ZIP and write-in PDF requests returned 403 and remain retained. The app, its observed scripts and the literal public data routes returned 200. [Response manifest](news/manifest.json) preserves exact requested/final URLs, UTC, HTTP headers/status and original/stored hashes. Generic `.html.gz` captures include actual JavaScript and JSON, not just HTML.

The discovery chain is app `<base>` → published main script → imported service chunk → literal `/results/public/api` endpoint and jurisdiction/election/data templates → returned public election IDs → returned ballot-item IDs. Election and contest IDs were read from actual responses. [Offline preflight](coordinator-primary-preflight.json) verifies this chain and exact source hashes. The jurisdiction list has 117 elections and no observed November 3, 2026 general-election entry. Its six root child localities belong to a recent special view; they are not a statewide authority roster.

Actual historical payloads retain all 159 county election records and selected county candidate/method breakdowns for `2024NovGen`, `2021JanFedRun`, `2022NovGen` and `2022DecGenRun`. All candidate/county/method sums reconcile within their returned source scopes. Selected statewide payloads contain no actual precinct or polling-place rows; the first-wave workers must investigate finer county data or original exports. County names, source GUIDs, parent ballot-item IDs and map fields are distinct from Census FIPS and current approved geography.

The 2024 app’s four named options total 5,250,047: Trump 2,663,117; Harris 2,548,017; Oliver 20,684; Stein 18,229. These exactly match the [retained House Clerk text](../../ohio/data/baselines/house-2024.txt) and its original PDF. This is a named-four scope. The separately linked [qualified write-in certification](https://sos.ga.gov/sites/default/files/forms/Write%20In%20Certification%20File%2011222024.pdf) was readable through the research web parser and lists nine presidential names totaling 858. Direct original bytes and county allocation were not acquired. [Qualified write-in observation](news/web-primary-observations.json) is explicitly a factual web-parser transcription, not original PDF bytes. Do not allocate those votes or imply their omission means zero.

Provisional named-only top 15 total 2,939,795. The first 43 supply 4,199,292; 44 supply 4,222,572. These are unaccepted preflight counts, subject to independent worker extraction, FIPS/native reconciliation and resolution of the stated candidate universe. All 159 counties must remain in arithmetic. No exact dispatch priority is approved here.

The January 2021 Ossoff–Perdue runoff is the same Class II seat as 2026. The simultaneous Warnock–Loeffler special runoff is the other seat. The 2022 Warnock–Walker–Oliver general and subsequent two-candidate runoff are distinct electorates/stages and must not be combined. Current 2026 majority/runoff rules still require official verification. Source election date, lastUpdated, asOf and current retrieval are separate clocks; January 2025 metadata is not proof of a particular correction mechanism. State `ballotsCast=0` and zero active/inactive registration beside populated county/total fields are not validated statewide zeros.

Reproduce with `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 -S senate/2026/georgia/data/preflight_primary_sources.py`. Exact discovery IDs/URLs and raw payloads are retained for read-only reuse, with independent extraction still required. No Georgia question agents have launched. Historical success proves no November feed admission, genuine release cadence, exact remaining votes, current crosswalk or calibrated forecast.
