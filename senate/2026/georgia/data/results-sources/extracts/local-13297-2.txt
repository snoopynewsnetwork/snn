General Election Information Absentee Information Election Results Frequently Asked Questions Board Information & Public Notices Advance Voting Find My Polling Location VOTING OPTIONS: ABSENTEE BY MAIL If you want to vote an absentee ballot by mail, you may request an application by phone, email or print the application by clicking HERE . You may email the application to elections@co.walton.ga.us, fax it to 770-267-1408 or hand deliver to our office located at 1110 E Spring St. Ste. 100, Monroe. You may also fill out the application online at MVP.sos.ga.gov . ADVANCE VOTING There are three advance voting locations: South Walton Community Center in Social Circle, Felker Community Center in Monroe and Meridian Park in Loganville. ELECTION DAY You may vote at your assigned polling location on Election Day. The polls will be open from 7:00 am to 7:00 p.m. Please call our office at 770-267-1337 if you do not know your assigned location.
