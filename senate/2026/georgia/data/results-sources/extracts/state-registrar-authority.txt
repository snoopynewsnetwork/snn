Find Registrar Each county's Board of Registrar's Office oversees elections. Every county in Georgia has a County Board of Registrar's Office (also known as a county registrar or an elections office). Each county registrar processes voter registrations, sends out requested absentee ballots, manages polling locations, and more. If you have questions or encounter issues while voting, your first step should be to contact your county registrar.
