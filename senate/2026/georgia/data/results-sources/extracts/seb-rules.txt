The Georgia State Election Board is committed to ensuring transparency in the rulemaking process and providing public access to the laws and rules that govern Georgia’s elections. This page serves as a central resource for the Official State Election Board Rules, proposed rule amendments, rulemaking notices, and applicable Georgia election laws. The State Election Board adopts and amends its rules in accordance with the Georgia Administrative Procedure Act (APA), O.C.G.A. Title 50, Chapter 13. During the rulemaking process, members of the public are encouraged to review proposed rules and submit written comments during the designated public comment period. Public participation is an important part of the Board’s rulemaking process. During that period, members of the public may submit written comments for the Board’s consideration before a rule is adopted. View Proposed Rules & Public Comment Rulemaking Process The State Election Board follows the rulemaking procedures set out in the Georgia Administrative Procedure Act (O.C.G.A. Title 50, Chapter 13). Key provisions include: O.C.G.A. § 50-13-4 — Procedures for the adoption, amendment, or repeal of rules, including public notice and the opportunity to submit comments. O.C.G.A. § 50-13-9 — The right of any interested person to petition the Board for the promulgation, amendment, or repeal of a rule. You can read the full Georgia Administrative Procedure Act, including the step-by-step rulemaking requirements, in O.C.G.A. Title 50, Chapter 13.
