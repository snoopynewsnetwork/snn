Public Notice of Consolidation In accordance with O.C.G.A. §§ 21-2-492 and 21-2-493, notice is hereby given that the computation and canvassing of returns for November 3, 2026, General / Special Election will be conducted at: Election Hub & Operations Center 5600 Campbellton Fairburn Road Union City, Georgia 30213 Tabulation is scheduled to begin at 7:00 p.m. on Election Day, Tuesday, November 3, 2026 Please be advised of the canvassing process: As part of the Fulton County canvassing process, all physical ballots are accounted for and compared to the number of votes cast prior to election certification. Physical ballots cast are counted, but not tabulated, beginning the Wednesday after Election Day to verify that the number of physical ballots matches the reported votes cast at each Advance Voting and Election Day polling location. If a discrepancy is identified between the number of physical ballots and the number of votes cast, the affected ballots may be recanvassed, pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 21-2-495, to resolve the discrepancy. If the Superintendent determines that a recanvass of the ballot batch is necessary, the process will occur on Friday and/or Saturday following the election. Recanvassing will take place in the presence of the Vote Review Panel. Additionally, the Georgia Secretary of State's Office, county political parties, and affected candidates will be notified in advance of this process. The recanvassing process will also be open to the public. For information regarding whether recanvassing is necessary for the current election, please contact the Fulton County Department of Registration & Elections main office, 404-612-7020. Call for Election - ESPLOST NOTICE OF CALL FOR SPECIAL ELECTION TO THE QUALIFIED VOTERS OF FULTON COUNTY TO CONSIDER A SPECIAL ONE PERCENT SALES AND USE TAX NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that on November 3, 2026, an election will be held in all of the election districts of Fulton County, at which time there will be submitted to the qualified voters of Fulton County for their determination the question of whether or not a special one percent (1%) sales and use tax shall be reimposed on all sales and uses in Fulton County beginning July 1, 2027, upon the expiration of the current sales and use tax, and continuing for a period of time not to exceed 20 calendar quarters to raise not more than $2,095,680,577.00 to be expended for the educational purposes described below. If the continuation of the tax is approved by the voters, such vote shall also constitute the approval of the issuance of the general obligation debt of the Atlanta Independent School District in a principal amount not to exceed $350,000,000.00 (the “APS Bonds”) for the purpose of funding all or a portion of the APS Projects detailed below. The several places for holding said election shall be at the regular and established voting precincts of all of the election districts of Fulton County, and the polls will be open from 7 a.m. until 7 p.m. on the date fixed for the election. The registration deadline for taking applications from persons desiring to register to vote in this election will be the close of business on October 5, 2026. Early in-person voting begins October 13, 2026. The last day to submit an absentee ballot application shall be October 23, 2026. Click here to view the entire official notice. Call for Election - TSPLOST NOTICE OF CALL OF ELECTION TO THE QUALIFIED VOTERS OF FULTON COUNTY YOU ARE HEREBY NOTIFIED THAT the Board of Commissioners of Fulton County, Georgia and the governing authorities of the Cities of Alpharetta, Chattahoochee Hills, College Park, East Point, Fairburn, Hapeville, Johns Creek, Milton, Mountain Park, Palmetto, Roswell, Sandy Springs, South Fulton, and Union City hereby call a special election on November 3, 2026, (“Election”) to determine the question of whether a .75 percent (.75%) Transportation Special Purpose Local Option Sales and Use Tax (“TSPLOST”) should be implemented on April 1, 2027, and concluding March 31, 2032. All qualified voters desiring to vote in favor of the question propounded shall vote “Yes” and all qualified voters desiring to vote in opposition of the question propounded shall vote “No.” The ballots to be used in such election should have written or printed thereon substantially the following language or such other language as may be required by law: ( ) Yes ( ) No Shall an additional 0.75 percent salesand use taxbe imposed in that part of Fulton County, Georgia, outside of the City of Atlanta, for a period not to exceed fiveyears for the purpose of transportation improvements and congestion reduction. The last day to register to vote in this election shall be October 5, 2026. Early in-person voting begins October 13, 2026. The last day to submit an absentee ballot application shall be October 23, 2026. Those residents of the County qualified to vote at said election shall be determined in all respects in accordance with the election laws of the State of Georgia. This notice is given pursuant to a vote of the Fulton County Board Registration and Elections.
