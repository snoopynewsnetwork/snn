SB 202 Section 28 Notice is hereby given that advanced voting shall take place for the November 3rd, 2026, General & Special Elections for Jackson County registered voters at the Election Center location at 441 Gordon St, Jefferson, Georgia 30549 on the following days and times. TUESDAY-SATURDAY October 13th – October 17th from 9:00 a.m. until 5:00 p.m. MONDAY-SATURDAY October 19 th – October 24th from 9:00 a.m. until 5:00 p.m. MONDAY-FRIDAY October 26 th - October 30th from 9:00 a.m. until 5:00 p.m. A Drop Box will be available for absentee ballots at the Election Center (located inside) ONLY during all early voting hours as per SB 202 and will close on October 30th at 5PM. Ballots may be dropped off until 7PM on November 3 rd , 2026, to the election staff located at 441 Gordon Street in Jefferson, absentee ballots will NOT be received at the polling location on Election Day. Absentee Ballot Applications may be submitted for November 3 rd , 2026, General & Special Elections beginning Monday, August 17th until Friday, October 23rd Additional Early Voting satellite locations will open the last week of early voting at the following locations, dates, and times. No drop boxes will be available at these locations. Commerce Administrative Complex (Civic Center) 110 State Street, Commerce, GA 30529 MONDAY-FRIDAY October 26th - October 30th from 9:00 a.m. until 5:00 p.m. Braselton Civic Center 27 E Lake Dr, Braselton, GA 30517 MONDAY-FRIDAY October 26th - October 30th from 9:00 a.m. until 5:00 p.m. All early voting will end on October 30th at 5:00 p.m. Jennifer Logan Election and Voter Registration Director Jackson County PUBLIC NOTICE FOR EARLY ABSENTEE OPENING Pursuant to SEB RULE 183-1-14-0.7-.15, this is to notify the citizens of Jackson County that the Jackson County Board of Elections and Registration will begin to open the outer envelope of accepted absentee ballots in the November 3rd, 2026, General & Special Elections for Jackson County, remove the contents including the absentee ballot, and scan the absentee ballot using one or more scanners on the following schedule: Thursday, October 29th beginning at 10AM until complete Tuesday, November 3 rd beginning at 1PM until complete The process will continue until all accepted absentee ballots have been opened and scanned in accordance with this rule. However, no person shall tally or tabulate, estimate or cause the voting equipment to produce any tally or tabulation, partial or otherwise, of absentee votes cast until the time for the closing of the polls on November 3rd, 2026. The opening and scanning of the ballots for the November 3rd, 2026, General & Special Elections for Jackson County, will take place at: Jackson County Election Center 441 Gordon Street Jefferson, Georgia 30549 Open to the Public. Jackson County Board of Elections and Registration 706-367-6377 PUBLIC NOTICE FOR EARLY TABULATION Pursuant to O.C.G.A. 21-2-386(a)(3), this is to notify the citizens of Jackson County that the Jackson County Board of Elections and Registration will begin the early tabulation of absentee ballots cast on November 3 rd , 2026, General & Special Elections for Jackson County. This will be conducted at: Jackson County Election Center 441 Gordon Street Jefferson, GA 30549 Dates & Times: October 29 th , 2026, at 10AM November 3rd, 2026, at 1PM All ballots accepted will be opened and scanned and early voting equipment will be closed out. Open to the Public. Pursuant to O.C.G.A §21-2-374(b) and O.C.G.A §21-2-379.25(c) notice is hereby given that the logic and accuracy (L&A) testing of the voting equipment to be used in the November 3, 2026 General & Special Elections will begin at 9 a.m. on Wednesday, September 9th, 2026 (if all state files have been received), and will continue day to day from approximately until completed. September 9th from 2 p.m. until 5 p.m. September 10 th from 8:30 a.m. until 5p.m. September 11 th from 10:00 a.m. until completed. A General Election runoff, if needed, shall be held on December 1st, 2026. L&A for the December 1 st General Election runoff will commence on November 16, 2026 at 10 a.m. (if state files have been received) and will continue day to day until completed. November 16th from 10 a.m. until 5 p.m. November 17th from 8:30 a.m. until 5 p.m. (if needed) You may contact our main office to be advised of any L&A changes, updates or schedule changes or to see if L&A has concluded at 706-367-6377 The testing and equipment storage will be conducted at the: Board of Elections & Registration Office 441 Gordon Street Jefferson, GA 30549 The logic and accuracy (L&A) testing process is open to the public. Members of the public, representatives of the parties and bodies, and news media are entitled to be present during testing, but shall not interfere in any way with such testing.
