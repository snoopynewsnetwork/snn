Board of Elections & Registration Mission The Camden County Board of Elections and Registration conducts local, state, and federal elections in a fair and impartial manner to safeguard the integrity of the electoral process. Responsibilities Registering qualified citizens to vote Maintaining all voter records Providing verification of voters and voter information Assigning voters to appropriate Districts and Precincts Assisting with the training of Poll Officers Expediting all Absentee Voting Strategic Plan The Board of Elections & Registration Strategic Plan FY 2022-FY 2026 (PDF) includes the Mission, Vision, and Values for the Board.
