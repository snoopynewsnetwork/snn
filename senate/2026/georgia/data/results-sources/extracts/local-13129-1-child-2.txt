On Election Day, polls will be open from 7:00 a.m. until 7:00 p.m. Those who wish to vote on Election Day must do so at their assigned precinct. To find out where you vote on Election Day go to www.mvp.sos.ga.gov or call the Gordon County Board of Elections and Registration Office at 706-629-7781. The last day to request an absentee ballot by mail is May 8, 2026. REGISTERATION DEADLINE Persons who are not registered to vote and who desire to vote in the General Primary/Nonpartisan Election must do so by April 20, 2026. You can register to vote online at www.registertovote.sos.ga.gov or go to the Board of Elections and Registration Office during normal business hours. PUBLIC NOTICE - RISK LIMITING AUDIT FOR THE MAY 19, 2026 GENERAL PRIMARY/NONPARTISAN ELECTION Pursuant to O.C.G.A. 21-2-498, notice is hereby given that the Risk Limiting Audit for the May 19, 2026 General Primary/Nonpartisan Election will be held on May 29, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. in the Gordon County Board of Elections and Registration Office, located at 100 S Wall Street, Suite 1, Calhoun, Georgia 30701. PUBLIC NOTICE - COMPUTATION AND CANVASSING NOTICE FOR MAY 19,2026 GENERAL PRIMARY/NONPARTISAN ELECTION Pursuant to O.C.G.A. 21-2-492, notice is hereby given that the computation and canvassing of the returns of votes cast in the May 19, 2026 General Primary/Nonpartisan Election will take place following the close of the polls, in the office of the Gordon County Board of Elections and Registration Office, located at 100 S Wall Street, Suite 1, Calhoun, Georgia, beginning at 7:00 p.m. until all absentee ballots received by the close of polls, including those cast by advance voting, and all ballots cast on the day of the election have been counted and tabulated and the results of such tabulation released to the public and, then, continuing with provisional ballots as provided in Code Sections 21-2-418 and 21-2-419 and those absentee ballots as provided in subparagraph (a) (1) (G) of Code Section 21-2-386 from day to day until completed. All public is welcome. This is the 28 th , day of April 2026. Submitted by: Shea Hicks, Chairperson ~ Gordon County Board of Elections and Registration PUBLIC NOTICE - INTENT TO PROCESS ABSENTEE BALLOTS EARLY AND PUBLIC NOTICE OF INTENT TO TABULATE ABSENTEE BALLOTS EARLY FOR THE MAY 19, 2026 GENERAL PRIMARY/NONPARTISAN ELECTION Pursuant to O.C.G.A. 21-2-386(a)(2) and State Election Board Rule 183-1-14-.14 this is to notify the citizens of Gordon County that the Gordon County Board of Elections and Registration will begin the processing of absentee ballots cast in the May 19, 2026 General Primary/Nonpartisan Election at 4:30 p.m. on May 19, 2026. Said processing shall be open to the view of the public and will take place in the Gordon County Board of Elections and Registration Office located in the Gordon County Courthouse at 100 S. Wall Street, Suite 1, Calhoun, Georgia. Pursuant to O.C.G.A. 21-2-386(a)(3), this is to notify the citizens of Gordon County that the Gordon County Board of Elections and Registration will begin the tabulation of absentee ballots cast in the May 19, 2026 General Primary/Nonpartisan Election at 4:30 p.m. on May 19, 2026. Said tabulation shall take place in the Gordon County Board of Elections and Registration Office located in the Gordon County Courthouse at 100 S. Wall Street, Suite 1, Calhoun, Georgia. The process for tabulating absentee ballots prior to 7:00 p.m. shall be conducted in a manner to maintain the secrecy of all ballots and to protect the disclosure of any balloting information before 7:00 p.m. on Election Day. PUBLIC NOTICE - LOGIC AND ACCURACY TESTING FOR MAY 19, 2026 GENERAL PRIMARY/NONPARTISAN ELECTION In accordance with Georgia Election Code 21-2-379.6(c) and SEB Rule No. 183-1-12-.08, NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the logic and accuracy testing and preparation of the voting equipment to be used in the May 19, 2026, General Primary/Nonpartisan Election will begin at 1 p.m. on Monday, March 30, 2026. Such preparation and testing will continue from day to day until complete, excluding holidays and weekends. Representatives of political parties and bodies, news media, and the public shall be permitted to observe such testing. Said testing and preparation will be conducted at the Gordon County Board of Elections and Voter Registration Office located at the Gordon County Courthouse at 100 S Wall Street, Suite 1, Calhoun, Georgia. PUBLIC NOTICE – QUALIFYING DATES, TIMES, and FEES The Gordon County Board of Elections and Voter Registration announces qualifying information for local offices to be filled in the 2026 election cycle. Qualifying for the May 19, 2026 General Primary will be conducted by the Democratic Party and the Republican Party beginning at 9:00 a.m. on Monday, March 2, 2026, and will end at 12 Noon on Friday, March 6, 2026.
