District 1 Jane Scoggins, District 2 Justin Halford, District 4 Overview About the Board of Elections The Coweta County Board of Elections and Voter Registration was created by the Georgia General Assembly in 2017 through local legislation. It consists of five members appointed by the Coweta County Board of Commissioners to serve 4-year terms. It serves as the Superintendent of Elections for Coweta County.
