Attention: Please see the following statement for changes in the way our Election Night Reporting Application works and for assistance in interpreting the information contained within the application. Because of a recent change in State law, each precinct now contains 3 different Precinct Voting Sources. The names of the Precinct Voting Sources are (P) for Polling Place votes cast on Election Day, (M) for Mail-in Absentee Ballots, and (A) for votes cast during the Advance Voting period. When looking at the returns by precinct, if Fully Reported states YES, then all three Precinct Voting Sources have been counted and reported for that precinct. When looking at the returns by precinct, if Fully Reported states NO, then all three Precinct Voting Sources have not been counted and reported for that precinct. You may use the key located on the Web page (or refer to the example below) to determine which of the Precinct Voting Sources have been counted and reported and which have not yet been counted and reported for each Precinct.
