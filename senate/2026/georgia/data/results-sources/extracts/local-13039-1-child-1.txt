Home Government Departments A - G Elections & Registration Candidate Qualifying Qualifying Fees for 2026 Elections Qualifying Fees for 2026 Elections Camden County Offices to be Filled in 2026 The following Camden County offices are to be filled by election in 2026: By District Board of Commissioners District 2 District 4 Board of Education District 2 District 4 Pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 21-2-131(a) (1), the following qualifying fees were set by the Camden County Board of Commissioners at their January 20, 2026 meeting. Said qualifying fees shall be fixed at three percent (3%) of the annual base salary for the elected position to be filled in the 2026 election cycle. Qualifying Fees for 2026 Elections Elected Office Qualifying Fee Board of Commissioners-District 2 $306.00 Board of Commissioners-District 4 $306.00 Board of Education-District 2 $108.00 Board of Education-District 4 $108.00 Qualifying for the offices listed above will begin at 9:00 a.m. on March 2, 2026, and will end at 12:00 p.m. on March 6, 2026. The General Primary will be held on May 19, 2026, and the General Election will be held on November 3, 2026.
