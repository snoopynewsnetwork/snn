Charlie Lord , Chairman, Appointing Authority: Superior Court Chief Judge Term Expires: December 31, 2028 Mary "Teeny" Allison , Appointing Authority: Republican Party Term Expires: December 31, 2028 Priscilla Baker , Appointing Authority: Democratic Party Term Expires: December 31, 2028 Pam Powell , Appointing Authority: Republican Party Term Expires: December 31, 2028 Johnny Ellington-Watson , Appointing Authority: Democratic Party Term Expire: December 31, 2028 Overview Board Profile : The Board of Elections and Registration is responsible for registering citizens to vote, maintaining voter registration records, conducting all county elections, mailing out absentee ballots, training poll workers, and keeping abreast of all state laws pertaining to elections and voter registrations. In addition, this Board also conducts city elections for Winder, Auburn, Bethlehem, Carl, and Statham on a contractual basis whereby each city pays for the cost of their city elections.
