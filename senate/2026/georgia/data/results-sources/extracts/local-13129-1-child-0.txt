Elections Posted on June 08, 2026 NOTICE OF SPECIAL CALLED RECOUNT OF ELECTION RESULTS NOTICE OF SPECIAL CALLED RECOUNT OF ELECTION RESULTS Notice is hereby given that at the direction of the Secretary of State pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 21-2-495, the Gordon County Board of Elections and Registration has scheduled a recount proceeding for the Republican Public Service Commission (PSC) District 3 race. This race was part of the Republican Party General Primary Election which occurred on following certification of the May 19, 2026. The recount is being conducted in all Georgia Counties. Testing for the recount followed immediately by the recount will begin on Tuesday, June 23, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. and will continue each day until completed, but no later than Friday, June 26, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. The recount will take place at the Gordon County Board of Elections and Registration Office, 100 S Wall Street, Calhoun, Georgia 30701. Pursuant to Georgia law, the affected political party may designate two representatives to be present during the recount, and each affected candidate may be present in person or by representative. The recount proceeding shall be open to the public; however, members of the public will be required to remain within designated viewing areas and comply with all instructions necessary to maintain the orderly conduct of the recount. The recount is being conducted upon formal receipt of a written recount request from candidate Branson Martin following certification of the election results because the margin of victory was less than one half of one present. The recount will be conducted in accordance with Georgia election law and applicable State Election Board rules and regulations.
