The Elections & Voter Registration Department, with oversight from the Habersham County Board of Elections and Voter Registration , enforces strict adherence to all current Federal, State and Local laws as well as to State Election Board Rules concerning registering to vote and voting. We serve all residents of Habersham County in election-related matters. Check out our newest voter outreach documents below. Our newest brochure has information on the upcoming November 3, 2026 General Election: The Community Guide covers the basics of voter registration and elections. | New Window We've also compiled a list of Frequently Asked Questions. | New Window My Voter Page: The Habersham County Board of Elections & Voter Registration Office recommends to all county residents that they utilize Georgia's My Voter Page. It provides a central hub to access vital information for voting in Georgia. This includes, among other things, your voter registration status, your polling location, sample ballots for upcoming elections, and locations for early/advanced voting. Visit and bookmark it at: www.mvp.sos.ga.gov. | New Window Click below to watch a brief instructional video on how to use My Voter Page. | New Window Cancel your Voter Registration: Voters who; are moving out of Georgia, wish to no longer be registered, or need to cancel a deceased loved one may use a new online portal to let us know. www.cancelmyregistration.sos.ga.gov For additional information regarding Georgia's Elections visit the Secretary of State's Election website by clicking here . OPEN RECORDS REQUESTS Habersham County will work diligently in fulfilling requests for documents in accordance with the Open Records Act. The below form, once completed, may be emailed to boc_records@habershamga.com or mailed to: Habersham County Board of Commissioner' ATTN: Open Records Request 130 Jacob's Way Suite 301 Clarkesville, GA 30523 Open Records Request Form | New Window Due to an increasing number of malicious emails and attachments, we ask that you provide your name, contact information, and a brief summary of what you are requesting in the body of emails when submitting Open Records Request forms to any records officer of Habersham County. Emails that do not provide enough information, or that are suspect of being malicious will not be responded to and will be reported to the Habersham County IT Department.
