If you miss this deadline, you will need to wait until the next election to vote. You may verify your registration status at the Georgia Secretary of State’s My Voter Page . If you met the deadline but do not see your information reflected there, please contact our office directly at Elections.VoterRegistration@fultoncountyga.gov to confirm your registration status, as certain regulations affect the processing of voter registration applications during an active election. Confirm Eligibility To register to vote in the State of Georgia, you must be: United States citizen Resident of Georgia At least 17½ to register (must be 18 to vote) Not currently serving a sentence for a felony of moral turpitude Not legally declared mentally incompetent Register To Vote To register online, you may visit the Georgia Secretary of State’s Online Voter Registration sit e . You must have a valid Georgia driver’s license or a Georgia Department of Driver Services (DDS)–issued ID. If you do not have one, you may print the application from the site and mail it or deliver to your Georgia county residence office. If you reside in Fulton County, you may visit one of our offices noted below to register to vote. Fulton County Voter Registration & Elections Offices: Elections Hub and Operation Center 5600 Campbellton Fairburn Road Union City, GA 30213 Phone: (404) 612-7020 Fulton County Government Center 130 Peachtree St, Ste 2186 Atlanta, GA 30303 North Fulton Service Center 7741 Roswell Rd, Rm 251 Sandy Springs, GA 30350 South Fulton Service Center 5600 Stonewall Tell Rd, Rm 105 South Fulton, GA 30349 Once you register to vote, you should receive a precinct card via postal mail within 3 to 4 weeks. You are not required to bring your precinct card to cast your ballot, but you must have acceptable photo ID. Acceptable forms of identification include: A valid Georgia driver’s license, in physical format, issued by the Department of Driver Services (DDS), even if expired. A Georgia DDS interim (temporary) license. A Georgia State-issued ID card, including: Student ID card from a Georgia public college or university (Note: Private institution IDs are not accepted) MARTA transit ID card. A valid U.S. passport. A valid Military ID card. A valid Tribal ID card. Georgia Weapons Carry License. A valid Employee ID card issued by any branch, department, agency, or entity of the US Government, Georgia, a Georgia County, Municipality, Board, Authority, or other Entity of Georgia. A valid Fulton County Georgia Voter ID Card (VIC). To receive a free Georgia Voter ID please visit our webpage . If you need assistance completing your voter registration information or find any discrepancies on your precinct card, please contact Fulton County Voter Registration Division at 404-612-3816 or Elections.VoterRegistration@fultoncountyga.gov . Where do i go to register to vote? The Voter Registration Division has four locations to register to vote in person: Fulton County Government Center , 130 Peachtree Street SW, Suite 2186, Atlanta, Georgia 30303 ​ South Fulton Service Center, 5600 Stonewall Tell Road, Room 105, College Park, Georgia 30349 ​ North Fulton Service Center, 7741 Roswell Road, Room 251, Sandy Springs, GA 30350 ​ Elections Hub and Operation Center (EHOC) , 5600 Campbellton Fairburn Road, Union City, GA 30213 After I have registered to vote how long will it take to receive my voter precinct card? A Voter Precinct card will be mailed to you from Fulton County 3 – 4 weeks after you register. Do you know where I can find a voter registration application? You can complete or download a Voter Registration Application at the Georgia Secretary of State Online Voter Registration Webpage . You may also visit one of our election offices to receive an application. How do I obtain a Precinct Card? You will only receive a precinct card when you register to vote for the first time, change your name or address. Please visit https://www.mvp.sos.ga.gov/MVP/mvp.do to print a copy of your precinct card. I am a college student. Can I register to vote with my school address? Please visit the College Student Voting information page for additional information. When is the last day to register to vote? Please visit our Election Key Dates webpage for deadlines pertaining to upcoming elections. I updated my voter registration with my new address, but it is not reflected in the system What do I do? Please contact the Voter Registration Office at 404-612-3816 for help to verify your address change. If I do not have a Georgia Driver License or ID, How do i obtain a voter ID card and where? Please visit Get a Voter ID for information on how to receive a FREE Georgia Voter ID. If I go to the polls and I am told that I am not registered to vote what should I do? Before heading to the poll, you should check your voter registration status GA My Voter Page or call the Voter Registration Office at 404-612-3816 to verify your status. If you are at the poll, ask for a provisional ballot, which will allow us time to review your voter information in an effort to cast your ballot.
