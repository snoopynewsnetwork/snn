Georgia My Voter Page Useful Resources Georgia Election Code Georgia General Assembly Contact 2025-2026 Elected Officials Georgia Secretary of State Laws, Rules & Regulations Open Records Requests Public Notices About The Department of Voter Registrations & Elections aims to enable all eligible citizens of Forsyth County to exercise their right to register and vote under the Constitution of the United States; to provide for the security and integrity of all elections in accordance with the Georgia Election Code and State Election Board Rules; to encourage voter participation; to continue providing excellent customer service to voters, candidates and the media; and to maintain public confidence through education of the voting process and laws governing elections.
