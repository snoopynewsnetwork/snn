                                                                   2026 ELECTION CALENDAR
                                                                                                           Revised 08/06/2026

This edition of the 2026 Idaho Election Calendar is subject to amendatory changes during the 2026
session of the Idaho Legislature. If changes occur, an updated version will be published.

   JAN. 2             Election Register Examination: Deadline for county clerks to examine the election register,
                      from the Nov. 4 election, and note challenges. [§34-432(1), Idaho Code]

   JAN. 15            Last Day for County Commissioners to Establish Election Precincts: Deadline for county
                      commissioners to establish the number of precincts and their boundaries for the following
                      year. After precincts have been established, county clerks shall provide maps of all precincts
                      to the secretary of state. [§34-301, Idaho Code]

   JAN. 16            Early Voting Security Plan: Deadline for county clerks to submit their ballot security plan to the
                      secretary of state. (*NOTE: The Early Voting Security Plan must be submitted at least 30 days
                      prior to implementing an early voting plan.) [§34-1013, Idaho Code]

   JAN. 20            County Clerk Deliver Precinct Committeeman List to Central Committees: County Clerks
                      shall deliver in writing a list of precinct committeemen who were elected at the last primary
                      election, or who have since been appointed as precinct committeemen. [§34-502, Idaho Code]

   FEB. 1             Last Day for Chairs of County Central Committees to Certify Precinct Committeemen:
                      Deadline for the chairman of the county central committee to certify precinct committeemen
                      information. [§34-502, Idaho Code]

   FEB. 23 – 27       Candidate Filing Period for Federal, State, Legislative, and Judicial Offices: Candidates may
                      file declarations of candidacy with the Secretary of State beginning at 8 a.m. on Monday and
                      must be filed by no later than 5:00 p.m. on the last day of the filing period. [§34-704, Idaho Code]

   FEB. 27 – MAR. 6   Publish Notification of Candidate Filing Deadline: County clerk must publish a notification of
                      the candidate filing deadline for all taxing districts for which officers will be elected in the May
                      19th election — during this period. The notice should appear in the official newspaper of the
                      political subdivision. [§34-1405 Idaho Code]

   MAR. 2             Secretary of State to Certify Candidates with Parties: The secretary of state shall notify the
                      legislative district and state central committees of the party candidates for the primary
                      election. [§34-706, Idaho Code]

   MAR. 2 – 13        Candidate Filing Period for County Offices, Precinct Committeemen, and Nonpartisan
                      Candidates: Candidates may file declarations of candidacy with the county clerk or the clerk
                      of the political subdivision beginning at 8 a.m. on Monday and must be filed by no later than
                      5:00 p.m. on the last day of the filing period. [§34-704, Idaho Code]

   MAR. 6             Write-In Candidate Filing Deadline for Federal, State, and Legislative Offices: Deadline for
                      write-in candidates to file a declaration of intent with the Secretary of State.
                      [§34-702A, Idaho Code]

   MAR. 6             Withdrawal Deadline for Federal, State, and Legislative Offices: Last day to withdraw from the
                      primary election. [§34-717, Idaho Code]

   MAR. 13            Ballot Question Submission Deadline: Political subdivisions must submit ballot language (to
                      the county clerk) for any bond, levy, initiative, referendum, or other question — to be placed on
                      the ballot — for the May 19 election. [§34-106, Idaho Code]

    MAR. 13   Recall Notification Deadline: Deadline to notify the county clerk if a recall election is

              form with the county clerk. [§34-411A. Idaho Code]

    MAR. 13   Investigation of Candidate Qualifications for Federal, State, Legislative, and Judicial
              Offices: Deadline to file complaint [§34-219, Idaho Code]

    MAR. 16   County Clerks to Certify Candidates with Parties: The county clerk shall notify the county
              central committees of the party candidates for the primary election. [§34-706, Idaho Code]

    MAR. 20   Clerks of Political Subdivisions to Certify Nonpartisan Nominees: Deadline for clerks of
              political subdivisions to certify candidates to be placed on the ballot. [§34-1404, Idaho Code]

    MAR. 20   Write-In Candidate Filing Deadline for County Offices and Precinct Committeemen:
              Deadline for write-in candidates to file declarations of intent with the county clerks.
              [§34-702A, Idaho Code]

    MAR. 20   Post-election Audit Directive to be Issued: The Secretary of State shall issue a directive
              outlining the Postelection Audit procedures for the Primary. [§34-1203A(2), Idaho Code]

    MAR. 20   Withdrawal Deadline for County Offices, Precinct Committeemen, and Nonpartisan
              Offices: Last day to withdraw with the county clerk or the clerk of the political subdivision
              for the primary election. [§34-717, §34-1405A, Idaho Code]

    MAR. 20   Secretary of State to Provide Sample Form to Clerks: Last day for the Secretary of State to
              provide the sample form for the primary election ballots. [§34-712, Idaho Code]

    MAR. 27   Investigation of Candidate Qualifications for County Offices, Precinct Committeemen,
              and Nonpartisan Offices: Deadline to file complaint [§34-219, Idaho Code]

    MAR. 27   Early Voting Security Plan: Deadline for county clerks to submit their ballot security plan
              to the secretary of state. (*NOTE: The Early Voting Security Plan must be submitted at least
              30 days prior to implementing an early voting plan. If a plan was submitted and approved
              earlier in the year, and has not changed, it need not be resubmitted.) [§34-1013, Idaho Code]

    MAR. 30   Absentee Print Deadline: Deadline for county clerks to print absentee ballots.
              [Secretary of State Directive 2015-1]

    APR. 1    Deadline for County Commissioners to Designate Mail Ballot Precincts: Last day for
              county commissioners to designate mail ballot precincts.) [§34-308, Idaho Code]

    APR. 3    Deadline for Absentee Ballot Delivery Witnesses: Last day for political parties to supply
              the county clerk with absentee ballot delivery witnesses. [§34-1003(5), Idaho Code]

    APR. 3    Mail Absentee Ballots: County clerks must begin mailing absentee ballots – to voters who
              have requested absentee ballots – for the primary election. [§34-1003(3), Idaho Code and
              Secretary of State Directive 2015-1]

    APR. 17   Deadline to Recommend Election Judges: Last day for precinct committeemen to
              recommend judges for the upcoming election to the county clerks. [§34-303, Idaho Code]

           APR. 17   Deadline for County Commissioners to Designation Polling Locations: Last day for county
                     commissioners to confirm the polling locations for the primary elections.
                     [§34-302, Idaho Code]

           APR. 24   Mail Ballot Precincts: County clerks may begin mailing ballots to voters located in
                     designated mail ballot precincts — for the primary election. [§34-308(3), Idaho Code]

           APR. 27   Early Voting May Begin: For the primary election in those counties that choose to conduct
                     early voting. [§34-1012, Idaho Code]

           MAY 1     Petition Signature Deadline: Last day for petitions in circulation to submit petition
                     signatures to the county clerks. This date is dependent upon when the 18-month
                     circulation period began. [§34-1802(2), Idaho Code]

           MAY 5     Mail Ballot Precincts: Deadline for county clerks to complete mailing ballots to voters in
                     designated mail ballot precincts – for the primary election. [§34-308, Idaho Code]

           MAY 7     Publish First Election Notification: Deadline for county clerk to publish the first notice of
                     election – in the county’s official newspaper(s) – for the primary election. (If possible, the
                     notice should appear in at least two newspapers published in the county. If not possible, it
                     should be published in one newspaper published or circulated in the county.)
                     [§34-602 and §34-1406, Idaho Code]

           MAY 7     Poll Watchers: Deadline for parties, candidates, or pro and con representatives to submit
                     a written request (to the county clerk) for the purpose of authorizing watchers to observe
                     at polling places – for the primary election. [§34-304, Idaho Code]

           MAY 8     Preregistration Deadline: Voter registrations must be received by the county clerk by 5
                     p.m. local time - for the primary election. [§34-408 and §34-410, Idaho Code]

           MAY 8     Absentee Ballot Application Deadline: Applications must be received by the county clerk
                     by 5 p.m. local time - for the primary election. [§34-1002(7), Idaho Code]

           MAY 14    Publish Second Election Notification: Deadline for county clerks to publish the second
                     notice of election along with a facsimile sample ballot – for the primary election. (If
                     possible, the notice should appear in at least two newspapers published in the county.
                     If not possible, it should be published in one newspaper published or circulated in the
                     county.) [§34-602 and §34-1406, Idaho Code]

           MAY 14    Emergency Absentee Ballot: County clerks may receive emergency absentee ballot
                     applications through 5 p.m. on May 18 from those who cannot be present for voting due to
                     hospitalization, if hospitalization occurred after 5 p.m. on May 8. [§34-1002A, Idaho Code]

           MAY 15    In-Person Absentee/Early Voting Ends: At 5 p.m. – for the primary election. [§34-1002(7) and
                     §34-1012, Idaho Code]

           MAY 19    Election Day: Polling places — and the county clerk’s office — to be open 8 a.m.–8 p.m.
                     Qualified individuals may register and vote at their designated polling place on election
                     day. [§34-211, §34-408A, and §34-1101, Idaho Code]

           MAY 19    Absentee Ballot Return Deadline: Voted absentee ballots must be received — by the
                     county clerk — by 8 p.m. for the May 19 election. [§34-1005, Idaho Code]

           MAY 20    Voter Registration Reopens [§34-408, Idaho Code]

    MAY 28         County Canvass: Deadline for the board of county commissioners to meet and conduct
                   the canvass – of the primary election. [§34-1205 and §34-1410, Idaho Code]

    MAY 28         County Election Certification: Deadline for county clerks to certify the results and issue
                   certificates for the primary election. (*This date may vary; it must be done immediately
                   after canvass.) [§34-1208, Idaho Code]

    MAY 29         Post-election Audit Selection: The Secretary of State shall conduct the randomized
                   selection for the counties to be audited for the primary election. [§34-1203A, Idaho Code]

    MAY 29         Central Committees to Elect Officers: Last day for county central committees of each
                   political party to meet and elect party officers. [§34-502, Idaho Code]

    MAY 30         Legislative District Committees to Elect Officers: Last day for legislative district
                   committees of each political party to meet and elect party officers. [§34-503, Idaho Code]

    JUNE 5         Election Certificates to Precinct Committeemen: The county clerk shall issue certificates
                   of election to the precinct committeemen of each political party who receive the highest
                   number of votes, if they received a minimum of 5 votes. [§34-1208, Idaho Code]

    JUNE 9         State Canvass: Deadline for the state board of canvassers to meet and conduct the
                   canvass – of the primary election. [§34-1211, Idaho Code]

    JUNE 9         Secretary of State to Issue Certificates: The Secretary of State shall issue certificates of
                   nomination to the political party candidates of each party. (*This date may vary; it must
                   be done immediately after canvass.) [§34-1214, Idaho Code]

    JUNE 17        Recount Applications for County and Nonpartisan Candidates and Local Ballot
                   Measures: Deadline – for nonpartisan candidates and supporters/opponents of local
                   ballot measures – to apply to the county clerk for a recount of the primary election.
                   Applications for a recount of county contests and ballot measures must be filed with the
                   Attorney General. (*This date may vary; it must be done within 20 days after the county
                   canvass.) [§34-2301, Idaho Code]

    JUNE 17        Election Disputes: Deadline for electors to contest the results of the primary election.
                   (*This date may vary; complaints must be filed in the proper court within 20 days of the
                   canvass.)[§34-2008, Idaho Code]

    JUNE 29        Recount Applications for Federal and State Candidates and Statewide Ballot Measures:
                   Deadline to apply to the Attorney General for a recount. (*This date may vary; it must be
                   done within 20 days after the state canvass.) [§34-2301, Idaho Code]

    JUNE 30        County Clerks to Verify Petition Signatures: Last day for county clerks to verify the
                   initiative petition signatures submitted. [§34-1802(3), Idaho Code]

    JULY 17        Election Register Examination: Deadline for county clerks to examine the election register,
                   from the primary election, and note challenges. [§34-432(1), Idaho Code]

                   notification of the candidate filing deadline for all taxing districts for which officers will be
                   elected in the Nov. 3 election — during this period. The notice should appear in the official
                   newspaper of the political subdivision. [§34-1405, Idaho Code]
