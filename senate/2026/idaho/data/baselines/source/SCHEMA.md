# Idaho partisan baseline source and calculation contract

All paths below are relative to `data/baselines/` unless explicitly stated. The only outputs outside that directory are `../../county-partisanship.md` (relative from `data/baselines/` to the state report) and `../county-baselines.csv`. Originals and peer outputs are read-only. No Git operation is part of this package.

## Reproduction

Run `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 senate/2026/idaho/data/baselines/reproduce.py` from the repository root. It runs extraction, supplementary source controls/PDF rendering, reciprocal comparison, calculation, synthetic live cases, report rendering, validation, asset sealing and a final read-only asset check. Standard Python and Poppler (`pdftotext`, `pdftoppm`) suffice offline. `requests` is needed only for separate public acquisition. No runtime dependency or cache tree is retained.

`acquire.py` retains complete HTTP entities, requested/final URLs, redirects, response headers/status, UTC retrieval and raw/stored hashes/sizes for twelve current authority/statistics/rule responses. `acquire_references.py` retains the actual distinct primary write-in PDF and independently fetches the Census reference, reusing identical original Census bytes once. Reacquisition can produce new page clocks/bodies and is not required for offline reproduction. Original downloaded response bodies are authoritative evidence, not regenerated from extracted text.

`source/reused-originals.json` records complete original receipts and references to the six historical artifacts retained once in `../news/`. `source/archive-members.json` inventories all original ZIP members, CRC32, byte lengths and SHA256. Read referenced gzip bytes, verify stored hash, decompress and verify raw length/hash. XLSX is itself an original ZIP; its internal worksheets, shared strings, relationships, formulas and unrelated offices remain intact. No truncation, byte splitting, rescaling or simplification occurs. All stored artifacts must be **strictly less than40,000,000 bytes**.

`source/additional-reused-originals.json` pins the raw Census county-code list, Senate class pages, official CFR PDF, national FEC/House Clerk PDFs and bounded Twin Falls sample ballot. Its `relative_path` is relative to this directory. National original metadata and headers remain at explicitly recorded paths under `senate/2026/`. `source/independent-reference-receipts.json` supplies the independent Census receipt and the actual2022 primary write-in receipt. Twin Falls full parent receipts remain in `../news/coordinator-oct19-recheck/acquisition-ballot-stage-v3.json`, with direct parent406 outcomes distinguished from successful web-reader link observations. Complete printed sample PDF is referenced once, separately from its independently rendered page PNG/text derivative.

`source/acquisition.json` separates server clocks, Last-Modified and retrieval time. Archive Last-Modified is not a canvass certification date. The eCFR direct HTTP200 challenge is retained as a failed substantive route; current web-reader text and the successful official2025 CFR original are the timezone evidence. No measured November publication cadence is claimed. Primary/current/historical display stages do not become general-election returns.

## Baseline and scenario columns

`../county-baselines.csv` is a440-row long table:44 counties × ten pair views. `native-baselines.csv.gz` is the corresponding complete native long table. `complete-candidate-baselines.csv.gz` retains each actual candidate on every native unit and county control, separate from pair views.

`pair_id`, `anchor_contest`, year, scope, source_unit_id and unit_id identify the view. Candidate_A/B and party_A/B are named, with IND/LIB preserved. `anchor_A_votes`/`anchor_B_votes` are **historical anchor counts** even in a current named scenario; they are not votes for a new2026 candidate. `basis` explicitly labels those transported assumptions. `all_candidate_complete` gates `all_candidate_votes`; `all_candidate_known_sum` never fills nulls. `pair_votes` is A+B only when both are known. `pair_participation=(A+B)/all_valid`; `A_share=A/(A+B)`; pair margin is100(2share−1); all-vote margin is100(A−B)/all_valid. Zero pool yields undefined share. `other_votes` never renames other candidates Democratic.

The three `current-*` named pairs are Achilles/Risch, Fleming/Risch and Loesby/Risch. Each conditionally transports2024 Harris/Trump shares and pair participation. `other_candidate_fractions_json` explicitly assigns half residual candidate volume to each of the two other printed opposition candidates. This is an arbitrary scenario assumption, not inferred support, a coalition, ballot certification, or a candidate effect. Historical Fleming/Risch and Cleveland/Crapo views retain actual independent votes separately.

`scenario-settings.json` declares historical low2022 Senate590890, intermediate/base2020 Senate859827 and high2024 president905057 all-candidate volumes and observed county mixes. Priority repeat uses2024; anchor-observed-repeat uses each preference anchor’s own county totals. For county c and preference anchor p, `T_c=V_volume,c × (A_p,c+B_p,c)/V_p,c`. Volume and preference anchors are separate. Historical2024 native sensitivity uses the2024 within-parent all-candidate proportions to distribute historical county volume, then each native pair participation. No cross-year native matching is performed. Native observed repeats use their observed pair counts and retain incomplete/zero pools.

`state-scenarios.csv` contains160 tie/cushion scenario rows; `unit-targets.csv.gz` contains63,518 contributing/selected-unit rows. Scenario IDs encode pair, volume setting, scope and target. County and native scope rows are alternatives, never summed together. Pair weights sum to one over the whole declared scope; all-candidate weights use the separate declared V. Native historical known-sum V is labeled as such where individual candidate totals are incomplete.

Unbounded tie shift is `(B−A)/(2(A+B))`. A+1 pair margin adds0.005 share before clipping. The bounded solver uses100 bisection iterations on δ∈[−1,1] for `sum T_i(2clip(s_i+δ)−1)=gT`. Zero-weight units require no preference; an unknown positive pool/preference makes the state scenario unknown instead of dropping it. Target beyond±T is impossible and preserves the unbounded shift. Already-winning signed negative shifts are retained separately from minimum nonnegative improvement. All reported native/county weights, contributions, gains and settings use these same formulas.

Per-unit target fields separate raw uniform target, bounded target, share change pp, pair margin pp, target net, net gain and unsaturated +1pp-share derivative. `bounded_gain_for_1pp_share` separately evaluates the actual local increment after clipping at1. Selected-only required share holds all other units at unchanged scenario preferences/volumes, with the exact fixed-other net recorded. Out-of-range required shares remain unbounded with feasible/already-secured/impossible/no-positive/unknown statuses. The command-line selector uses observed pair counts; it does not pretend to change a historical volume setting. `--others-shift-pp` explicitly clips other preferences, and `--target-margin-pp` uses final pair volume. Fractional targets are expectations, never rounded historical votes.

State full-field checks preserve every historical other candidate or explicit current other allocation. A plurality win requires A strictly exceed **every** opponent, not just B. Pair A+1 can still fail; the historical2020 Fleming/Risch example leaves Jordan ahead. Incomplete full-field assumptions remain unknown. Ties use Idaho’s coin-toss rule rather than an automatic win.

## Conditional live-remainder interface

`live_remainder.py INPUT.json` accepts A/B names; `counted` mapping of every named candidate to fixed nonnegative counts; and `remaining_units`. Each unit has a unique id, `valid_candidate_pool` (null if unknown), pair_fraction∈[0,1], A_share_of_pair (explicitly clipped), and other_allocation mapping of named nonpair candidates to nonnegative fractions totaling1 whenever a nonpair pool exists. A0 remaining pool needs no preference. Pool means valid candidate votes, not registered voters, ballots, or target turnout minus counted votes. The user must supply any ballot-to-valid-vote conversion separately.

Optional selected_unit holds all other remaining units at their stated shares. `target_margin_pp` defaults0; `target_denominator` is `pair` or `all_candidate`. Final pair denominator is countedA+countedB+remainingPair. Final all-candidate denominator is all counted votes+remainingValid. Output retains counted values, raw/clipped preferences, remaining allocations, projected full field, final denominators, pair/all margins and separate pair-target/legal-plurality thresholds. A legal tie threshold must be exceeded for a strict win. No simulated probability is produced.

Unknown remaining pool/preferences are not zero. Negative/nonfinite counts or pools are invalid. No positive pair pool exposes observed/fixed-assumption outcomes without division. Saved14 synthetic cases cover unknown/no-pool/negative, impossible/secured, low/high clipping, all versus pair denominators, no-pair pool, a third candidate winning, already-winning negative required shift and selected-unit constraints. These are arithmetic QA, not calibration or actual2026 assumptions.

`validation.json` records executable checks and remaining research gates. `asset-manifest.json` covers every owned file except its own recursive hash, plus read-only original/source references with stored/raw hashes and byte bounds. Validation is written before manifest sealing; `validate.py --verify-assets` checks without rewriting the sealed report. No approval of historical/current precinct geometry or statewide/later eligible write-ins follows.
