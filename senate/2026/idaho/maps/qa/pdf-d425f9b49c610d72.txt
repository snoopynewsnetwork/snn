NOVEMBER ELECTION INFORMATION 
November 3, 2026 General Election 
Election Day Polling Places will be open from 8 AM to 8 PM on November 3rd  
Ballot Questions  
• 
Ballot Question Submission Deadline August 28th : Political subdivisions must submit ballot 
language to the county clerk for any bond, levy, initiative, referendum, or other question to be 
placed on the ballot for the November 3rd Election – Idaho Code 34-106 
 
Voter Pre-Registration Deadline 
• 
October 23, 2026 Deadline- Voter Registration must be received by the county clerk for the 
November 3rd  Election. The deadline is 5 PM for paper applications or until midnight for online 
applications.  Voters can register to vote at the polls on Election Day.  
 
• 
Go to https://voteidaho.gov/ to register to vote.  
 
Absentee Voting 
• 
To request an absentee ballot and check the status of your request please 
visit: https://voteidaho.gov/  or you can download a 2026 ABSENTEE REQUEST FORM. 
 
• 
The request can be dropped off, mailed, emailed, or faxed to the Elections Office. The deadline to 
request a mail-in absentee ballot is October 23rd at 5:00 PM.   
 
Early Voting   
• 
Early voting will be available at the County Elections Office starting October 19, 2026 
through October 30, 2026. Early voting hours are Monday through Friday from 9AM to 
5PM.   
 
Sample Ballots 
• 
Sample Ballots will be available the last week of September for the November 3, 2026 Election. 
 
Logic and Accuracy Test  
• 
The Logic and Accuracy test is open to the public and will be on Wednesday, October 28, 2026 at 
10:00 AM. Location will be in the Elections Office at the Bonner County Administration Building 
located at 1500 Hwy 2 in Sandpoint.
