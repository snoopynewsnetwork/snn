MAY ELECTION INFORMATION 
May 19, 2026 Primary Election 
Election Day Polling Places will be open from 8 AM to 8 PM on May 19th   
Ballot Questions  
• 
Ballot Question Submission Deadline March 13th : Political subdivisions must submit ballot 
language to the county clerk for any bond, levy, initiative, referendum, or other question to be 
placed on the ballot for the May 19th Election – Idaho Code 34-106 
 
Voter Pre-Registration Deadline 
• 
May 8, 2026 Deadline- Voter Registration must be received by the county clerk for the May 19th 
Election. The deadline is 5 PM for paper applications or until midnight for the online 
applications.  Voters can register to vote at the polls on Election Day.  
 
• 
Go to https://voteidaho.gov/ to register to vote.  
 
Absentee Voting 
• 
To request an absentee ballot and check the status of your request please 
visit: https://voteidaho.gov/  or you can download a 2026 ABSENTEE REQUEST FORM. 
 
• 
The request can be dropped off, mailed, emailed, or faxed to the Elections Office.  The deadline 
to request a mail-in absentee ballot for the May 19th Election is May 8th at 5:00 PM.   
 
Early Voting   
• 
Early voting will be available at the County Elections Office starting May 4, 2026 through 
May 15, 2026. Early voting hours are Monday through Friday from 9AM to 5PM.   
 
Sample Ballots 
• 
Sample Ballots will be available the first week of April for the May 19, 2026 Election. 
 
Logic and Accuracy Test  
• 
The Logic and Accuracy test is open to the public and will be on Wednesday, May 13, 2026 at     
10:00 AM. Location will be in the Elections Office, Suite 124 at the Bonner County Administration 
Building located at 1500 Hwy 2 in Sandpoint.
