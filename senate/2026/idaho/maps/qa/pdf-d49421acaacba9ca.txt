Road Addressing and Naming Fee Schedule 
WHEREAS, Idaho Code§ 67-6518 provides the authority for the Board of County Commissioners to adopt
development standards for roadways, streets, lanes, bicycleways, pedestrian walkways, rights-of-ways,
grades, alignments and intersections and other public and private development, street numbers and
names, house numbers; and
WHEREAS, Idaho Code § 31-870 and Idaho Code § 63-1311 provide that the Boards of County
Commissioners of each county in Idaho may collect fees for services provided; and
WHEREAS, The Bonner County GIS Department has demonstrated a need to establish a new
addressing and road naming fee schedule; and
WHEREAS, Bonner County advertised notice in the Daily Bee for a public hearing on June 17, 2020 as
per Idaho Code 63-1311A;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the Bonner County Board of Commissioners, that the
following fees and requirements are hereby established:
