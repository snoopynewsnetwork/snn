The [SOS county directory](https://www.sos.nm.gov/voting-and-elections/county-clerk-information/) supplies the actual current clerk labels, contact fields, printed website labels, literal hrefs and blanks. Clerk, canvassing and publication roles differ. Under NMSA 1-13-1 the clerk prepares the county canvass report; the commissioners are the ex officio canvassing board unless their polling-place resolution designates the board of registration. The board approves the report and certificate; the clerk transmits it. An exact current local designation was not independently proved for any of the 33 parents, so that field is null in every record. A clerk webpage, a vendor host or an unofficial result screen is not itself a canvass approval. [Complete current code and section contexts](data/results-sources/operative-code-sections.json).

The state canvassing board comprises the Governor, Secretary of State and Chief Justice under state Constitution Article V §2. The Secretary of State prepares the state report; the board approves the canvass and declares statewide/multicounty results under 1-13-15/16. For U.S. Senate, 1-15-13 expressly applies the state-officer casting/counting/return/canvass process and requires transmission of the result to the President of the U.S. Senate. Publication by SOS or a county-owned host remains a separate, source-specific role. [State Constitution original receipt](data/results-sources/receipts/nm-state-constitution-frame-pdf.json), [Election Code original receipt](data/results-sources/receipts/nm-elections-code-frame-pdf.json).

### Winner, tie, failure, recount and contest
