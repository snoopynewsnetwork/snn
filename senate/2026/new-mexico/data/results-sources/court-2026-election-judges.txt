14         WHEREAS, the Election Code requires the assignment of district judges to

20   election contests and other Election Code proceedings that may arise as a result of

21   the upcoming general election; and

23   Judiciary under the Constitution of the State of New Mexico, N.M. Const. art. VI,

24   § 3, and the authority of the Chief Justice to designate district judges under N.M.

11         IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that, except for the Election Code proceedings

12   brought pursuant to the statutes or rule set forth above, the Chief Judge in each

13   judicial district shall appoint district judges to carry out other election day, election

14   night, and post-election duties required of judges under the Election Code;

15         IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that any district judge designated under this

16   order who is assigned to preside over an election contest or other Election Code

17   proceeding concerning or affecting a retention or contested election for that judge’s

18   position shall recuse from presiding in the matter, and the Chief Justice shall

19   designate another judge to preside over the matter;

20         IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that Election Judges shall not recuse in any

21   election contest or Election Code proceeding except as set forth above and under

22   N.M. Const. art. VI, § 18;




                                           Page 6 of 8
 1         IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that should the Election Judge believe that

 2   recusal is mandatory due to bias or prejudice or when the judge's impartiality may

 3   reasonably be questioned, because it is a matter in which the judge has an interest,

 4   see N.M. Const. art. VI, § 18, or for any other reason justifying recusal, the Election

 5   Judge shall immediately apprise the Chief Justice of the perceived need for recusal

 6   and the Chief Justice shall determine whether recusal is appropriate. State ex rel.

 7   Anaya v. Scarborough, 1966-NMSC-009, ¶¶ 2, 4, 75 N.M. 702, 410 P.2d 732

 8   (explaining that a judge is presumptively biased if they meet any of the grounds for

 9   recusal set forth in N.M. Const. art. VI, § 18 and that "an 'interest' necessary to

10   disqualify a judge must be a present pecuniary interest in the result, or actual bias or

11   prejudice, and not some indirect, remote, speculative, theoretical or possible

12   interest."; Rule 21-211(A) NMRA (mandating recusal where a judge's impartiality

13   may reasonably be questioned);

14         IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that in the event that all Election Judges in a

15   single judicial district recuse, the affected judicial district shall notify the Supreme

16   Court the day of said recusal to request the designation of an out-of-district Election

17   Judge and shall identify the case as an election contest or Election Code proceeding;

18         IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that any district judge appointed by a Chief


                                           Page 7 of 8
 1   Judge under this order to carry out election day, election night, and post-election

 2   judicial duties shall not designate any other person to serve in the judge’s place

 3   unless otherwise provided in the Election Code;

 4         IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that any mandamus proceedings that may be

 5   filed under NMSA 1978, § 1-14-21, may be filed in the district court or the Supreme

 6   Court, as appropriate, but shall not be filed in the New Mexico Court of Appeals;

 7   and

 8         IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that the Election Judges designated in this order

 9   are not subject to the exercise of peremptory excusals notwithstanding the provisions

10   in NMSA 1978, § 1-14-7 (1969), and Rule 1-087(E) NMRA.

11         IT IS SO ORDERED.
