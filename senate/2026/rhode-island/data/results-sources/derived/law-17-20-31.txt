         § 17-20-31. Investigation of complaints.
         The board of elections shall consider all complaints that it receives alleging criminal
            violations of this chapter and shall refer those complaints which it receives which
            it deems to be more than frivolous to the state police for investigation.
         
         
            History of Section.P.L. 1983, ch. 172, § 19.
