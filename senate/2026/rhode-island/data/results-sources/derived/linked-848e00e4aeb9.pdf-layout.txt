                                                 INSTRUCTIONS
        Who may File an Appeal/Protest/Complaint
        Any Rhode Island registered voter who has standing. Eligible standing to ﬁle an
        appeal/protest/complaint is dependent upon the type being ﬁled.

        How to File an Appeal/Protest/Complaint
        To ﬁle an appeal/protest/complaint with the Board of Elections, it must:
               Be in writing
               Sworn to under oath and under penalty of perjury
               Signed by the complainant/petitioner or their legal counsel
               Notarized
               Include the full name, telephone number, and mailing address of the
                complainant/petitioner
               Include a description of the alleged violation of law or regulation suﬃcient so the Board
                and Respondent are clear on the nature and speciﬁcs of the appeal/complaint/protest
               For appeals, the written statement must include a summary of the order, decision, decree
                or other ﬁnal action appealed from, the date that it was entered by the Board of
                Canvassers and a concise summary of the reasons for appealing said action
               If a hearing is requested, it must be indicated in writing
               Filed with the Board of Election and shall certify that a copy of the
                appeal/protest/complaint was provided via U.S. mail to each respondent
               For Appeals of Board of Canvassers Decision:
                Filed within twenty (20 days) of the local board’s decision
                For Election Protests
                Filed by 4 p.m. day following Primary; by 4 p.m. on 7th day following General Election
                For Complaints:
                Filed within ninety (90 days) of the incident alleged in the complaint or within 90 days
                after the Complainant knew, or with the exercise of reasonable diligence, should have
                known of those actions or events
               The appeal/protest/complaint is considered ﬁled on the date that the original signed and
                notarized document is actually received and ﬁled with the Board. (Electronic or facsimile
                ﬁlings are not accepted).

        Where to File an Appeal/Protest/Complaint
               Board of Elections, 2000 Plainﬁeld Pike, Cranston, RI 02921 during regular business hours

        Hearing and Administrative Determination
               Upon timely ﬁling with the Board, the Board will review and determine if a hearing is to be
                scheduled.
               A ﬁnal determination on the appeal/protest/complaint will be made by the Board.
               Appeals of Board of Elections decisions are ﬁled with the Supreme Court.
For a complete list of requirements, refer to the corresponding RI Gen. Law and/or BOE Rule & Regulation.
The term complaint in the instructions section on page 1 refers to appeals/protests/complaints.
