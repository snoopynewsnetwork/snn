         § 17-22-8. Lists of absentee voters.
         The state board, not later than thirty (30) days after any election day at which voting
            by mail ballot is permitted by chapter 20 of this title, shall prepare and mail to
            the local board of each city and town a list of names of every voter of the city or
            town who has voted by mail ballot.
