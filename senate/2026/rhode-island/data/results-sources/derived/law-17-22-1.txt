         § 17-22-1. Commencement and continuance of tabulations.
         The state board shall commence the counting, canvassing, and tabulating of all votes
            cast, including mail ballots, at eight o’clock (8:00) p.m. on any election day at
            which mail ballots may be cast and within twenty-four (24) hours after any other election
            or primary, and shall continue and complete the tabulation with all reasonable expedition
            by using an optical scan count system.
