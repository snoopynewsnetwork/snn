         § 17-19-39. Power to open voting equipment.
         The state board of elections shall have the right to open any and all voting equipment
            either before, during or after an election in the presence of the proper election
            officials of the city or town in which the election is held for the purpose of examining
            the voting equipment; provided, that no programmed memory cartridge shall be erased
            until after the expiration of ten (10) days from the election at which the voting
            equipment was used, unless said memory cartridge is required by the state board for
            the purpose of conducting recounts.
