         § 17-22-5.1. Investigation by board of elections prior to certification.
         At any time prior to the certification of the results of a particular election, the
            state board of elections may, upon its own motion, investigate any allegation of error,
            irregularity, or impropriety in the conduct of any facet of the election which comes
            to its attention and may, upon its own motion, take appropriate action based upon
            the findings of the investigation.
