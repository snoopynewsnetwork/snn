         § 17-18-11. Time of closing of polls.
         Elective meetings in all cities and towns shall be continuously kept open for voting
            until 8 p.m.; provided, that any qualified voter who is waiting in line to vote at
            8 p.m. shall be entitled to cast his or her vote. When all persons entitled to vote
            have been afforded a reasonable opportunity to do so, the polls shall be closed.
