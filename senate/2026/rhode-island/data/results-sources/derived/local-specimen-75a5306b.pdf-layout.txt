To request an Accessible Mail Ballot Application, see Section C on the front.

Instructions                       1. Complete and sign the application - Use a black or blue pen to fill in your information and select the
                                      eligibility category that applies to you.
                                   2. Return your application - Must be received by your local board of canvassers by the deadline.
                                      Postmark must be dated on or before the deadline date.
                                   3. Tracking your mail ballot - Visit vote.ri.gov to sign up for BallotTrax. You will receive
                                      notifications on the status of your mail ballot application and mail ballot delivery information.
                                      Mail ballots are sent to voters approximately 3 - 4 weeks before the election.
                                   4. Receiving your mail ballot - Your mail ballot packet will have your mail ballot, instructions,
                                      voter’s certificate and the required postage-paid envelope to return your voted mail ballot to the State
                                      Board of Elections.


Notice to applicant                • Any registered voter can use this form to request a mail ballot for Rhode Island elections.
                                   • Your application is a matter of public record, but your vote is confidential.
                                   • If anyone attempts to intimidate or unduly influence you, or interfere with your right to vote, contact
                                     your Local Board of Canvassers.
                                   • Any person knowingly and willfully making a false application or certification or knowingly and willfully
                                     aiding and abetting in the making of a false application or certification shall be guilty of a felony and
                                     shall be subject to the penalties provided for in section 17-26-1, [RIGL 17-20-8(D)].
