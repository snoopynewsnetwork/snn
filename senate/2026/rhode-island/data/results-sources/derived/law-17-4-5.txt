         § 17-4-5. Time of election of senators.
         The election of a senator in congress shall be held on the Tuesday next after the
            first Monday in November, in the year 1958, and on the Tuesday next after the first
            Monday in November in every sixth year thereafter, and on the Tuesday next after the
            first Monday in November in the year 1960, and on the Tuesday next after the first
            Monday in November in every sixth year thereafter.
