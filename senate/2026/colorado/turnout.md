Ordinary ballots must arrive by 7 p.m. Mountain Time November 3; postmark alone does not qualify. Cure forms for signature/required-ID issues must arrive by 11:59 p.m. MT November 12. The generic rule is eight days, but November 11 is Veterans Day and the current calendar applies the holiday rule; blindly adding eight days gives the wrong operational date. Eligible mailed UOCAVA ballots sent by 7 p.m. MT Election Day have the separate November 12 close-of-business receipt deadline; electronic returns are due 7 p.m. Election Day. [Official UOCAVA guidance](https://www.sos.state.co.us/pubs/elections/FAQs/UOCAVA.html) and [clerks' election dates](https://www.clerkandrecorder.org/2026-general-election) distinguish those cases. Rule 7 also delays publication statewide if a court extends any VSPC's hours. No such future Election Day order is assumed.
