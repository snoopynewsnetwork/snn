# Wyoming precinct maps

Research retrieval: **2026-10-18**. The retained snapshot was fetched 17:28-17:39 UTC (Converse and Big Horn WFS evidence re-fetched last) after source discovery earlier the same day ([acquisition summary](maps/raw/acquisition-summary.json)). Open the [offline map index](maps/index.html) for previews and downloads by county.

**Coverage.** The accepted priority set is the 15 counties of the 2024 Senate top 15, in [turnout](turnout.md) order. It contains the exact 12-county 80% set (Laramie through Converse). Actual precinct assets were downloaded for all 15:

- **County-published vector precinct layers for 14 counties**, including all 12 counties of the 80% set. Each layer comes from the county's own public election or GIS channel: an ArcGIS REST layer, the TopoJSON behind a Terra GIS county viewer, or a MapServer WFS.
- **Goshen has PDF maps only.** Its 2022 redistricting precinct maps come from the County Clerk's page. No Goshen vector layer was found.
- **Big Horn and Converse publish geometry without precinct attributes.** Their codes were recovered from the county server's own rendering and are labeled as inferred.

Wyoming has **no single statewide official precinct layer**. The Secretary of State publishes a statewide precinct **code roster** but no boundaries. The other checked routes have no precinct geometry: the Legislative Service Office (LSO) and the University of Wyoming WyGISC GeoHub. So the assets are county-by-county.

**Feature counts:** 14 primary layers contain **453 polygon features**. Four county alternate layers add 114 more features (one is geometry-identical to its primary). There are **12 official county PDF maps**, plus the 2020 Census VTDs as a labeled alternative. Feature counts are polygon counts: several counties split one precinct code into several polygons, so a feature count is not a precinct or reporting-unit count.

**Three kinds of unit, kept apart:**

Matching uses a canonical `district-precinct` integer key (`01-02`, `1-02`, `Lander 1-2` and `1-2-1` all mean district 1, precinct 2). A trailing third number or letter marks a split part and is kept in [features.csv](maps/features.csv). Exact-string matches are counted separately. Never join on the precinct number alone, and never across counties.

## County notes

Each primary layer is retained as the original response (Esri JSON in the layer's native spatial reference, the TopoJSON file, or WFS GML). Each also has a derived [OGC:CRS84 GeoJSON](maps/derived/) with full coordinate precision and unchanged attributes, and a generated preview. Edit dates come from the layer's ArcGIS `editingInfo` or feature edit fields. Terra GIS HTTP `Last-Modified` values (mostly 2026-10-17) are file-generation times, not boundary dates.

1. **Laramie (56021).** [CLCGISC FeatureServer/0 "Precincts"](https://maps.clcgisc.com/server/rest/services/administrative_restricted_CLK/administrative_restricted_CLK/FeatureServer/0). It is the "Precinct" layer of the County Clerk's [Political Boundaries and Voter Info app](https://maps.clcgisc.com/portal/apps/webappviewer/index.html?id=6c122e09faec455eab3a05f9e896448a), linked from [Voting in Laramie County](https://www.laramiecountywy.gov/Resident-Services/Elections/Voting-in-Laramie-County). The service copyright is "Cheyenne and Laramie County GIS Cooperative". It has **43 features and 41 keys**, NAD83 / Wyoming East ftUS (EPSG:3736). Every feature was created 2022-05-12 and last edited between 2022-05-12 and 2022-09-15. **IDs differ from both rosters**: the layer has `1-8`, which is in neither the 2024 rows nor the 2026 roster, and lacks `6-2`, which is in the 2026 roster. `1-1.2` is treated as the SD5/HD12 split part of 1-1, consistent with the roster listing 1-01 in SD5, SD8, HD12 and HD44. All 40 of the 2024 rows match by key, but none match as exact strings, because the layer writes `2-3` where the results write `02-03`. Treat this as a 2022-vintage layer that needs a crosswalk check before 2026 use.

## Statewide sources and labeled alternatives

- **SOS roster:** see the units table above. Both DP-014 PDFs are retained and parsed: 919 roster rows on 35 pages, 0 unparsed lines.
- **Census 2020 VTDs** ([TIGER/Line 2020 PL ZIP](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2020PL/LAYER/VTD/2020/tl_2020_56_vtd20.zip), 512 statewide VTDs; the whole 3.9 MB ZIP is kept unchanged). This is a **fallback historical layer, not current precincts**. Key-level agreement with the 2024 rows is high in some counties (Natrona, Park, Sheridan, Converse, Carbon, Big Horn, Lincoln) but 0/11 in Albany and 9/35 in Sweetwater. Many VTDs are pseudo VTDs (`VTDI20 = P`), for example all 13 in Big Horn and all 24 in Goshen.
- **Redistricting Data Hub** (third party): only its [2024 README](maps/raw/statewide-rdh-2024-readme/readme_wy_2024_gen_prec.txt.gz) is kept. It says RDH built 2024 boundaries from county GIS, "Terra GIS", or edited Census VTDs depending on the county, and checked them against an L2 voter file. That source mix matches what was found here. The RDH boundary file was not downloaded or used.
- **No statewide layer.** The [GeoSpatial Hub / UW GeoHub search](maps/raw/statewide-geohub-precinct-search/geohub-search-precinct.json.gz) returned no precinct dataset. `wyoleg.gov` returned only a JavaScript application shell, and `/Redistricting/` returned HTTP 403 ([LSO attempt](maps/raw/statewide-lso-redistricting-attempt/retrieval.json)). The Legislature's 2022 redistricting products are legislative-district maps, which are not precincts.

## Checks

[validate.py](maps/validate.py) writes [validation.json](maps/validation.json); [build-summary.json](maps/build-summary.json) records library versions and read-only input hashes.

- **Paging.** Every ArcGIS layer was fetched by object-ID batches of at most 100, always below its maxRecordCount (1,000 or 2,000), in native SR. For all 10 ArcGIS layers, returnCountOnly = returnIdsOnly = returned features, the returned OIDs equal the ID list, and no `exceededTransferLimit` appeared. Both WFS layers satisfy hits = numberMatched = numberReturned = parsed members = renders (32 and 13). The TopoJSON files are static, whole-file downloads, so pagination does not apply.
- **Hashes.** Each response stores its entity SHA-256/bytes and stored SHA-256/bytes. Text originals use deterministic gzip with `mtime=0`; PDFs, ZIPs and PNGs are stored unchanged. The manifest hashes every original, derived file and preview.
- **No simplification.** Each derived GeoJSON has the same feature count, the same total vertex count and, for ArcGIS, attributes identical to its original. Coordinates were transformed with pyproj 3.7.0 / PROJ 9.4.1 using the default NAD83-to-WGS84 operation, with no grids. That can shift points by about a metre; native originals remain authoritative.
- **Geometry defects (not repaired).**
  - **Invalid features (ring self-intersections):** Natrona 1, Campbell 1, Park 1, Sheridan Terra 4, Teton 2, Lincoln 1, Uinta Terra 1. All other layers are 0. No geometry is empty, and every input ring is closed.
  - **Exact duplicates:** Sheridan Terra 2 pairs.
  - **Cross-precinct overlaps above 1 m²:**
    - Sweetwater: 45.0 km².
    - Carbon: 1.25 km² (Terra layer) and 2.69 km² (ArcGIS alternate).
    - Big Horn: 0.75 km².
    - Park: 0.038 km².
    - Lincoln: 0.016 km².
    - Albany: 8,197 m² across precincts, plus 0.47 km² between parts of the same precinct code.
    - Laramie, Natrona, Campbell, Converse and Sheridan: under 0.006 km².
  - **County coverage:** every layer covers at least 99.79% of its 2020 Census county extent, and every feature's representative point lies inside its county, which also confirms the inferred TopoJSON CRSs.
  - **Full lists:** [geometry-checks.json](maps/geometry-checks.json); alternate comparisons are in [layer-comparisons.json](maps/layer-comparisons.json).
- **IDs.** Validation recomputes every reconciliation from [features.csv](maps/features.csv), the read-only [2024/2022 result labels](data/baselines/precinct-labels.csv) and the parsed roster. All 345 of the 2024 result rows in the 14 vector counties match a key in their county's primary layer. The 15 priority counties hold 359 of the 432 statewide rows; Goshen's 14 have no vector layer. Against the 2026 roster, the only mismatches are Laramie's extra `1-8` and missing `6-2`.
- **Visual review.** Five PDF maps were compared with the GIS previews ([visual-review.json](maps/visual-review.json)).
- **Files.** All files are well under 40 MB; the largest is the 11.9 MB Carbon Rawlins PDF. Relative links in this report and in `index.html` resolve.


- [sources.json](maps/sources.json) is the source registry. It gives every URL, publisher, role, vintage statement, ID field and CRS evidence, including blocked and negative routes.
- [acquire.py](maps/acquire.py) uses the system curl, because some county sites refuse other clients. It writes `maps/raw/<source>/` and its `retrieval.json`.
- [build.py](maps/build.py) is offline. It writes `derived/`, `previews/`, [manifest.csv](maps/manifest.csv) / [manifest.json](maps/manifest.json), [map-index.json](maps/map-index.json), [features.csv](maps/features.csv), [id-reconciliation.csv](maps/id-reconciliation.csv), [geometry-checks.json](maps/geometry-checks.json), [layer-comparisons.json](maps/layer-comparisons.json), [wfs-render-join.json](maps/wfs-render-join.json), [pdf-audit.json](maps/pdf-audit.json), [sos-2026-roster.csv](maps/sos-2026-roster.csv), [index.html](maps/index.html) and the table above.
- [validate.py](maps/validate.py) uses only the standard library.
- **Previews are research aids.** They are drawn without a basemap and outlined with the 2020 Census county extent. PDF previews are page-1 renders. None is an official map.

## Gaps and limits

- **November 2026 approval is not established.** No county's polygon layer is certified as the November 3, 2026 reporting geography. Code agreement with the June 2026 roster is necessary, but not sufficient.
- **Laramie (rank 1)** publishes 2022-edited polygons whose keys disagree with the 2026 roster: it adds `1-8` and lacks `6-2`. No newer Laramie layer or PDF precinct map was linked from the County Clerk election or GIS pages that were checked. The 2024 county list explains 6-2's 2024 annexation, but not its return in the 2026 roster.
- **Converse and Big Horn codes are inferred** from server renderings, because their WFS hides attributes. For Big Horn, the largest-overlap 2020 VTD agrees for all 13 polygons. For Converse, the 2022 PDFs agree on visual review, and the 2020 VTD agrees for 24 of 32 polygons; the differences are expected after the 2022 redraw. No attribute download confirms either county.
- **Goshen has no vector layer.** Its 2022 PDFs were not georeferenced or digitized.
- **Older labels.** Teton's geometry is from 2022. Sweetwater, Carbon (Terra) and Uinta (Terra) carry 2022 labels; Park carries 2024. Campbell and Lincoln show no dates. Campbell's `LIFECYCLE` field is hidden.
- **Natrona's county website** blocked scripted access, so its 2026 precinct-change notices are unread. The NRGISC layer description and July 2026 edit dates are the evidence used here.
- **Reporting units versus polygons.** Polygons are geographic precincts. Several counties use vote centers, for example Laramie and Fremont. Absentee, early or vote-center reporting rows may not map one-to-one onto polygons. No votes are allocated, and no precinct-to-2026 crosswalk is claimed beyond key matching.
- **Defects kept as published.** Overlaps, duplicates and invalid rings were not repaired. Overlay and area weighting need reviewed, labeled repairs.
- **Inferred CRSs.** The TopoJSON CRSs come from viewer configs. County containment supports them, but the files do not declare a CRS.
