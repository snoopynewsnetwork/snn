## Time, late ballots and completion

Preserve the applicable county timezone and any extension; a single Central timestamp cannot stand for every Texas polling place. Early voting runs October 19–30. An early/mail-heavy first release is a selected counting batch, not a random sample of statewide preferences.

The calendar allows qualifying timely marked domestic ballots until November 4 at 5 p.m., with distinct overseas and military/FPCA categories extending to November 9. Keep those categories and eligibility rules separate; follow the full calendar before assigning any ballot to a remaining-vote pool. Requests, accepted ballots, outstanding envelopes and provisional ballots are administrative quantities, not candidate votes. Never infer vote choice from party registration or count a ballot twice across accepted and tabulated records. Collect aggregate evidence rather than personal voter records.

The same calendar distinguishes local canvass deadlines from the later state canvass. A county reporting all precincts/vote centers does not certify its final total or prove that qualifying late/cured/provisional ballots are exhausted. Preserve raw completion numerators/denominators and verify their scope; do not display them as the percentage of votes counted. The [official-source report](official-results.md) verifies Senate plurality under [chapter 2](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/EL/htm/EL.2.htm#2). A candidate must exceed each opponent; the calendar’s local majority-runoff date does not create a Senate runoff. Tie/recount rules remain separate. Most Texas counties close at scheduled 7 p.m. CST (November 4 01:00 UTC); El Paso and Hudspeth close at scheduled 7 p.m. MST (02:00 UTC). Preserve extensions and actual source jurisdictions rather than applying one statewide clock.

The dated calendar sets registrar review November 13, general-election ballot-board completion November 16, local canvass deadline November 17 and the governor’s state-canvass window November 21–December 7. Those windows are not verified meeting appointments or evidence that every pending envelope contains a valid Senate vote. The current [SOS SB2753 advisory](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/elections/laws/advisory2025-10.shtml) expressly is not the required final implementation report; applicability to this election remains unresolved. Follow the inspected dated November calendar’s October 19–30 period as the research assumption, and recheck current official instructions before operation.
