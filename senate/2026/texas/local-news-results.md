## Comparing count observations

The [official inventory](official-results.md) defines authority chains across all 254 counties; the [turnout report](turnout.md) defines the exact deep-inspection priority set. Historical volume prioritizes attention, but all counties remain in statewide arithmetic. County summaries may include countywide vote centers and central early/mail units that do not correspond one-to-one to precinct polygons. Preserve actual reporting unit IDs and method labels instead of joining on a precinct number alone.

As a concrete official preflight, Tarrant's actual November landing page returned 200 and linked Clarity/Civix results applications; both vendor requests returned 403. Its linked November ZIP returned 200 but contains only a multilingual future-export notice, with no votes. The [strategy](live-polling-strategy.md) records the exact links. A successful ZIP download is not validation of a count feed.

Maintain election, contest, county FIPS/native unit, method, candidate ID, source, status, publisher time and retrieval UTC for every observation. Verify local timezone where relevant rather than treating every Texas source as Central. Compare the same geographic/method coverage at compatible release times. Never add a state view to county subtotals already included, two newsroom totals covering the same electorate, or successive cumulative snapshots. Keep corrections that decrease totals.

The Tribune's inspected primary product uses AP. Other stations may share AP, another supplier or official county uploads; page structure and station ownership alone do not prove supplier identity or independence. KVIA's neighboring-state primary display and KCBD's local runoff demonstrate why title/state/election/contest checks must precede parsing. A current page-fetch or transformation date does not establish a new candidate-vote release.

The Tribune's actually linked application module exposes [public primary JSON](https://s3.amazonaws.com/sos-test-2.texastribune.org/2026_primary/republic_json/results_FLAT.json). The retained response has 435 March 3 primary race records. The two Senate party contests each retain all 254 counties, with every candidate's county sum, statewide total and 10,006 reporting/total precinct flags reconciling exactly. Democratic total is 2,319,187; Republican total is 2,166,910. Its county code is a three-digit suffix, not full county FIPS, and no precinct rows are exposed in these county arrays. Positive counts coexist with zero early-vote/registration and high/mid/low estimate fields; those zeros do not prove method counts or no outstanding ballots. The race records lack a publisher count timestamp. The [offline inspector](data/news/inspect_evidence.py) verifies scope and sums. The hostname's wording alone does not decide test status, but the explicit primary election date excludes this dataset from November ingestion regardless. AP calls and `contest_result` winner/runoff fields remain separate from counts.

A newsroom fallback may temporarily replace a missing official county observation only with explicit source/time, coverage and substitution reason. Reconcile it with the verified official observation when available. Original regional reporting about mail processing, late boards, delayed uploads or unreported vote centers can inform remaining-vote ranges, but distinguish attributed estimates from counted votes. Keep all external race calls, estimated completion and any NYT/Needle forecast separate from candidate counts.

KRIS provides a concrete historical counting-context example: its [December 14, 2024 local runoff report](https://www.kristv.com/news/local-news/in-your-neighborhood/corpus-christi/northwest-side/too-close-to-call-city-council-district-1-runoff-election-results-still-in-the-air), modified December 15, reports a two-vote council lead while mail ballots remain to be counted. This is original, dated local reporting with an unresolved-ballot caveat, not an exact remainder or evidence of November 2026 Senate processing. The source's rounded percentages and provisional leader language cannot substitute for an official final result.

Before November, verify the actual Senate general-election page and ballot IDs for each product, public access, county coverage, upstream credit and genuine change behavior. Do not reuse March/May primary runoff URLs or current local runoff values. The [collection strategy](live-polling-strategy.md) will specify proposed request intervals and manual fallback, not a publisher guarantee.
