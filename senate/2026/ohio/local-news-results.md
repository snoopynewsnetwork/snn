## Official and media overlap

The official [Ohio Decides site](https://liveresults.ohiosos.gov/) currently displays a **mock-election** warning. This survey's early direct request returned 403; the official-results worker later successfully downloaded the linked public JSON/XML/workbook and confirmed the test identity, complete county coverage and zero setup values. The differing access observations are dated in their separate manifests. These are preparation data, not live votes. Future-looking dates, a generated-clock change and JSON `isElectionOfficial=true` cannot bypass XML `IsTest=true` or the mock warning.

Normalize each observation by election, special/regular contest, county FIPS/native reporting-unit ID, candidate, voting method, source, status and time. Keep publisher time separate from retrieval/HTML transformation time. Compare coherent statewide and county views at compatible versions; never add a statewide view to its county subtotals or combine successive cumulative snapshots. Missing counties and unavailable precinct detail remain unknown.

WCPO's statewide AP channel and WOSU/NPR AP results share a supplier. Cleveland 19 and 13abc use inspected GSync page structures, but those structures alone do not prove a common count supplier or independence. WCPO's historical DDHQ panel uses a distinct product, with actual upstream official releases still potentially shared. NYT supplier and county coverage need observation before independence claims.

Use a newsroom observation as a temporary county fallback only with source/time and substitution reason displayed. Preserve discrepancies, later decreases and candidate corrections; prefer the reconciled official county/state record when it returns. A local reporter's description of absentee/provisional processing can explain what remains, but an estimate is not a count. Keep AP/DDHQ/NYT calls, estimated completion and any external forecast separate from reported candidate votes.

Before November, verify active Senate special-election rows and candidate IDs, reject mock/primary/wrong-state products, confirm access and supplier credits, and observe real changes to measure lag. No channel inspected here establishes a complete tested November Senate feed or a guaranteed publication cadence.
