## RCV and geographic comparisons

The [Division of Elections' current explanation](https://www.elections.alaska.gov/election-information/) confirms general-election ranked choice voting. Keep election, contest, candidate ID, round, geography, counts, exhausted/inactive ballots where published, publisher time and retrieval time on every observation. A first-choice lead is not a final-round majority. A later RCV tally is a replacement view of the same ballots at a new round, not additional votes. No D-versus-R two-party swing should be inferred by relabeling two Republican finalists or an independent challenger.

Use the state's actual house-district, precinct and regional/administrative units. Do not force newsroom county maps onto Alaska's reporting schema or aggregate unmatched precincts into boroughs/census areas. Record whether a figure includes absentee, early or questioned ballots that are reported separately from polling-place precincts. Missing detail stays unknown.

Compare newsroom counts with the [official inventory](official-results.md) at the same election, geography and round. Never add a state total to its district subtotals or add multiple products' cumulative totals. A manual newsroom substitution needs source/time and a replacement rule; return to the reconciled official count when available. Retain decreases and round changes as separate revisions. Different suppliers can still depend on the same Division release; measure observed lag instead of assuming independence.

AKPM and KTOO shared articles are one reporting chain. The Beacon's observed historical scoreboard uses DDHQ. Alaska's News Source's inspected page does not establish a supplier independent of either. Keep external calls, completion estimates and NYT/Needle forecasts separate from observed votes. Before November, confirm each active product's election, candidate identities, geographic coverage, RCV display, accessibility and actual update behavior.
