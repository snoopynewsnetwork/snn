        EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT

        WHEREAS, On the 5 th day of November, 2024, pursuant to the statute in such case made and provided,
  an election was held in the State of Illinois for the purpose of electing on a general ballot, nineteen Electors of
  President and Vice President of the United States and nineteen Alternate Electors of President and Vice
  President of the United States; and

        WHEREAS, In accordance with the statute aforesaid for the final ascertainment of the result of said
  election, held as aforesaid, the members of the State Board of Elections are the officers appointed by law to
  canvass the returns made by the several counties in the State of the votes given at said election on the 2 nd day
  of December, 2024; and

        WHEREAS, At the office of the State Board of Elections, in the City of Springfield, State of Illinois, the
  members of the State Board of Elections proceeded to canvass the returns of the election as aforesaid, being
  the official abstracts transmitted to the State Board of Elections of this State of all votes given in each and every
  county in the State of Illinois at the election held November 5, 2024, for Electors and Alternate Electors for
  President and Vice President of the United States; therefore, be it

        ASCERTAINED, It appears as the results of such canvass that the following named persons received
  votes for the offices of Electors and Alternate Electors of President and Vice President of the United States in
  this State; and be it further

        ASCERTAINED, The number of votes given for each person is set opposite to the respective name of the
  person, this is to say:
