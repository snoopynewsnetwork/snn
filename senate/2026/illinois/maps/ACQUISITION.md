# Bounded acquisition and clocks

Research is observed October 19 Central / October 20 UTC, 2026. Literal public
URLs were discovered on the official State Board of Elections precinct page,
the actual state directory listing, and the official Census VTD directory.
No guessed service/layer/unit/contest/candidate IDs or authenticated endpoints
were used. Source constructors for ArcGIS were not invented: no ArcGIS layer
was acquired. The native ArcGIS paging requirements are therefore inapplicable
to this ZIP/PDF/raster package; no GeoJSON export or projected service response
was substituted for a native layer.

folder. Aurora is not one of the 108 source parents and is not silently added
to the historical inventory. All 108 actual authority folders are fetched.
Every linked nested folder within them is followed, including all 50 Chicago
ward folders and both Will folders. All literal names remain in full original directory responses. Structured file
lists recognize4,256map/document links. An additional24Thumbs.db names are
not acquired as local caches; the linked Stark `Stark Precincts on web.rtf`
companion remains unacquired, with its exact href preserved in the full original
Stark directory. No legal/identity meaning is inferred from that uninspected RTF. A bounded first leaf per authority receives an actual GET or HEAD;
selected complete originals are retained. An observed 130,299,774-byte Calhoun
PDF makes the reason for a byte-aware bound concrete. HEAD-only leaf checks
are explicitly unacquired bodies; they never substitute for the complete
statewide historical geographic fallback.

The normal leaf pass acquires a complete GET after an observed HEAD
Content-Length at most 1,500,000 bytes. Exact originals already downloaded in
the first pass are reused even if larger. A few specifically named complete
Cook/Bloomington/Rockford shared maps and the St. Clair abbreviations companion
are additional bounded supplements. These are historical shared maps or
samples, not proof of full current authority coverage. Every child observation
retains the exact source hierarchy and its own gaps; no leaf identity follows

The entire Census statewide ZIP and all its members are retained. County slices
are built from the source fields and full geometry without asking a service to
transform output. Complete internal XML metadata, native PRJ/CPG and the full
Census technical PDF are retained along with SHP/SHX/DBF. Headers and internal
metadata have distinct clocks. Publisher directory modification times, PDF
creation dates and filenames can differ from a boundary-effective date. Such
differences are not resolved by guessing. All raster/PDF originals are stored
losslessly; no automatic scan cleanup, cropping or map-content repair occurs.

bodies. Do not refresh in place. Preserve this sealed package and acquire a
future vintage into a separate authorized location. The offline build needs no
