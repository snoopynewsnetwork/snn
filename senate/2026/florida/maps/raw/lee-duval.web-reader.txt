L61:   5. # Updating your Florida Driver's License or ID Card Number
L62: Have you recently updated or been issued a new Florida driver's license or ID card? Due to changes implemented to the numbering system by the Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles (DHSMV), your new driver's license or identification card number may be different from the one on your voter registration record. I t’s important to update your voter registration information with your new number.
L63: This identification number helps us verify your identity when processing your request for a vote-by-mail ballot or an address change.
L64: To update your voter registration with your new Florida driver’s license or ID card number, you may:
L65:      * Call us at (239) LEE-VOTE (533-8683)
L66:      * Visit cite14†RegisterToVoteFlorida.gov†www.registertovoteflorida.gov to update your record.
L67:      * Complete and submit a Florida Voter Registration Application

L77:   7. # Congressional Redistricting
L78: In 2026, the Florida Legislature passed House Bill 1D, which establishes new or revised congressional districts in several counties across the state. While Lee County’s voters remain in either Congressional District 17 or 19, your congressional district may have changed from 17 to 19 or vice versa under the new legislative map. Some voters will not experience a change in their congressional district.
