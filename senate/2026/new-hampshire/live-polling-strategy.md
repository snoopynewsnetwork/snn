## Proposed cadence and source precedence

| Lane | Initial interval | Admission requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verified state result publication | Five minutes with jitter | Correct November contest, native geographic scope, tested schema and current status. |
| Verified distinct priority clerk publication | Two to five minutes | Source limits and actual additional information; avoid duplicating shared vendors. |
| Other clerk documents / manual reports | Five to ten minutes during counting | Timestamp publication and observed change; avoid repeated unchanged large downloads. |
| Regional / NYT checks | Five minutes where access is verified, otherwise manual | Correct election and compatible geography; supplier attribution. |
| Recount/canvass checks | Thirty to sixty minutes during posted activity, otherwise daily | Current official notices and deadlines. |

These are proposed request intervals, not measured publication cadence. A primary liveblog timestamp, application timer or aggregate polling-place count does not establish November data flow. Use per-host limits, conditional requests where supported, jitter, timeouts and backoff. Separate last fetch, last parse, publisher timestamp and last changed result; stable totals may be healthy and HTTP 200 may be historical or empty.

Default to a coherent official record for each nonoverlapping native unit. An authenticated newer clerk correction can supersede the same unit with explicit provenance; a newsroom/manual fallback must identify source, contest, candidates, scope and time. Never add a state total to included clerk totals, two AP views or successive cumulative snapshots. Retain disagreements until compatible observations reconcile. Track missing/unmatched units separately from zero votes.

## Rules, historical comparisons and remainder

The [retained primary hour notices](data/results-sources/poll-hours-evidence.json) include Bedford/Dover 7 pm and Windham 8 pm closes, in EST(UTC−5) on November 3. They are examples, not a complete statewide roster; recheck each actual local notice and voters-in-line requirements. [Current RSA 657:22](https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/LXIII/657/657-mrg.htm) gives ordinary completed absentee receipt by 5 pm election day with specifically referenced exceptions, rather than a general postmark rule. [RSA 659:49–53](https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/LXIII/659/659-mrg.htm) separates absentee processing from final counting. Current cure/signature instructions, any later court action and a public eligible outstanding-vote universe remain unverified; do not manufacture a post-election tail or assume it is zero. Poll closing and reporting-place coverage are not count completion.

Keep ballots cast, registered voters, contest votes, requested/returned/accepted absentee ballots and candidate counts separately labeled. Administrative totals can bound a remaining-vote range only after eligibility, as-of time and overlap with tabulated votes are checked. Avoid personal voter records. A town's announced hand count, delayed upload or unreported ward may inform uncertainty when attributed and timed; elapsed time and reporting percentages cannot provide exact votes remaining.

Compare incoming returns to compatible historical town/ward/method observations and boundary vintages. A county-derived historical target must reconcile to every included native unit and statewide primary totals. Early/mail or early-reporting towns are selected batches, not random statewide samples. Keep observed votes fixed and estimate only the remainder with explicit low/base/high ranges and source assumptions. As the count develops, inspect units contributing the largest remaining uncertainty as well as historical-volume priorities.

For a stated two-candidate comparison, counted D-minus-R lead `L` and positive remaining major-party votes `U` give tie share `(1-L/U)/2`. A selected-unit target subtracts assumed net votes elsewhere before solving its share. Values outside 0–100% are secured/impossible under the assumptions. The actual [Farmington November general sample](https://www.farmington.nh.us/DocumentCenter/View/3121), linked by its official clerk, prints John E. Sununu (Republican), Edmond Laplante (Constitution), Chris Pappas (Democratic) and a write-in space for Senate. This corroborates a third candidate; no current results numeric IDs are inferred. Retain each candidate and verify the legal outcome/tie rule separately. Historical uniform shifts and remaining-vote break-even targets are scenarios, not probabilities.

## Evidence, corrections, maps and rehearsal

Save immutable response bytes, URL, retrieval UTC, HTTP metadata, hash, election/contest, native municipality/ward/method, candidate keys, publisher time/timezone, parser version and unofficial/canvassed/certified status. Preserve source labels and unmatched rows. Retain decreases, reassignment, revised coverage and candidate-universe changes. Replace cumulative observations for matching unit/source; derive deltas only across compatible releases. A renamed ward or new reporting label is not automatically new votes.

Validate candidate/method sums, municipal/ward relationships and statewide totals. Do not splice mismatched versions into a complete-looking state. Map assets require actual downloads, provenance, boundary vintage and geometry diagnostics. Town boundaries, ward boundaries, polling-place points and legal November precincts are different objects. An explicit results crosswalk is needed before allocating votes; preserve historical/current/approved distinctions and do not silently simplify original geometry.

Before operation pin actual November ballot/source IDs, confirm clerk coverage and manual contacts, and replay genuine timestamped updates to check duplicates, corrections, missing units, stale-source display and fallback replacement. Operators retain source/document, publisher and observation times, exact scope, candidate values and substitution reason. Display all native units, unresolved coverage and remaining ranges. External forecasts/calls stay separate. A probability model needs a dated prior, genuine historical replay and held-out calibration under the [root plan](../../../ELECTION_NIGHT_PLAN.md); synthetic report orders cannot validate a live Needle.
