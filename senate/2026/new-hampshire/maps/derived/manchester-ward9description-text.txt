§ 12.09  WARD 9.
Beginning at a point at the intersection of the centerline of Beech Street and the centerline of Shasta Street; 
thence easterly by the centerline of Shasta Street to the centerline of Hall Street; thence southerly by the 
centerline of Hall Street to the centerline of South Hall Street; thence southerly by the centerline of South 
Hall Street to the centerline of Parkview Street; thence westerly by the centerline of Parkview Street to the 
centerline of South Willow Street; thence southeasterly and southerly by the centerline of South Willow 
Street to the centerline of Interstate 293; thence westerly by the centerline of Interstate 293 to the 
centerline of the Merrimack River; thence upstream by the centerline of the Merrimack River to the 
centerline of Queen City Avenue; thence easterly by the centerline of Queen City Avenue to the centerline of 
the northernmost onramp of Queen City Avenue; thence easterly by the centerline of the northernmost 
onramp of Queen City Avenue to the centerline of Willow Street; thence northwesterly by the centerline of 
Willow Street to the centerline of Silver Street; thence easterly by the centerline of Silver Street to the 
centerline of Beech Street; then southerly by the centerline of Beech Street to the centerline of Shasta 
Street, to the point of the beginning.
