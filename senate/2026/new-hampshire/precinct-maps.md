All 54 priority ward identities have an actual ward reference, rather than just their city's outer boundary: **12 individual Manchester ward maps and descriptions**, all-ward PDFs for Nashua, Concord, Dover, Rochester, Portsmouth, Lebanon and Claremont, and **five full native Keene ward polygons**. A municipal outer boundary alone does not prove a town's current precinct configuration. [Per-entry qualifications and downloads](maps/index.html) are part of the delivered coverage, not claims of November approval.

## Geometry preservation and diagnostic limits

The original SHP/SHX/DBF/PRJ and all16 archive members pass CRC and native record checks. Polygon records have **259 parts and91,469 vertices**, with complete attributes and native doubles. The773 line records also retain every point, part and attribute. SHX byte offsets/content lengths, SHP record numbers/file lengths, DBF field definitions/counts/deletion flags and every member hash are checked. No native file is replaced by a simplified derivative. [Native control details](maps/derived/zip-controls.json).

All **seven ArcGIS layer acquisitions** use count+complete IDs before and after, bounded batches≤100, full fields, full native geometry, requested Z/M/true curves, and actual transfer flags. Count/ID sets agree before/after; these are not transactionally isolated snapshots, so intervening record edits cannot be excluded by ID stability alone. No rounding, quantization, generalization or outSR is requested in originals. They retain259 municipal polygons, five Keene ward polygons/five site points, two Merrimack ward polygons/two site points and two Hudson district polygons/two site points. Geometry/native IDs remain separate from unavailable results IDs. The downloaded layers report no Z/M and return no true-curve rings; this is not a claim about unpublished source databases. All raw metadata and batch streams remain in the [source inventory](maps/sources.html).

Native GRANIT/Merrimack/Hudson polygon CRS is **EPSG3437**, NAD83 New Hampshire State Plane US survey feet; Keene ward CRS is **EPSG6525**, NAD83(2011) New Hampshire State Plane US survey feet. Point layers retain their own recorded CRS. Municipal GeoJSON derivatives transform every coordinate to longitude/latitude without simplification; native source streams remain authoritative. SVG coordinates are rounded only in display viewBox units; actual PDF PNG previews are reduced-resolution rasterizations. PDF cartographic scale/units come from each page; no CRS is invented for image-only PDFs. [Transformation/schema details](maps/source/SCHEMA.md).

[Topology diagnostics](maps/derived/topology.json) report **zero invalid/empty polygons, positive-area pair overlaps and enclosed union holes** in the259 archive polygons,259 current-service polygons, five Keene wards, two Merrimack wards and two Hudson districts. All records are retained without repair, snapping or buffering. These checks do **not** establish legal boundary correctness, absence of external-edge gaps, exact matching to an independently approved municipal envelope, or cross-source/cross-year equality. No legal reference envelope or approved crosswalk was acquired. Native area/length/population fields are not votes or allocation weights.
